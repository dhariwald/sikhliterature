# Ras 2, Ansu 35

## The Yogi Is Taught the Vedanta

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਸ਼੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਨਿਧਾਨ ਸੁਨਿ, ਲਖਿ ਕੈ ਸੁਧਿ ਅਧਿਕਾਰ।\
ਨਿਰਨੈ ਕੀਨਸਿ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਕੋ, ਤਿਸ ਪ੍ਰਤਿ ਬਾਕ ਉਚਾਰ ॥੧॥

Sri Guru, the treasure of mercy, listened to all that the yogi had asked, and saw that his mind was pure and that he was fit to be taught;\
so he set out for him the true nature of Brahm,[^1] the One behind all things, and spoke to the yogi in these words. (1)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਭੇ ਜੋਗੇਸ਼੍ਵਰ! ਅਚਰਜ ਅਹੈ। ਅਕਹਿ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਕੋ ਕਿਮੁ ਕੋ ਕਹੈ।\
ਨਾਮ ਅਗੋਚਰ ਭਾਖਤਿ ਤਾਂਹਿ। ਕਿਸੀ ਰਿਖੀਕ ਬਿਸ਼ੈ ਕਬਿ ਨਾਂਹਿ ॥੨॥

"O lord of yogis! This is a wonder beyond all other wonders:\
how can anyone speak of Brahm, which no tongue can ever tell?\
Men give it the name of the Unseen, the one beyond the reach of the senses,\
for never, at any time, is it the object of any of the senses. (2)

ਤਊ ਜਨਾਵਨ ਸਿੱਖਨਿ ਕਾਰਨ। ਕਰੁਨਾ ਕਰਿ ਗੁਰੁ ਕਰਹਿਂ ਉਚਾਰਨ।\
ਰਚਹਿਂ ਸਮਿੱਗ੍ਰੀ ਜੋ ਮਨ ਅਪਨੇ। ਸੱਤਯ ਪ੍ਰਤੀਤਿ ਹੋਤਿ ਸਭਿ ਸੁਪਨੇ ॥੩॥

"Yet, so that his Sikhs may come to know it,\
the Guru, out of his compassion, puts it into words for them.\
Whatever things a man's mind builds up for itself while he is dreaming,\
all of them seem real and true to him for as long as the dream lasts. (3)

ਭੈ ਕੋ ਪਾਇ ਉਠੇ ਬਰੜਾਇ। ਤ੍ਰਿਯ ਸੰਗਮ ਤੇ ਰੇਤ ਮੁਚਾਇ।\
ਹਸਹਿ ਕਹੂੰ, ਕਬਿ ਰੁਦਨ ਪੁਕਾਰੈ। ਕਬਹੁਂ ਬਿਖਾਦ ਅਧਿਕ ਉਰ ਧਾਰੈ ॥੪॥

"In his dream he meets with fear, and starts up from his sleep, babbling;\
he lies with a woman in the dream, and his seed is spilled;\
now he laughs aloud, and now he cries out and weeps,\
and at times he carries a heavy bitterness deep in his heart. (4)

ਜੋ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਮਹਿਂ ਜਾਨੇ ਸਾਚੇ। ਸੋਗ ਹਰਖ ਤੌ ਤਿਹ ਛਿਨ ਰਾਚੇ।\
ਜੇ ਕਰਿ ਕੂਰ ਲਖਹਿ ਇਹੁ ਤਬੈ। ਸੁਪਨ ਕ੍ਰਿਯਾ ਇਹ ਹੋਇ ਨ ਸਬੈ ॥੫॥

"Because he took the things he saw in the dream to be real,\
grief and joy took hold of him in that very moment;\
but if he had known at that time that it was all false,\
none of these workings of the dream would have come about at all. (5)

ਜਿਮ ਬਾਜੀਗਰ ਅਪਨੀ ਮਾਯਾ। ਮਿੱਥਯਾ ਲਖਿ ਸੋਗ ਨ ਹਰਖਾਯਾ।\
ਜਬਿ ਸੁਪਨਾ ਇਸ ਕੋ ਮਿਟਿ ਜਾਵੈ। ਝੂਠੋ ਲਖਹਿ ਨ ਪੁਨ ਬਿਰਮਾਵੈ ॥੬॥

"Just as a conjurer knows the illusion[^2] he himself has made\
to be false, and feels neither grief nor joy because of it,\
so, when this man's dream is wiped away and he wakes,\
he knows it was false, and is never led astray by it again. (6)

ਪਿਖਯੋ ਕੇਹਰੀ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਮਾਂਹੀ। ਜਾਗੇ ਤੇ ਭਾਗੈ ਕਿਤ ਨਾਹੀ।\
ਸੁਪਨ ਸਮਿੱਗ੍ਰੀ ਸਕਲੀ ਲਖਿ ਇਮ। ਹਰਖ ਸ਼ੋਕ ਕੋ ਕਰਹਿ ਨ ਉਰ ਕਿਮ ॥੭॥

"A man may see a lion while he is dreaming,\
but once he is awake he does not run away anywhere at all;\
in the same way, whoever knows all the things of the dream for what they are\
never lets joy or grief take hold of his heart in any way. (7)

ਤਿਸੀ ਰੀਤਿ ਜਾਵਦ ਅੱਗਯਾਨਾ। ਜਗਤ ਸਾਚ ਜਨਿਯਤਿ ਬਿਧਿ ਨਾਨਾ।\
ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤਿ ਇਸ਼ਟ ਹਰਖ ਉਰ ਭਾਰੈ। ਹੋਤਿ ਅਨਿਸ਼ਟ ਸ਼ੋਕ ਕਰਿ ਭਾਰੈ ॥੮॥

"In just the same way, for as long as ignorance[^3] lasts,\
the world, in all its many kinds and forms, seems to be real;\
when a man gets what he longs for, his heart swells with joy,\
and when what he dreads befalls him, he is weighed down with grief. (8)

ਰਾਗ ਦ੍ਵੈਸ਼ ਭੈ ਆਦਿਕ ਜੇਈ। ਇਨ ਕੋ ਧਰਤਿ ਅਨਿਕ ਦੁਖ ਖੇਈ।\
ਬਡੇ ਭਾਗ ਤੇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪਾਇ। ਮਿਟਹਿ ਕਲੇਸ਼ ਸੁ ਲੇਤਿ ਬਚਾਇ ॥੯॥

"Desire and hatred, fear, and all the others like them:\
a man takes these up, and suffers countless pains because of them.\
Then, by great good fortune, he finds the Satguru;\
his afflictions[^4] are wiped away, and the Satguru keeps him safe. (9)

ਜਿਮ ਸੁਪਨੇ ਮਹਿਂ ਸ਼ੇਰ ਕਰਾਲ। ਦੇਖਤਿ ਉਪਜਯੋ ਤ੍ਰਾਸ ਬਿਸਾਲ।\
ਭਾਗਯੋ ਜਾਇ ਨ, ਗਿਰ ਗਿਰ ਜਾਵੈ। ਹੋਤਿ ਅਸ਼ੱਕਯ, ਡਰੇ, ਬਰੜਾਵੈ ॥੧੦॥

"Think of a man who sees a fearsome lion in his dream:\
as he looks at it, a great terror rises up in him;\
he cannot run away, but stumbles and falls, and falls again;\
his strength drains away, he trembles with fear, and he cries out in his sleep. (10)

ਇਤਨੇ ਮਹਿਂ ਜਾਗ੍ਰਤ ਨਰ ਆਯੋ। ਦੁਖਤਿ ਜਾਨ ਕੈ ਪਕਰਿ ਜਗਾਯੋ।\
ਤ੍ਰਸਤਿ ਜਾਨਿ, ਤਿਮ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਦੇਖਹਿਂ। ਉਪਦੇਸ਼ਹਿਂ ਸੁਖ ਦੇਤਿ ਬਿਸ਼ੇਖ਼ਹਿਂ ॥੧੧॥

"Just then a man who is awake comes by,\
sees that he is in torment, takes hold of him and wakes him up;\
in the same way the Satgurus, when they see a man in terror,\
teach him, and give him a peace that is beyond every other. (11)

ਜਨਮ ਮਰਣ ਦ੍ਵੈ ਤੁਝ ਮਹਿਂ ਨਾਂਹੀ। ਭੂਖ ਨ ਤ੍ਰਿਖਾ ਨ ਹ੍ਵੈ ਤੁਹਿ ਮਾਂਹੀ।\
ਹਰਖ ਸ਼ੋਕ ਕੋ ਲੇਸ਼ ਨ ਤੋ ਮੈਂ। ਇਨ ਤੇ ਪਰੇ ਰੂਪ ਲਖਿ ਸੋ -ਮੈਂ- ॥੧੨॥

"'Birth and death, these two, have no place in you at all;\
neither hunger nor thirst can ever be found within you;\
there is not the smallest trace of joy or grief in you;\
know that the form which stands beyond them all is your own *I*.' (12)

ਇੱਤਯਾਦਿਕ ਜਬਿ ਦੈ ਉਪਦੇਸ਼। ਰਿਦੈ ਹਾਨ ਅੱਗਯਾਨ ਅਸ਼ੇਸ਼।\
ਤਬਿ ਇਹੁ ਜਗਤ ਝੂਠਿ ਇਮ ਮਾਨੈ।' ਤਿਸ ਪਰ ਸ਼੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰੁ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਬਖਾਨੈ ॥੧੩॥

"When the Satguru gives teaching such as this, and more like it,\
all the ignorance in the heart is destroyed, with nothing left behind;\
then a man holds this whole world to be false, as false as a dream."\
On this, Sri Guru recited a shabad (one of his own, in Raag Suhi). (13)

> *guri pūrai haumai malu dhoī · kahu nānak merī param gati hoī 4 1*\
> The perfect Guru has washed away the filth of ego; says Nanak, I have reached the highest state.

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

'ਬਾਜੀਗਰ ਜਿਮ ਬਾਜੀ ਪਾਵਹਿ। ਅਨਿਕ ਰੂਪ ਕਹਿ ਸ੍ਵਾਂਗ ਦਿਖਾਵਹਿ।\
ਕ੍ਰਿਯਾ ਕਰਾਵਤਿ ਅਨਿਕ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਰਾ। ਫਿਰਹਿ, ਲਰਹਿ ਬਡ ਬਾਂਧਿ ਅਖਾਰਾ ॥੧੪॥

The Guru explained it: "A conjurer sets out his show,\
and, giving each a name, displays many forms in costume and disguise;\
he makes his figures perform actions of every kind,\
so that they march about, or mark out a great ring and fight in it. (14)

ਇਕ ਬਾਜੀਗਰ ਤਿਨ ਮਹਿਂ ਸਾਚਾ। ਬਾਜੀ ਸਭਿ ਮਿੱਥਯਾ ਕਚ ਪਾਚਾ।\
ਸਗਰੀ ਪੁਤਰੀ ਪੁਰਖ ਅਧੀਨੁ। ਨਾਚਹਿਂ ਜਥਾ ਨਚਾਵਨ ਕੀਨੁ ॥੧੫॥

"Of all of them, only the conjurer himself is real;\
the whole show is false, as flimsy and worthless as glass;\
all the puppets are in the power of that one man,\
and they dance in just the way that he makes them dance. (15)

ਕਿਸਹਿ ਪਲਾਵਹਿ, ਕਿਸੇ ਜਿਤਾਵਹਿ। ਕੋ ਸੋਗੀ ਕੋ ਹਰਖ ਉਪਾਵਹਿ।\
ਜਬਹਿ ਸੰਕੋਚਹਿ ਸਕਲ ਪਸਾਰਾ। ਰਹੈ ਇਕਾਕੀ ਕਰਨੈਹਾਰਾ ॥੧੬॥

"He makes one run away in defeat, and makes another win;\
he makes one of them grieve, and brings joy to another;\
and when at last he folds up the whole of his display,\
the one who made it all remains, alone, by himself. (16)

ਤਿਮ ਜਾਨਹੁਂ ਪਰਮੇਸ਼ੁਰ ਰੂਪ। ਨਿਜ ਇੱਛਾ ਤੇ ਕਰਹਿ ਅਨੂਪ।\
ਅਖਿਲ ਚਰਾਚਰ ਕੋ ਉਪਜਾਯੋ। ਅਪਨ ਸ਼ਕਤਿ ਤੇ ਮਧਿ ਪ੍ਰਵਿਸ਼ਾਯੋ ॥੧੭॥

"In the same way, know the form of the Supreme Lord:\
by his own will he makes this world, beyond compare;\
he brought forth all things, the moving and the unmoving,\
and by his own power he entered into the midst of them. (17)

ਚੇਤਨਤਾ ਜੀਵਨੁ ਕਹੁ ਹੋਈ। ਕ੍ਰਿਆ ਕਰਤਿ ਜਿਸ ਤੇ ਸਭਿ ਕੋਈ।\
ਦੁਖ ਸੁਖ ਤੇ ਹ੍ਵੈ ਬਿਖਮ ਮਹਾਨੇ। ਇਸ ਮਹਿਂ ਕਰਮ ਹੇਤਤਾ ਜਾਨੇ ॥੧੮॥

"So the living beings came to have consciousness,\
and it is by this that every one of them acts;\
through pain and pleasure they pass through great hardship,\
and in all this they believe themselves to be the cause of their deeds. (18)

ਜਬਿ ਚਾਹਤਿ ਇਨ ਲੈ ਕਹੁ ਕਰਿਬੇ। ਅਖਿਲ ਬਿਨਾਸਹਿ ਏਕਹੁ ਥਿਰਬੇ।'\
'ਉਤਪਤਿ ਕੌਨ ਰੂਪ ਇਹੁ ਹੋਇ? ਕਹ ਬਿਨਾਸ਼ਮਾਨ ਹੈ ਕੋਇ?' ॥੧੯॥

"When he wishes to dissolve them all back into himself,\
all things perish, and the One alone remains, unmoved."\
The yogi asked,[^5] "In what form, then, does this creation come to be?\
And what is it, in all of this, that perishes?" (19)

ਇਸ ਪਰ ਸੁਨਹੁਂ ਅਪਰ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟਾਂਤ। ਜਿਸੁ ਬਿਧਿ ਹੋਇ ਸੁਨਹੁਂ ਸਭਿ ਭਾਂਤਿ।\
ਜਲ ਤੇ ਅਧਿਕ ਤਰੰਗ ਉਠੰਤੇ। ਲਘੁ ਦੀਰਘ ਹ੍ਵੈ ਪੁਨ ਬਿਨਸੰਤੇ ॥੨੦॥

The Guru said, "Hear another example of this,\
and hear, in every way, how this comes about:\
countless waves rise up out of the water,\
small ones and great ones, and then they perish once again. (20)

ਭਏ ਤਰੰਗ ਅਹ ਜਲ ਸੋਈ। ਨਾਮ ਮਾਤ੍ਰ ਇਕ ਤਿਨ ਕਹੁ ਹੋਈ।\
ਜੇ ਨਹਿਂ ਜਾਨਤਿ ਸੋ ਇਮੁ ਕਹੀ। -ਭਯੋ ਤਰੰਗ ਸੁ ਜਲ ਹ੍ਵੈ ਨਹੀਂ- ॥੨੧॥

"The waves that came to be are nothing but that same water;\
it is only a name that is given to them, and nothing more;\
but the man who does not know this speaks like this:\
'The wave that has come to be is not water at all.' (21)

ਜੋ ਜਲ ਜਾਨਹਿਂ ਅਖਿਲ ਤਰੰਗੁ। ਸਿੱਧ ਕਰਹਿ ਕਾਰਜ ਸਰਬੰਗ।\
ਪਾਨ ਸ਼ਨਾਨ ਕਰਹਿ ਸਬਿ ਰੀਤਿ। ਤ੍ਰਿਖਾ ਮਲਿਨਤਾ ਬਿਨ ਹ੍ਵੈ ਨੀਤ ॥੨੨॥

"The man who knows that every wave is water\
gets done, in full, every task that water can do:\
he drinks it and he bathes in it in every way,\
and he is always freed from his thirst and from his dirt. (22)

ਅਪਰ ਤੀਸਰੋ ਜੋਗੀ ਸੁਨੋ। ਸ਼ੁਭ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟਾਂਤ ਰਿਦੈ ਇਮੁ ਗੁਨੋ।\
ਕੰਚਨ ਏਕ ਬਨਯੋ ਬਹੁ ਭਾਂਤਿ। ਕੁੰਡਲ, ਕਟਿਕ ਆਦਿ ਦਰਸਾਤ ॥੨੩॥

"Now, yogi, hear a third example besides,\
a fine one, and turn it over like this in your heart:\
a single piece of gold is shaped in many different ways,\
and is seen as earrings, as bracelets, and all the rest. (23)

ਕੰਚਨ ਨਾਮ ਛੋਰਿ ਕਰਿ ਕਹੈਂ। ਇਹੀ ਕਟਿਕ, ਇਹ ਕੁੰਡਲ ਅਹੈ-।\
ਜੇ ਸਰਾਫ ਪਰਖਨ ਤਿਹ ਕਰੈ। ਕੰਚਨ ਬਿਨਾ ਨ ਕੁਛ ਮਨੁ ਧਰੈ ॥੨੪॥

"Men leave aside the name of gold, and say,\
'This one is a bracelet, and this one here is an earring';\
but if a goldsmith puts them to the test,\
he keeps nothing at all in his mind but the gold. (24)

ਨਾਮ ਅਕਾਰ ਗਿਨਹਿ ਨਹਿਂ ਕੋਇ। ਕਨਕ ਵਾਸਤਵ ਪਰਖਹਿ ਸੋਇ।\
ਤਿਮ ਗਯਾਨੀ ਕੋ ਨਾਨਾ ਨਾਂਹਿ। ਸਭਿ ਮਹਿਂ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਰੂਪ ਦਰਸਾਹਿ ॥੨੫॥

"He takes no account of name or shape at all,\
but tests the gold, which is what they truly are;\
in the same way, for the man of knowledge there are not many things,\
and he sees the form of Brahm in every one of them. (25)

ਭੂਖਨ ਤੇ ਪੂਰਬ ਸ਼ੁਭ ਕੰਚਨ। ਭੂਖਨ ਬਨੇ ਭੇਦ ਤਊ ਰੰਚ ਨ।\
ਬਿਨਸੇ ਤੇ ਬਿਨ ਕਨਕ ਨ ਆਨੁ। ਇਮ ਜਾਨਤਿ ਹੈਂ ਜੇ ਬੁਧਿਵਾਨੁ ॥੨੬॥

"Before the ornaments were made, there was pure gold;\
when the ornaments were made, still it was not different in the least;\
when they are broken up, there is nothing left but gold;\
and this is how those who have understanding know it. (26)

ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮ ਜਗਤ ਤੇ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਸਰੂਪ। ਬਨਯੋ ਅਨਿਕ ਆਤਮਾ ਅਨੂਪ।\
ਨਸ਼ਟ ਹੋਹਿ ਨਾਨਾ ਬਿਧਿ ਜੋਇ। ਬਿਨਾਂ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਤੇ ਦੁਤੀ ਨ ਹੋਇ ॥੨੭॥

"Before the world, there was the form of Brahm;\
it became the many selves of living beings, beyond compare;\
and when all things of every kind are destroyed,\
there is nothing second, nothing at all apart from Brahm. (27)

ਆਦਿ ਨਹੀਂ ਜੋ ਅੰਤ ਨ ਰਹੈ। ਮੱਧਯ ਕੁਤੋ ਸਾਚੋ ਤਿਹ ਕਹੈਂ।\
ਯਾਂ ਤੇ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਰੂਪ ਸਭਿ ਜਾਨ। ਅਪਰ ਅਕਾਰ ਭਰਮ ਹੀ ਮਾਨ ॥੨੮॥

"What was not there at the beginning, and does not remain at the end,\
how can anyone call it real in the middle?\
So know that everything there is is the form of Brahm,\
and take every other shape to be nothing but delusion. (28)

ਅਪਰ ਰੀਤਿ ਹੇਰਹੁ ਤਿਸ ਕਰਨੀ। ਜਬਿਹੀ ਬੀਜ ਬੀਜਿਓ ਧਰਨੀ।\
ਅੰਕੁਰ ਹੋਇ ਪਾਤ ਦਰਸਾਵਹਿ। ਸ਼ਾਖਾ ਅਨਿਕ ਫੂਲ ਬਿਗਸਾਵਹਿਂ ॥੨੯॥

"Now see its working in another way," the Guru went on.\
"As soon as a seed has been sown in the earth,\
it sprouts, and its first leaves come into sight,\
and then many branches, and flowers that open wide. (29)

ਸੋ ਇਕ ਬੀਜ ਬਹੁਤ ਬਿਧਿ ਭਯੋ। ਨਾਨਾ ਰੰਗ ਰੂਪ ਨਿਰਮਯੋ।\
ਫਲ ਲਗ ਕੈ ਪੁਨਿ ਪਾਕਨ ਹੋਵਾ। ਬੀਜ ਇਕਾਕੀ ਬਾਕੀ ਜੋਵਾ ॥੩੦॥

"That one seed became many different kinds of things,\
and was shaped into many colours and many forms;\
then fruit set on it, and the fruit ripened,\
and in the end the seed alone was seen to be left. (30)

ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮ ਪਿਛਾਰੀ ਬੀਜਹਿ ਰਹਯੋ। ਮੱਧਯ ਕਾਲ ਜਗ ਕੇ ਸਮ ਲਹਯੋ।\
ਏਕ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਬਹੁ ਬਿਧਿ ਲਖਿ ਜਾਤਿ। ਜੋਗੀ! ਸੁਨਹੁ ਔਰ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟਾਂਤ ॥੩੧॥

"At the first and at the last there was only the seed;\
the time in between, take it to be like the world;\
so the one Brahm is seen in many forms and ways.\
Yogi! Now hear yet another example. (31)

ਲਾਖਹੁਂ ਘਟ ਮਹਿਂ ਏਕ ਗਗਨ ਲਹਿ। ਰਹਹਿ ਅਲੇਪ ਲੇਪ ਜਿਸ ਕੋ ਨਹਿਂ।\
ਜਬਿਹੀ ਫੂਟ ਜਾਹਿਂ ਘਟ ਸਾਰੇ। ਸੋ ਨਭ ਇਕ ਹੀ ਪਰਹਿ ਨਿਹਾਰੇ ॥੩੨॥

"Know that in hundreds of thousands of pots there is one sky;\
it stays untouched, and nothing can ever stain it;\
and as soon as all the pots are broken,\
that sky is seen to be one, and one alone. (32)

ਘਟ ਲੇ ਗਮਨਹਿਂ ਇਤ ਉਤ ਮਾਂਹਿ। ਨਭ ਰਸ ਏਕ ਨ ਆਵਹਿ ਜਾਹਿ।\
ਜੋ ਹੋਵਹਿ ਪੂਰਨ ਸਭਿ ਥਾਨ। ਤਿਸ ਕੋ ਬਨਹਿ ਨ ਆਵਨਿ ਜਾਨੁ ॥੩੩॥

"Men carry the pots about with them, here and there,\
but the sky, the same throughout, neither comes nor goes;\
for that which fills every place to the full\
can have no coming and no going. (33)

ਤਥਾ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਇਕ ਰਸ ਸਭਿ ਮਾਂਹਿ। ਸਭਿਹਿ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਸ਼ਹਿ ਲੇਪਹਿ ਨਾਹਿ।\
ਸਤ ਚੇਤਨ ਆਨੰਦ ਸਰੂਪ। ਜੀਵ ਜੀਵ ਪ੍ਰਤਿ ਅਹੈ ਅਨੂਪ ॥੩੪॥

"Just so, Brahm is one and the same in all things;\
it gives light to them all, and nothing ever clings to it;\
its form is being, consciousness and joy,[^6]\
beyond compare, in each and every living being. (34)

ਭੂਤ ਭਵਿੱਖਯ ਸਦਾ ਹੀ ਰਹੈ। ਸਭਿ ਜੀਵਨ ਮਹਿਂ ਸਤਿ ਇਹੁ ਅਹੈ।\
ਕ੍ਰਿਯਾ ਕਰਤਿ ਚੇਤਨਤਾ ਜਾਨਿ। ਇਹੁ ਭੀ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਰੂਪ ਪਹਿਚਾਨ ॥੩੫॥

"It was in the past, it will be in the future, it always remains;\
this is the being, the truth, in all living beings;\
know the consciousness by which every one of them acts,\
and recognise this, too, as the form of Brahm. (35)

ਬਿਸ਼ਿਯਨ ਮਹਿਂ ਆਨਂਦ ਅੱਗਯਾਨੀ। ਨਿਜਾਨੰਦ ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੈ ਗਯਾਨੀ।\
ਸਭਿ ਕੋ ਭੂਲ ਦੇਹਿ ਧਰਿ ਹੰਤਾ। ਪੁਨਹਿ ਪਦਾਰਥ ਮਮਤਾਵੰਤਾ ॥੩੬॥

"The ignorant look for joy in the objects of the senses," he went on;\
"the man of knowledge has reached the joy of his own self.\
Yet everyone forgets, takes up the body and calls it 'I',[^7]\
and then calls the things of the world 'mine'. (36)

ਏ ਸਭਿ ਮਾਯਾ ਕੇਰਿ ਵਿਕਾਰ। ਜੋ ਭਾਸਤਿ ਹੈਂ ਨਾਨਾਕਾਰ।\
ਨਹੀਂ ਵਾਸਤਵ ਜਾਨਯੋ ਜਾਇ। ਇਹ ਮਾਯਾ ਕੋ ਰੂਪ ਕਹਾਇ ॥੩੭॥

"All these are the workings of Maya,[^8]\
which appear to us in so many shapes and forms;\
through them the Real cannot be known;\
and this is what is called the form of Maya. (37)

-ਮੈਂ ਨਹਿਂ ਜਾਨੌਂ- ਕਰਨ ਬਖਾਨ। ਇਸੈ ਨਾਮ ਮਾਯਾ, ਅਗਯਾਨ।\
ਉਭੈ ਸ਼ਕਤਿ ਕੇ ਧਾਰਨਿਹਾਰੀ। ਇਕ ਆਵਰਨ ਵਿਖੇਪ ਉਚਾਰੀ ॥੩੮॥

"That which makes a man say, 'I do not know,'\
is what is named Maya, and is named ignorance;\
it holds within itself two powers,\
and one of them is called veiling, and the other projection.[^9] (38)

ਕਰਹਿ ਸਰੂਪ ਅਛਾਦਨਿ ਜੋਇ। ਗਯਾਨੀ ਭਨਹਿ ਂ-ਆਵਰਣ- ਸੋਇ।\
ਜਿਹ ਨਾਨੱਤ੍ਵ ਪ੍ਰਤੀਤ ਕਰਾਈ। ਸੋ ਵਿਸੇਖ, ਜਹਿਂ ਕਹਿਂ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟਾਈ ॥੩੯॥

"The power that covers over the true form\
is the one the wise call 'veiling';\
the power that makes the many appear, wherever one looks,\
is the one they call 'projection'. (39)

ਉਪਾਦਾਨ ਇਹ ਸਬਿ ਜਗ ਕੇਰੀ। ਹੈ ਅਘਟਨ ਘਟਨਾ ਸੁ ਘਨੇਰੀ।\
ਜਥਾ ਰਜੂ ਹੁਇ ਬੱਕ੍ਰ ਪਰੀ ਹੈ। ਕਿਹ ਨਰ ਨਿਸ਼ਚੈ ਸੋ ਨ ਕਰੀ ਹੈ ॥੪੦॥

"This is the material cause[^10] of the whole world;\
in great abundance it brings about what could never come about;[^11]\
so it is when a rope lies coiled and twisted on the ground,\
and some man fails to make out what it really is. (40)

ਬਿਨ ਜਾਨੇ ਰਜੂ ਤਿਸ ਕਾਲ। ਲਖਯੋ -ਸਰਪ ਇਹ ਪਰਯੋ ਕਰਾਲ-।\
ਰਜੁ ਧਿਸ਼ਟਾਨ ਪਾਇ ਅੱਗਯਾਨ। ਕਾਰਜ ਕੀਨੋ ਸਰਪੁ ਮਹਾਂਨ ॥੪੧॥

"Not knowing it for a rope at that moment,\
he thought, *A fearsome snake is lying here*;\
ignorance, resting on the rope as its ground,[^12]\
brought forth the great effect of a snake. (41)

ਤਿਮਿ ਮਾਯਾ ਧਿਸ਼ਟਾਨ ਧਰੰਤਿ। ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਆਸਰੇ ਬਿਸ਼ੈ ਕਰੰਤਿ।\
ਜਥਾ ਤਿਮਰ ਹੁਇ ਕੋਸ਼ਠ ਅੰਤਰ। ਕੋਠਾ ਆਸ਼੍ਰੈ ਤਾਂਹਿ ਨਿਰੰਤਰ ॥੪੨॥

"In the same way, Maya takes Brahm as its ground,\
and, resting on Brahm, makes Brahm its object;\
just as darkness lies inside a room,\
and rests upon the room, always and without a break. (42)

ਬਿਨ ਕੋਸ਼ਠ ਅੰਧਕਾਰ ਨਹੀਂ ਹੈ। ਯਾਂ ਤੇ ਆਸ਼੍ਰੈ ਤਾਂਹਿ ਉਹੀ ਹੈ।\
ਪੁਨ ਸੋ ਤਿਮਰ ਫੈਲ ਤਹਿਂ ਹੀ ਕੋ। ਬਿਸ਼ੈ ਕਰਤਿ ਤਿਸ ਕੋਸ਼ਠ ਹੀ ਕੋ ॥੪੩॥

"Without the room there could be no darkness;\
so it is on the room that the darkness rests;\
and then that darkness spreads only through that room,\
and it is that same room that it takes hold of and covers. (43)

ਛਾਦਿ ਲੇਤਿ ਚਹੂੰ ਓਰਨ ਮਾਂਹਿ। ਜਿਸ ਤੇ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟਿ ਪਰਤਿ ਹੈ ਨਾਂਹਿ।\
ਤਥਾ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਕੋ ਮਾਯਾ ਜਾਨਿ। ਆਸ਼੍ਰੈ ਬਿਸ਼ੈ ਕਰਤਿ ਬੁਧਿਵਾਨ ॥੪੪॥

"It shrouds the room on every side,\
so that nothing within it can be seen;\
in just this way the wise know Maya:\
it rests on Brahm, and makes Brahm its object. (44)

ਜਬਿ ਅੱਗਯਾਨ ਸਕਾਰਜ ਸਾਰੇ। ਭ੍ਰਮ ਛੁਟ ਜਾਇ ਸਰੂਪ ਨਿਹਾਰੇ।\
ਪਰਮ ਤੱਤ ਕੋ ਮਤਿ ਕਰਿ ਜਾਨਾ। ਏਕੰਕਾਰ ਮਹਤ ਮਹੀਯਾਨਾ ॥੪੫॥

"When ignorance and all its works[^13] are gone,\
delusion falls away, and a man beholds the true form;\
with his understanding he comes to know the highest reality,\
the One, Ekankar,[^14] greater than the greatest. (45)

ਏਕ ਆਤਮਾ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਅਨਸ਼੍ਵ ਹੈ। ਸਦਾ ਅਨਾਦੀ ਜੋ ਇਕ ਰਸੁ ਹੈ।\
ਆਇ ਨ ਜਾਇ ਕਿਤਹੁਂ ਤੇ ਕੋਈ। ਬਯਾਪਕ ਸਰਬ ਗਗਨ ਸਮ ਸੋਈ ॥੪੬॥

"The one Self, Brahm, is imperishable;\
it is forever without beginning, and always one and the same;\
it does not come from anywhere, and it goes nowhere,\
but pervades everything, as the sky does. (46)

ਅੱਦ੍ਵੈ ਅਨਂਦਾਤਮ ਸਭਿ ਥਾਨ। ਨਿਹਕਲ, ਅੱਕ੍ਰੈ, ਅਜੈ ਮਹਾਂਨ।\
ਗੁਰ ਪੂਰਨ ਸੋਂ ਮਿਲਿ ਹੈ ਜਬੈ। ਹਉਮੈ ਮਲ ਨਿਰਵਾਰਹਿ ਸਬੈ ॥੪੭॥

"It is without a second, the self of joy in every place;\
free of all imagining, free of all action, unborn,[^15] and great;\
when a man meets the perfect Guru,\
all the filth of his ego is cleared away. (47)

ਸਭਿਹਿਨਿ ਤੇ ਉਤਕ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟ ਜੁ ਗਤਿ ਹੈ। ਸੋ ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤਿ ਹੁਇ ਆਨਂਦ ਸਤਿ ਹੈ।\
ਪੁਨਰਾਵਰਤਿ ਨਹੀਂ ਜਿਤ ਹੋਇ। ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤ ਰੂਪ ਏਕ ਰਸੁ ਸੋਇ' ॥੪੮॥

"Then he reaches the state that is higher than all others,\
the state that is joy, and that is true;\
from it there is no coming back[^16] into the world again;\
it is the One, unchanging, whose very form is amrit." (48)

---
*Thus, in Sri Gur Pratap Suraj Granth, in the Second Ras, ends the thirty-fifth ansu, named "The Episode of the Yogi".*

### Notes

[^1]: Brahm: the Absolute of the Vedanta, the one reality behind all that is. The teaching answers the questions the yogi asked at the end of Ras 2.34.
[^2]: The poet's word is māyā, here the conjurer's illusion; it is the same word that names Maya later in the teaching.
[^3]: Ignorance: agyān (avidyā), in the Vedanta the not-knowing that makes the one Brahm appear as many things.
[^4]: Afflictions: klesh. The yoga teaching counts five: ignorance, the sense of I, desire, hatred and clinging to life; the desire, hatred and fear of this verse are three of them.
[^5]: Bhai Vir Singh reads the question as the yogi's, or as the Guru putting it to himself to carry the teaching on.
[^6]: Being, consciousness and joy: sat, chetan, ānand, the Vedanta's sachchidānanda, the nature of Brahm.
[^7]: The poet's words are hantā, the sense of I, and mamtā, the sense of mine.
[^8]: Maya: in the Vedanta, the power by which the one Brahm appears as the world of many forms.
[^9]: Veiling and projection: āvaraṇ and vikshep, the two powers of Maya in the Vedanta, the one hiding the Real, the other throwing out the world of many things. The poet writes the second ਵਿਸੇਖ in the next verse.
[^10]: Material cause: upādān, the stuff a thing is made of, as clay is of the pot (Bhai Vir Singh).
[^11]: The poet's phrase is aghaṭan ghaṭanā, making the impossible happen, the Vedanta's phrase for the power of Maya.
[^12]: Ground: adhishṭhān, the substratum on which a false appearance rests, as the rope lies under the snake.
[^13]: Bhai Vir Singh: the work of ignorance is the visible world; once the true self is seen, ignorance and its world are found to be nothing apart from their ground, as the snake was nothing apart from the rope.
[^14]: Ekankar (ਏਕੰਕਾਰ): the One, the word of the shabad's refrain above.
[^15]: Bhai Vir Singh reads ਨਿਹਕਲ, ਅੱਕ੍ਰੈ, ਅਜੈ as free of imagining, free of action, and unborn; ਅਜੈ could also be read as unconquered.
[^16]: Coming back: punarāvṛtti, returning to be born again in the world (Bhai Vir Singh).

# Panth Prakash, Episode 43

## The Episode of Maler

### (maler koṭle dī sudhāī: Malerkotla is brought to account)

>**Questions**
>
>1. Kishan Das's request?
>2. What was done with Khwaja Khidar's body?
>3. Miracle of the Panipat banners?

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਬੰਦੇ ਮਲੇਰਹਿ ਚੜ੍ਹਤੀ ਕਰੀ ॥ ਸੋਊ ਮਲੇਰਹਿ ਧਾਕ ਤਿਮ ਪਰੀ ॥\
ਲੂਟ ਕਤਲ ਕੀ ਉਠੀ ਅਵਾਜ਼ ॥ ਤੁਰੇ ਲੁਟੇਰੇ ਬਹੁ ਤਿਸ ਕਾਜ ॥੧॥

Banda marched out against Maler[^1] at the head of his army,\
and the same dread fell upon Maler as on the other towns;\
a cry went up everywhere of plunder and of slaughter to come,\
and a great many plunderers set out to have their share of that work. (1)

ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਵਾਕ ਉਹਾਂ ਆਇ ਅੜੋ ॥ ਆਵਤ ਜਮ ਦਲ ਕਰ ਦੀਓ ਖੜੋ ॥\
ਔਰ ਚਹਿਯਤ ਗੁਰ ਔਰ ਬਣਾਈ ॥ ਦੇਖਹੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਉਲਟ ਬਡਆਈ ॥੨॥

But the Satguru's word came and stood firm in the way there,\
and the host of death that was coming on, it brought to a halt;[^2]\
one thing was wanted, but the Guru made it into another;\
see the greatness of the Satguru, who turned it all around. (2)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਏਕ ਸਮੈਂ ਬੰਦਾ ਤਹਾਂ ਥੋ ਰਹਯੋ ਮਹੀਨੇ ਚਾਰ ॥\
ਕਿਸ਼ਨ ਦਾਸ ਸੇਵਾ ਕਰੀ ਬਣੀਏਂ ਵਿਚ ਬਜ਼ਾਰ ॥੩॥

Once, long before, Banda had stayed there in Maler for four whole months together,\
and a merchant named Kishan Das, who kept his shop in the bazaar, had served him then.[^3] (3)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਸੁਨਤ ਸੁ ਬਣੀਓ ਆਇ ਸੁ ਮਿਲਯੋ ॥ ਉਸਕੋ ਬੰਦੋ ਆਦਰ ਕਰਯੋ ॥\
ਕਹਯੋ ਮਾਂਗ ਜੋ ਮਾਂਗਨ ਜੋਗਾ ॥ ਉਸ ਮਾਂਗਯੋ ਦੁਖ ਦੇਹ ਨ ਲੋਗਾ ॥੪॥

Hearing that Banda had come, that merchant came and met him,\
and Banda received him with honour and showed him respect;\
he said to him, "Ask for whatever is fit and worth asking for";\
the merchant asked, "Do not cause the people here any pain. (4)

ਕਹਰ ਕਤਲ ਤੇ ਸਭ ਰਖ ਲੇਹੋ ॥ ਪੈਸਾ ਚਾਹੋ ਤੇਤਾ ਲੋਹੋ ॥\
ਪਿਛਲ ਸਯਾਣ ਉਨ ਆਨ ਸੁਨਾਈ ॥ ਇਮ ਗੁਰ ਬਚ ਤਬ ਅੜਯੋ ਆਈ ॥੫॥

"Save them all from your wrath and from the slaughter;\
take from us as much money as you want, whatever you ask."\
He spoke to Banda of their old acquaintance from before;\
and so the Guru's word came and stood firm in the way. (5)

ਭਾਜ ਪਠਾਣ ਗਏ ਤਹਿਂ ਸਾਰੇ ॥ ਬਚੇ ਲੜਾਈਓਂ ਜੋ ਨਹਿਂ ਮਾਰੇ ॥\
ਜਾਇ ਰਲੇ ਵਹਿ ਦਿੱਲੀ ਲਹੌਰ ॥ ਉਰੇ ਬਚੈਂ ਨਹਿਂ ਕਿਸ ਹੀ ਠੌਰ ॥੬॥

All the Pathans who were there ran away and fled,\
the ones who had come through the battles and had not been killed;\
they went off and joined the armies at Delhi and at Lahore,\
for anywhere on this side of those cities they were safe in no place at all. (6)

ਪਠਾਣਨ ਮਾਲ ਸੁ ਜਪਤੀ ਕਰੀ ॥ ਸ਼ਹਰ ਉਗਰਾਹੀ ਪੈਸੇ ਧਰੀ ॥\
ਲੈ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੋ ਦੀਨੋ ਬਾਂਟ ॥ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਆਛੈ ਦਿਨ ਭਏ ਕਾਟ ॥੭॥

Banda confiscated all the goods and property of the Pathans,\
and laid a levy of money on the city, to be paid to him;\
he took it and shared it out among the Singhs,\
and the Singhs passed their days in plenty and in ease. (7)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਫੌਜ ਟਕਿਆਨ ਸੌਂ ਸੂਧੀ ਭਈ ਔ ਮੁਲਕ ਉਗਰਾਹੇ ਔਰ ॥\
ਆਕੀ ਰਹਯੋ ਸੁ ਨਹਿਂ ਛਡਯੋ ਸੋ ਮਾਰਯੋ ਕਰ ਧੌਰ ॥੮॥

The army was set up well, with money in its hands, and more of the country paid its revenue;\
whoever stayed in rebellion against him was not spared, but was struck down by force. (8)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਅਖਵਾਜ ਖਿਦਰੁ ਜੋ ਹੁਤ ਮਰਦੂਦ ॥ ਪਠਾਣਨ ਮੈਂ ਥੋ ਬਡੋ ਗਦੂਦ ॥\
ਕੱਢਿ ਗੋਰ ਤੇ ਖੜੋ ਕਰਾਯੋ ॥ ਸਾਥ ਕੰਧ ਕੈ ਉਸੈ ਟਿਕਾਯੋ ॥੯॥

Then there was Khwaja Khidar,[^4] the man who had been named the Accursed,[^5]\
who had been the hardest knot of gristle[^6] among the Pathans;\
the Singhs dug him up out of his grave and stood him up on his feet,\
and propped him up there, leaning against a wall. (9)

ਬੰਦੈ ਕਹਯੋ ਥੋ ਬਡੋ ਜੁਆਨ ॥ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਮਾਰਯੋ ਗਿਦੜ ਸਮਾਨ ॥\
ਆਗ ਲਗਾਇ ਫੂਕ ਉਸ ਦਯੋ ॥ ਔਰ ਦੂਜੋ ਸ਼ੇਖ ਸਦੋ ਫੂਕਵੈਯੋ ॥੧੦॥

Banda said, "This was a big, strong man, in the prime of his youth";\
and yet the Singhs had struck him down like a jackal.\
They set fire to the body and burned it to ashes,\
and they had another one burned as well, Sheikh Sado.[^7] (10)

ਬੁੱਢੋ ਪੀਰ ਜੁ ਮਲੇਰ ਸਦਾਯੋ ॥ ਤ੍ਰਿਯਾ ਪੁਰਸ਼ ਉਸ ਦੇਹਿਂ ਪਹਿਰਾਯੋ ॥\
ਉਸ ਕੀ ਜਾਗਾ ਦਈ ਢਹਾਇ ॥ ਓਇ ਭੀ ਕੱਢ ਦੀਯੋ ਗੋਰੌਂ ਫੁਕਾਇ ॥੧੧॥

There was the Old Pir, as he was called in Maler,\
to whose tomb women and men alike brought cloths to dress it;\
they pulled his shrine down and levelled it with the ground,\
and they dug him too out of his grave and had him burned.[^8] (11)

ਤੁਰਕ ਮੁਲਕ ਸਭ ਲੀਯੋ ਉਗਰਾਹਿ ॥ ਦਿਯੋ ਮਲਵਈਅਨ ਮਿਲੇ ਜੁ ਅਇ ॥\
ਭਾਈ ਫਤੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਕੀਓ ਮੁਹਿਰੈਲ ॥ ਮਲਵਈ ਲਗਾਏ ਸਭ ਉਸ ਗੈਲ ॥੧੨॥

Banda made all the country of the Turks[^9] pay its revenue,\
and he gave it to the Malwais[^10] who had come and joined him;\
he made Bhai Fateh Singh[^11] the leader at the head of them,\
and he set all the Malwais to follow behind him. (12)

ਮਲਵੀਅਨ ਕੋ ਸੁ ਕੀਓ ਨਿਬਾਬ ॥ ਬੰਦੈ ਦੀਨੋ ਉਸੈ ਖਿਤਾਬ ॥੧੩॥

He made Bhai Fateh Singh the nawab over all the Malwais, their chief and ruler,\
and it was Banda himself who gave him that title, the title of nawab. (13)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਹਿਸਾਰ ਬਾਵਨੀ ਪਰਗਣੋ ਲੀਨੇ ਕਈ ਮਿਲਾਇ ॥\
ਕੈਂਥਲ ਜੀਂਦ ਪਾਣੀਪਤੋ ਮੂਣਕ ਕਰਨਾਲ ਰਲਾਇ ॥੧੪॥

Hisar and the Bavani,[^12] many a district he took and joined together under his rule,\
and he added to them Kaithal, and Jind, and Panipat, and Munak, and Karnal as well.[^13] (14)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਪਾਣੀਪਤ ਝੰਡੇ ਬੰਦੈ ਗਡਵਾਏ ॥ ਤੁਰਕ ਫੌਜ ਕੋਊ ਲੰਘਣ ਨ ਪਾਏ ॥\
ਚੋਰੀ ਜੋਰੀ ਸੋ ਪੱਟੈ ਜੋਊ ॥ ਤਿਮ ਤਿਮ ਬਹੁ ਡੂੰਘੋ ਅਤਿ ਹੋਊ ॥੧੫॥

At Panipat Banda had his banners planted in the ground,\
so that no army of the Turks could get past that way;\
whoever tried to pull one up, by stealth or by force,\
the harder he pulled, the deeper and deeper it sank. (15)

ਕਟੈ ਡੋਰੀ ਕੋਊ ਸੰਗ ਹਥਿਆਰ ॥ ਗਿਰੇ ਬਾਹਿਂ ਤਿਹ ਪਹਿਲੇ ਵਾਰ ॥\
ਰਖਯੋ ਨ ਰਾਖੋ ਉਸ ਪਹਿ ਕੋਇ ॥ ਤੁਰਕ ਢੁਕਤ ਅਗ ਤਨ ਲਗੈ ਸੋਇ ॥੧੬॥

If anyone tried to cut its ropes with a weapon in his hand,\
his own arm dropped off at the very first stroke he struck;\
no guard at all was set there to keep watch over the banners,\
for when a Turk came close to one, his body burst into flames. (16)

Punjabi commentary from the Prachin Panth Prakash Steek, part 1, of Singh Sahib Baba Santa Singh Akali, Jathedar of the 96 Krori Budha Dal. Kulwant Singh is named in full in the notes; Baba Santa Singh Akali is BSS. Cite as Panth Prakash 43.4.*

### Notes

[^1]: Maler: Malerkotla, the town of the Pathans south-west of Sirhind.
[^2]: BSS: the word Guru Gobind Singh spoke for Sher Muhammad of Malerkotla, who protested at the killing of the Sahibzadas, saying that his root would remain (Episode 38).
[^3]: BSS: in the days when Banda wandered as a faqir he spent the four months of the rains at Malerkotla, and Kishan Das, a bania, looked after him.
[^4]: Khwaja Khidar: killed at Ropar (Episode 38); the poet writes Akhvāj Khidar, usually Khwaja Khizar.
[^5]: Accursed: the poet's mardūd (Persian), cast out, accursed, the name the Guru gave him.
[^6]: hardest knot of gristle: the poet's gadūd, a gland, a hard lump; a man hard to kill (Episode 38).
[^7]: Sheikh Sado: the poet's line may also read "another, called a Sheikh". Kulwant Singh: Sheikh Sadruddin, the founder of Malerkotla.
[^8]: The second line is unclear. BSS: women and men alike made offerings to him. BSS adds that Banda burned him by the power of a mantra.
[^9]: Turks: the poet's word for the Muslim rulers and their armies.
[^10]: Malwais: the Singhs of the Malwa, the country south of the Satluj.
[^11]: Bhai Fateh Singh: Kulwant Singh: of the family of Bhai Bhagtu, a Sikh of Guru Gobind Singh's time.
[^12]: Bavani: the fifty-two, the district of Sirhind (Episode 41).
[^13]: Hisar, Kaithal, Jind, Panipat, Munak and Karnal: towns and their districts south and south-east of Sirhind, most of them in present-day Haryana, reaching as far as Panipat on the road to Delhi.

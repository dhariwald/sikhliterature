# Ras 2, Ansu 36

## The Yogi Is Taught and Set Free

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਸੁਨਿ ਜੋਗੀ ਬਿਸਮੈ ਭਯੋ, ਬਾਨੀ ਸੁਧਾ ਸਮਾਨ।\
ਪੁਨਹਿ ਪ੍ਰਸ਼ਨ ਕੀਨਸਿ ਭਲੇ, ਨਿਰਨੈ ਕਰਨ ਮਹਾਂਨ ॥੧॥

When the yogi heard this he was struck with wonder, for the Guru's words were like nectar to him;\
and once again he put fine and searching questions, to settle the great matter once and for all. (1)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

'ਕੌਨ ਬਾਦ ਕਰਿ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਸਿੱਧ ਕਿਯ? ਜਿਸ ਤੇ ਸਰਬ ਪ੍ਰਪੰਚ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟਿ ਥਿਯ।\
ਕੰਚਨ ਭੂਖਨ ਕੋ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟਾਂਤਿ। ਵਾਦ ਪ੍ਰਣਾਮ ਭਯੋ ਵੱਖਯਾਤ ॥੨॥

"By which doctrine has Brahm[^1] been proven and established," he asked,\
"Brahm, out of which this whole visible world[^2] has come to be seen?\
With the example of gold and the ornaments that are made from it,\
the doctrine of transformation[^3] has become famous everywhere. (2)

ਹੇਮੁ ਪ੍ਰਣਾਮਯੋ ਨਿਜ ਅਕਾਰ। ਕੰਕਨ ਕੁੰਡਲਾਦਿ ਲੰਕਾਰ।\
ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਪ੍ਰਣੰਮ ਹੋਤਿ ਭਾ ਤੈਸੇ। ਜਗਤ ਚਰਾਚਰ ਉਪਜਯੋ ਐਸੇ ॥੩॥

Gold changes its own shape and becomes bracelets,\
earrings and every other kind of ornament that people wear;\
in just the same way Brahm changes and transforms itself,\
and so the whole world, the moving and the unmoving, was born. (3)

ਜਿਸ ਮਾਯਾ ਕੇ ਅਪੁ ਬਤਾਵੌ। ਸੋ ਝੂਠੀ ਕੈ ਸਾਚ ਜਨਾਵੌ।\
ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਸਰੂਪ ਬਿਲੋਕਯੋ ਕੈਸੇ। -ਸੂਖਮ ਤੇ ਸੂਖਮ- ਕਹਿ ਐਸੇ' ॥੪॥

And that Maya[^4] of which you yourself have spoken,\
make it known to me: is it false, or is it true?\
And how can the true form of Brahm ever be seen,\
when men describe it like this, as 'subtler than the subtle'?" (4)

ਮਹਾਂ ਪੁਰਖ ਜੋਗੀ ਤੇ ਸੁਨਿ ਕੈ। ਉੱਤਰ ਦੀਨਸਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਗੁਨਿ ਕੈ।\
'ਵਿਵਰਤ ਵਾਦਿ ਇਹੁ ਹਮ ਨੇ ਕਹਯੋ। ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਯ ਪ੍ਰਪੰਚ ਬ੍ਰਹਮੁ ਤੇ ਲਹਯੋ ॥੫॥

Hearing all this from the yogi, that great man,\
the Satguru weighed it well in his mind and gave his answer:\
"What we have taught you is the doctrine of appearance:[^5]\
this visible world is seen, appearing out of Brahm. (5)

ਜਥਾ ਰਜੂ ਤੇ ਸ੍ਰਪ ਉਪਜੰਤਾ। ਭੈ ਆਦਿਕ ਤੇ ਕੰਪ ਉਠੰਤਾ।\
ਤੈਸੇ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਤੇ ਜਗ ਇਹੁ ਦੀਖਾ। ਹਰਖ ਸ਼ੋਕ ਦੇ ਤਿਸੇ ਸਰੀਖ਼ਾ ॥੬॥

Just as a snake seems to rise up out of a rope, though it is only rope,\
and fear and all the rest of it set a man trembling,\
so this world is seen, appearing out of Brahm,\
and it gives joy and grief exactly as that snake does. (6)

ਬਿਨਾਂ ਗਯਾਨ ਤੇ ਮਿਟੇ ਜੁ ਨਾਂਹੀ। ਸਦਾ ਸਸ਼ੰਕਤਿ ਦੁਖ ਸੁਖ ਮਾਂਹੀ।\
ਜਨਮ ਅਨੇਕਨ ਮਹਿਂ ਬਿਰਮਾਵਤਿ। ਊਚੈ ਨੀਚੈ ਬਹੁਤ ਭ੍ਰਮਾਵਤਿ ॥੭॥

Without true knowledge that false snake is never wiped away;\
a man lives always in doubt, caught between sorrow and joy,\
and he wanders lost through many and many births,\
driven to wander high and low, again and again. (7)

ਵਾਦ ਪ੍ਰਣਾਮ ਅਪਰ ਬਿਧਿ ਮਾਨੋ। ਰੂਪ ਦੂਧ ਤੇ ਦਧਿ ਹੁਇ ਜਾਨੋ।\
ਦਧਿ ਤੇ ਬਹੁਰ ਦੁਗਧ ਹੁਇ ਨਾਂਹਿ। ਪੂਰਬ ਰੂਪ ਨਾਸ ਭਾ ਤਾਂਹਿ ॥੮॥

Now take the doctrine of transformation to be of another kind:\
it is like milk, when its form is changed and it turns into curd,\
for curd can never turn back again into milk;\
the earlier form of the milk has been destroyed for good. (8)

ਹਾਟਕ ਹੋਇ ਨ ਐਸੀ ਭਾਂਤਿ। ਭੂਖਨ ਮਹਿਂ ਸਰੂਪ ਦਰਸਾਤ।\
ਬਿਨਸੇ ਅਲੰਕਾਰ ਆਕਾਰ। ਪੂਰਬ ਰੂਪ ਹੇਮ ਲੇ ਧਾਰਿ ॥੯॥

Gold does not behave in that way at all;\
its own nature can still be seen inside the ornaments,\
and when the shape of the ornament is broken up and destroyed,\
the gold takes on its earlier form once again. (9)

ਮਾਯਾ ਕਰਿ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਤੇ ਜਗ ਭਾਸਾ। ਜਿਮ ਬਾਜੀਗਰ ਕੇਰ ਤਮਾਸ਼ਾ।\
ਹੋਹਿ ਬਿਨਾਸ਼ ਜਬੈ ਅੱਗਯਾਨ। ਨਹਿਂ ਪੁਨ ਰਹੈ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਬਿਨੁ ਆਨ ॥੧੦॥

It is through Maya that the world appears out of Brahm,\
like the show a conjurer puts on to fool the eye;\
and when ignorance[^6] is at last destroyed,\
then nothing at all remains except Brahm alone. (10)

ਨਹਿਂ ਅਸੱਤਯ ਨਹਿਂ ਸੱਤਯ ਸੁ ਮਾਯਾ। ਸੱਤਯਾਸੱਤਯ ਭੀ ਨਹਿਂ ਬਨਿ ਆਯਾ।\
ਲਖਹੁ ਅਨਰ ਬਚਨੀ ਇਹੁ ਯਾਂ ਤੇ। ਕਿਹ ਬਿਧਿ ਕਹਿਬੇ ਜਾਹਿ ਨ ਕਾ ਤੇ ॥੧੧॥

Maya is not untrue, and Maya is not true,\
nor can it be called both true and untrue together;\
so know from this that it cannot be described:[^7]\
there is no way in which anyone can put it into words. (11)

ਹੈ ਅਨਾਦਿ ਜਿਸੁ ਆਦਿ ਨ ਮਾਨ। ਅੰਤ ਪਾਇ ਉਪਜੇ ਜਬਿ ਗਯਾਨ।\
ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੇ ਸੁਨਿਬੈ ਉਪਦੇਸ਼ੁ। ਹਤਹਿ ਅਬਿੱਦਯਾ ਸਹਤ ਕਲੇਸ਼ ॥੧੨॥

Maya has no beginning, and where it began cannot be known;\
it reaches its end only when true knowledge is born.\
When a man listens to the teaching of the Satguru,\
ignorance is slain, together with all of its afflictions.[^8] (12)

ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਆਤਮਾ ਪੂਰਨ ਜੋਇ। ਬਿਸ਼ੈ ਨ ਕਿਸੁ ਇੰਦ੍ਰੈ ਕਬਿ ਹੋਇ।\
ਦਿਖਿਯ ਨ ਆਂਖਨਿ, ਸੁਨਿਯ ਨ ਕਾਨੀ। ਰਿਦੈ ਲਖਹਿ ਪ੍ਰੱਗਯਾ ਤੇ ਗਯਾਨੀ ॥੧੩॥

"Brahm, the Self, which is whole and fills everything," the Guru went on,\
"is never at any time the object of any of the senses.\
It cannot be seen with the eyes or heard with the ears;\
the man of knowledge perceives it in his heart, through understanding. (13)

ਇੰਦ੍ਰੈ ਬਿਸ਼ੈ ਨ ਕਰਿ ਜਿਨ ਮਨ ਕੋ। ਬਿਸ਼ੈ ਕਰਤਿ ਮਨ ਇਨ ਸਭਿਹਿਨਿ ਕੋ।\
ਤਿਮ ਮਨੁ ਬਿਸ਼ੈ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਨਹਿਂ ਹੋਇ। ਸਭਿਹਿਨਿ ਕੋ ਸੱਤਾ ਦੇ ਸੋਇ ॥੧੪॥

Just as the senses can never make the mind their object,\
while the mind makes every one of the senses its object,\
so Brahm can never become the object of the mind;\
it is Brahm that gives being to them all. (14)

ਸਭਿਨਿ ਅਧਾਰ ਭੂਤ ਇਕ ਅਹੈ। ਨਾਨਾ ਨਸਹਿ ਨੀਕ ਜੇ ਲਹੈ।\
ਸੂਖਮ ਤੇ ਅਤਿ ਸੂਖਮ ਜਾਨ। ਕਹਿਯਤਿ ਮਹਾਂ ਮਹਿਦ ਮਹੀਆਨ ॥੧੫॥

It is the one support on which all things rest;\
all this many-ness vanishes if a man grasps it well.\
Know it to be subtler than the very subtlest thing,\
and it is called great, the greatest of all the great.[^9] (15)

ਸੂਰਜ ਆਦਿ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਸ਼ਤਿ ਜੇਈ। ਤਿਸ ਕੀ ਜੋਤਿ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਸ਼ਹਿ ਏਈ।\
ਕਾਰਣ ਭੂਤ ਸਕਲ ਕੋ ਏਕ। ਹੁਇ ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤਿ ਉਰ ਕਰਤਿ ਬਿਬੇਕ ॥੨੬॥

The sun and all the other lights that shine in the sky\
shine only because the light of Brahm is shining in them;\
it is the one cause of everything there is,\
and it is reached by the man who practises discernment in his heart. (16)

ਗਗਨ ਰੂਪ ਪਸਰਯੋ ਸਭਿ ਮਾਂਹਿ। ਨਿਤ ਕੂਟੱਸਥ ਨ ਆਵਹਿ ਜਾਹਿ।\
ਤਿਸ ਮਹਿਂ ਥਿਰਤਾ ਮਨ ਕੀ ਪਾਵੈ। ਸੋ ਜਨੁ ਅੰਮ੍ਰਿਤਮਯ ਹੁਇ ਜਾਵੈ ॥੧੭॥

Like the sky it is spread out through everything,\
eternal and changeless,[^10] never coming and never going.\
Whoever finds the stillness of his mind in it,\
that person becomes one made of amrit, beyond the reach of death. (17)

ਜਿਨਹੁ ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਮਯ ਜਗਤ ਪਛਾਤਾ। ਤਜਿ ਨਾਨੱਤ੍ਵ ਅਨਂਦ ਇਕ ਮਾਤਾ।\
ਕਹੀਅਤਿ ਜੀਵਨ ਮੁਕਤ ਸੁ ਪ੍ਰਾਨੀ। ਮੂਲ ਕਲੇਸ਼ ਅਵਿੱਦਯਾ ਹਾਨੀ ॥੧੮॥

"Those who have recognised the world as Brahm itself," said the Satguru,\
"who have left the many behind and are drunk with the one joy,\
such beings are called liberated while they are still living,[^11]\
for they have destroyed ignorance, the root of every affliction. (18)

ਜਹਾਂ ਹੋਹਿ ਤਹਿਂ ਦੇਹ ਬਿਨਾਸਾ। ਥਲ ਅਪਵਿੱਤ੍ਰ ਕਿ ਤੀਰਥਿ ਪਾਸਾ।\
ਤਹਾਂ ਅਨਂਦ ਮੈਂ ਲਯਤਾ ਪਾਇ। ਜਨਮੁ ਮਰਨ ਨਹਿਂ ਆਇ ਨ ਜਾਇ ॥੧੯॥

Wherever it may be that their body perishes,\
whether in an unclean place or beside a place of pilgrimage,\
there they are absorbed into joy and lost in it;\
for them there is no birth and no death, no coming and no going. (19)

ਰਹਹੁ ਦੇਹ ਕੈਧੋਂ ਬਿਨਸਾਇ। ਨਹਿਂ ਸੋਚਹਿ ਉਰ ਇਕਰਸੁ ਭਾਇ।\
ਅਗਨਿ ਸਿੰਧੁ ਕੇ ਤਟ ਪਰ ਹੋਇ। ਸਗਲੀ ਦ੍ਵੈਤ ਜਿਨਹੁਂ ਨੇ ਖੋਇ' ॥੨੦॥

Whether the body remains or whether it perishes,\
they do not worry, for their hearts hold one unchanging feeling;\
though they live on the very shore of the ocean of fire,[^12]\
they have lost all sense of duality, every last trace of it." (20)

ਇਮੁ ਕਹਿ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟਿ ਗੁਰ ਹੇਰੀ। ਕਰੀ ਬਿਨਾਸ਼ ਮੋਹੁ ਕੀ ਬੇਰੀ।\
ਸੁੱਧ ਗਯਾਨ ਜੋਗੀ ਉਰ ਜਾਗਾ। ਜਨਮਾਦਿਕ ਮਦ ਪੀਵਤਿ ਤਯਾਗਾ ॥੨੧॥

Having spoken in this way, the Guru looked on him with a glance of grace,[^13]\
and broke apart the shackles of attachment that held him;\
pure knowledge awoke within the yogi's heart,\
and he gave up drinking the wine of pride in birth and all the rest. (21)

ਗੁਰੂ ਬਾਕ ਕੋ ਲਖਯੋ ਪ੍ਰਤਾਪਾ। ਜਿਸ ਕੇ ਸੁਨਤਿ ਮੋਹੁ ਸਭਿ ਖਾਪਾ।\
ਨੰਮ੍ਰ ਭਯੋ ਚਰਨਨ ਸਿਰ ਧਰਯੋ। ਅਪਨੋ ਗਯਾਨ ਨਿਵੇਦਨ ਕਰਯੋ ॥੨੨॥

He came to know the glory and the power of the Guru's word,\
at the hearing of which all his attachment was destroyed;\
humbled, he laid his head down at the Guru's feet,\
and told the Guru all that he had now come to know. (22)

'ਅਬਿ ਮੋ ਕਹੁ ਨਿਸ਼ਚਾ ਦ੍ਰਿੜ੍ਹ ਭਯੋ। ਹੁਤੋ ਜਾਨਿਬੋ ਜਾਨਿ ਸੁ ਲਯੋ।\
ਤੁਮ ਕਰੁਨਾ ਤੇ ਸੰਸੈ ਨਾਸ਼ੇ। ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤਿ ਚੇਤਨ ਆਨਂਦ ਰਾਸੇ ॥੨੩॥

"Now my conviction has become firm and unshaken," he said;\
"all that was there to be known, I have come to know.\
Through your compassion all my doubts are destroyed,\
and I have reached the treasure of consciousness and joy. (23)

ਅਬਿ ਸ਼ਰੀਰ ਮੈਂ ਤਯਾਗਨ ਕਰਿ ਹੌਂ। ਜਿਯਤਿ ਰਹੌਂ ਨਹਿਂ ਕਾਜ ਬਿਚਰਿ ਹੌਂ।\
ਜੋ ਤਰਤੱਬ ਸੁ ਮੈਂ ਕਰਿ ਲੀਨਸਿ। ਤੁਮਨੇ ਪੂਰ ਕਾਮਨਾ ਕੀਨਸਿ' ॥੨੪॥

Now I shall leave this body behind me;\
I have thought it over, and I have no work left to live for.\
The essence that was there to be grasped, I have grasped;[^14]\
you have fulfilled the whole desire of my heart." (24)

ਸੁਨਿ ਸ਼੍ਰੀ ਗੁਰ ਨੇ ਬਹੁ ਸਨਮਾਨਾ। 'ਧੰਨ ਧੰਨ ਤੁਮ ਬਡ ਬੁਧਿਵਾਨਾ।\
ਦਰਸ਼ਨ ਦੇਹੁ ਰਹਹੁ ਹਿਤ ਕਰੀਏ। ਨਾਨਾ ਜਗ ਮਹਿਂ ਏਕ ਨਿਹਰੀਏ ॥੨੫॥

Hearing this, Sri Guru honoured him greatly and said:\
"Blessed, blessed are you, a man of great wisdom.\
Stay on, and out of kindness give the people a sight of you;\
in all the many things of the world, see only the One. (25)

ਲੋਕਨ ਪਰ ਉਪਕਾਰ ਤੁਮਾਰਾ। ਦੇਖਤਿ ਪਾਪਨਿ ਹਰਹਿਂ ਬਿਕਾਰਾ।\
ਪਰਹਿਂ ਸੁਮਗ ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤਿ ਕੱਲਯਾਨਾ। ਜੀਵ ਮਲੀਨ ਪੂਤ ਹੁਇ ਨਾਨਾ' ॥੨੬॥

Your staying would be a great kindness to the people;\
at the very sight of you, sinners would have their vices taken away,\
they would set foot on the good path and gain liberation,\
and many souls soiled with sin would be made pure." (26)

ਸੁਨਿ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਤੇ ਮ੍ਰਿਦੁਲ ਸੁਹਾਏ। ਪੁਨ ਜੋਗੀ ਨੇ ਬਾਕ ਸੁਣਾਏ।\
'ਨਰਨਿ ਉਧਾਰਨ ਤੁਮਰੇ ਹੱਥ। ਅਪਰ ਕਿਸੂ ਮਹਿਂ ਨਹਿਂ ਸਮਰੱਥ ॥੨੭॥

Hearing these soft and gracious words from the Satguru,\
the yogi spoke once more and said to him:\
"The saving of men lies in your hands alone;\
there is no one else who has the power to do it. (27)

ਇਸ ਹਿਤ ਰਾਵਰ ਨੇ ਤਨ ਧਾਰਯੋ। ਸੱਤਯ ਨਾਮ ਸਿਮਰਨ ਬਿਸਤਾਰਯੋ।\
ਇਮੁ ਸਭਿ ਜਗ ਕੋ ਭਾਰ ਜਿ ਧਰਨੋ। ਤੁਮ ਬਿਨ ਅਪਰ ਕੌਨ ਇਮੁ ਕਰਨੋ ॥੨੮॥

It was for this that you, revered one, took on a body,\
and spread far and wide the remembrance of Satnam,[^15]\
taking on yourself in this way the burden of the whole world;\
apart from you, who else could ever do such a thing? (28)

ਮੁਝ ਕੋ ਆਇਸੁ ਦੇਹੁ ਸਮਾਵੌਂ। ਰਹਿਬੇ ਕੋ ਨ ਸੰਕਲਪ ਉਠਾਵੌਂ।\
ਅਬਹਿ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਇਸ ਬਿਧਿ ਕੀ ਕਰੋ। ਜਿਸੁ ਤੇ ਆਪਨਿ ਤਨ ਪਰਹਰੋਂ' ॥੨੯॥

Give me the command to merge into the One;\
I do not raise even a single thought of staying on.\
Grant me now a grace of such a kind\
that by it I may leave behind this body of mine." (29)

ਦਿਢ ਨਿਸ਼ਚਾ ਜੋਗੀ ਕੋ ਜਾਨਾ। ਸ਼੍ਰੀ ਅਰਜਨੁ ਬਿਗਸੰਤਿ ਬਖਾਨਾ।\
'ਕਰੋ ਜਥਾ ਅਭਿਲਾਖ ਤੁਮਾਰੀ। ਜਿਮੁ ਉਚਰਹੁ ਤਿਮ ਹਮ ਅਨੁਸਾਰੀ' ॥੩੦॥

Seeing how firm and unshaken the yogi's conviction was,\
Sri Arjan, his face opening into a smile, said to him:\
"Do just as you yourself desire;\
as you say, so we shall follow you in it." (30)

ਪੁਨ ਬੁੱਢੇ ਮੋਂ ਗੁਰੂ ਬਖਾਨੀ। 'ਜਿਮ ਇਹੁ ਜੋਗੀ ਭਾਖੈ ਬਾਨੀ।\
ਤਿਮ ਤੁਮ ਸੰਗ ਹੋਇ ਕਰਿ ਕਰੀਏ। ਪੁਰਸ਼ੋਤਮ ਆਇਸੁ ਅਨੁਸਰੀਏ' ॥੩੧॥

Then the Guru turned and spoke to Baba Buddha:[^16]\
"Whatever this yogi tells you to do,\
go with him and do it, just as he says;\
follow the command of this best of men." (31)

ਹੁਕਮ ਪਾਇ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੋ ਖਲੇ। ਅਵਲੋਕਹਿਂ ਲਖਿ ਪਾਵਨ ਭਲੇ।\
ਹੁਤੇ ਜਿ ਲੋਕ ਫਿਰਤਿ ਚਹੁਂ ਪਾਸੇ। ਗਮਨਤਿ ਭਏ ਮਿਲੇ ਨਰ ਰਾਸੇ ॥੩੨॥

Receiving the Satguru's command, they stood ready beside him,\
and gazed on him, seeing how holy and how good he was.\
The people who were moving about on every side\
came there too, and men gathered in throngs. (32)

'ਨਿਕਸਯੋ ਜੋਗੀ' ਸੁਨਿ ਸੁਨਿ ਧਾਏ। ਕਰਯੋ ਬਿਲੋਕਨ ਮਨ ਬਿਸਮਾਏ।\
ਚਿਰੰਕਾਲ ਕੀ ਆਰਬਲਾ ਜਿਹ। ਅਚਰਜ ਇਸ ਜੁਗ ਮਹਿਂ ਦੇਖਹਿਂ ਤਿਹ ॥੩੩॥

Hearing it said again and again, "A yogi has come out," they came running,\
and they looked upon him, their minds filled with wonder:\
here was a man whose span of life ran back through the ages,\
and they were seeing such a wonder in this age of theirs. (33)

ਧਾਇ ਧਾਇ ਹਰਖਹਿਂ ਬਿਸਮਾਵਹਿਂ। ਅਪਰ ਅਪਰ ਸੋ ਕਰਹਿਂ ਬੁਝਾਵਹਿਂਂ।\
'ਪੂਰਬਲੇ ਜੁਗੁ ਕੋ ਤਨੁ ਇਹੁ ਹੈ। ਦੀਰਘਤਾ ਸਭਿ ਅੰਗਨਿ ਲਹਿ ਹੈ ॥੩੪॥

Running and running, they rejoiced and marvelled,\
and each one told another, and that one told the next:\
"This body belongs to an earlier age of the world;\
you can see its great length in every one of its limbs. (34)

ਪਰਮ ਬ੍ਰਿੱਧ ਮੁਖ ਬਰਨ ਦਿਪਾਵਤਿ। ਸੇਤ ਸਮੱਸ਼੍ਰੈ ਕੇਸ ਸੁਹਾਵਤਿ।'\
ਨਿਕਸ ਵਹਿਰ ਸਭਿ ਮੈਂ ਥਿਰ ਹੋਵਾ। ਦੇਖਯੋ ਜੋਗੀ ਸਭਿਨਿ ਖਰੋਵਾ ॥੩੫॥

He is very, very old, and yet the colour of his face is shining,\
and his white beard and his hair are a beautiful sight."[^17]\
He came out and stood steady in the midst of them all,\
and everyone there saw the yogi standing before them. (35)

ਕਹਿਂ 'ਇਹੁ ਥਾਨ ਪੁਨੀਤ ਮਹਾਂਨਾ। ਜੋ ਸੇਵੈਂ ਨਰ ਆਨ ਸੁਜਾਨਾ।\
ਸ਼ਰਧਾ ਧਰੈਂ ਬੰਦਨਾ ਠਾਨਿ। ਸੱਤਯਨਾਮ ਸਿਮਰਹਿਂ ਗੁਨ ਗਾਨ ॥੩੬॥

Then he said: "This place is supremely holy;\
the wise men who come here and serve it,\
holding faith in their hearts and bowing low in reverence,\
will remember Satnam and sing its praises. (36)

ਇਸ ਥਲ ਕੀਜਹਿ ਤਾਲ ਬਿਸਾਲਾ। ਮੱਜਤਿ ਤਯਾਗਹਿਂ ਪਾਪ ਕਰਾਲਾ।\
ਨਾਸਤਿ ਹ੍ਵੈਂ ਸਭਿ ਮਨ ਕੇ ਦੋਸ਼। ਪ੍ਰਾਪਤ ਹੋਵੈ ਤਿਹ ਸੰਤੋਸ਼' ॥੩੭॥

Let a great pool be made on this very spot;[^18]\
those who bathe in it will cast off their most terrible sins,\
all the faults of their minds will be destroyed,\
and they will find contentment there." (37)

ਨੰਮ੍ਰ ਥਾਨ ਪੁਨ ਕਹਿ ਖਨਵਾਯੋ। ਬੈਠਿ ਬੀਚ ਸਭਿ ਪੌਨ ਚੜਾਯੋ।\
ਦਸਮੇ ਦ੍ਵਾਰ ਜੋਰ ਕੇ ਪਾਵਾ। ਬ੍ਰਹਮ ਰੰਧਰ ਕੋ ਫੋਰਿ ਕਰਾਵਾ ॥੩੮॥

Then, humbly, he asked for a place to be dug out in the ground,\
and sitting down inside it, he drew all his breath upward;\
he forced it with all his strength into the Tenth Door,[^19]\
and made it burst open the opening at the crown of his head. (38)

ਤਬਿ ਸਭਿ ਲੋਕਨ ਫਲ ਬਸਾਏ। 'ਜੈ ਜੈ' ਮੁਖ ਤੇ ਬਾਕ ਸੁਨਾਏ।\
ਸਰਬ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਰ ਬੰਦਨਾ ਕਰੈਂ। ਹੇਰਿ ਹੇਰਿ ਅਚਰਜ ਕੋ ਧਰੈਂ ॥੩੯॥

Then all the people showered him with flowers,[^20]\
and the cry of "Jai! Jai!" rose from their lips;\
they bowed before him in every way they knew,\
and looked and looked again, lost in wonder. (39)

ਬਹੁਰੋ ਸਭਿਨਿ ਮ੍ਰਿੱਤਕਾ ਪਾਈ। ਜੋਗੀ ਕਰਯੋ ਅਲੋਪ ਤਿਥਾਈਂ।\
ਬਿਸਮਤਿ ਹੋਇ ਸਕਲ ਹਟਿ ਆਏ। ਮਿਲਹਿਂ ਪਰਸਪਰ ਕਹਤਿ ਸੁਨਾਏ ॥੪੦॥

After that they all heaped earth over him,\
and so the yogi was hidden from sight in that place.\
Astonished, they all turned and came back home,\
and as they met one another, they talked and told each other: (40)

'ਚਿਰੰਕਾਲ ਆਰਬਲਾ ਜਾਂਹੀ। ਮੁਖ ਪਰ ਦਿਪਤਿ ਜੋਤਿ ਬਹੁ ਤਾਂਹੀ।\
ਧੰਨ ਪੁਰਖ ਐਸੇ ਜਗ ਮਾਂਹੀ। ਮਨੁ ਬਿਕਾਰ ਜਿਨ ਪਰਸਯੋ ਨਾਂਹੀ' ॥੪੧॥

"His span of life ran back through ages beyond counting,\
and yet such a great light was shining on his face.\
Blessed indeed are such men as this in the world,\
whose minds have never once been touched by any vice." (41)

---
*Thus ends the thirty-sixth ansu of the second Ras of Sri Gur Pratap Suraj Granth, named "The Episode of the Yogi".*

### Notes

[^1]: Brahm: the Absolute of the Vedanta. The yogi's questions and the Guru's answers in this chapter are put in the terms of the Vedanta schools.
[^2]: prapañch: the world spread out before the senses.
[^3]: pariṇāma vāda: the teaching that Brahm really changes into the world, as milk turns into curd (v. 8).
[^4]: Maya: the power through which the world appears; see vv. 10 to 12.
[^5]: vivarta vāda: the teaching that the world is an appearance upon Brahm, as a snake upon a rope, while Brahm itself does not change. Bhai Vir Singh: Brahm alone is true; the world shows from it through Maya, and to one with knowledge nothing but Brahm shows.
[^6]: The poet's words for ignorance are agyān (v. 10) and avidyā (vv. 12, 18): not knowing one's own true nature.
[^7]: anirvachanīya (ਅਨਰ ਬਚਨੀ): beyond description, the Vedanta's word for Maya, which is neither real nor unreal.
[^8]: klesh: the afflictions that bind a soul to birth and death; the yoga schools count five, with ignorance as their root (v. 18).
[^9]: The line echoes the Katha Upanishad, aṇor aṇīyān mahato mahīyān (subtler than the subtle, greater than the great); v. 16, where the sun shines by Brahm's light, echoes the same Upanishad.
[^10]: kūṭastha: the changeless one, ever the same (Bhai Vir Singh).
[^11]: jīvan mukt: one liberated while still in the body.
[^12]: The ocean of fire is the world (Bhai Vir Singh): they live in it and yet have lost all duality.
[^13]: Bhai Vir Singh: the yogi was learned and a master of the breath, but had not received the gift of life (jīa dān), which comes only by grace (prasād); his own guru had told him he would receive it from Sri Guru Arjan Dev, the avatar of the Kaliyuga, and for this he had sat with his breath drawn up. It was this glance of grace, and not the teaching alone, that freed him; he points to the same grace in the story of Chudala in the Yoga Vasishtha.
[^14]: ਤਰਤੱਬ is obscure: read here as tattva, the essence or reality; it may also mean what had to be done.
[^15]: Satnam: the True Name.
[^16]: Baba Buddha (ਬੁੱਢੇ): the elder Sikh who had served every Guru since Guru Nanak.
[^17]: Bhai Vir Singh takes this verse as proof that men of earlier ages kept their hair and beards. He adds that a man lying with his breath drawn up into the Tenth Door, or buried in a cell, is no great wonder: a yogi who came to the court of Maharaja Ranjit Singh was shut in a large jar and buried for 40 days under guard, with Europeans and Indians looking on, and was brought back to his senses with massage.
[^18]: This pool is Santokhsar (Bhai Vir Singh); santokh is the contentment of the line.
[^19]: dasam duār, the Tenth Door: Bhai Vir Singh calls it the brahma randhra, a hidden passage at the crown of the head, which yogis burst open to send out the soul.
[^20]: The source prints ਫਲ (fruit), which Bhai Vir Singh does not gloss; flowers taken as the sense of the shower. ਬਸਾਏ: rained down (Bhai Vir Singh).

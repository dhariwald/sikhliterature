# Panth Prakash, Episode 86

## How Tara Singh Attained Martyrdom

>**Questions**
>
>**Define (1 line each):** Ghumanda (ਘਵੰਡੋ), Nihang (ਨਿਹੰਗ), sauṅchī (ਸੌਂਚੀ, a Punjabi game of strength), Taki (ਤਕੀ), Purbias (ਪੂਰਬੀਏ), "gave his head but not his resolve" (ਸੀਸ ਦਯੋ ਪਰ ਸਿਰਰ ਨ ਦੱਯਾ)
>
>**Summarize (2–3 sentences each):**
>
>1. How Ghumanda and the Pathan tried to save Tara Singh.
>2. The night battle, up to the fight for the elephant.
>3. How the battle ended at daybreak.

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਮੋਮਨ ਚੜ੍ਹਯੋ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਪੈ ਸੰਗ ਲੈ ਕਈ ਸਰਦਾਰ ॥\
ਮੁਹਰੈ ਰਾਤਿ ਸੁ ਉਨ ਲਈ ਬੇਖਬਰ ਹੁਇ ਸਿੰਘ ਮਾਰ ॥੧॥

Moman Khan set out on an expedition to crush the Singhs, taking a host of chiefs with him;\
using the darkness of the night ahead, they meant to fall on the Singhs unawares and kill them. (1)

ਤੋ ਊਸੁ ਜਾ ਉਪਲ ਜਟ ਹੁਤੋ ਮਿਲਿਯੋ ਘਵੰਡੋ ਧਾਇ ॥\
ਤਾਰੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਨਿਹੰਗ ਕੋ ਮੈਂ ਚਲ ਦਿਓਂ ਦਿਖਾਇ ॥੨॥

Then (a local man) Ghumanda,[^1] an Uppal Jatt by caste, went out and met the advancing troops,\
saying, "I will go and show you Tara Singh the Nihang."[^2] (2)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਸੋ ਮੋਮਨ ਨੈ ਮੁਹਰੇ ਲਾਯੋ ॥ ਉਨ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਤੇ ਕੁਛਕ ਖੁੰਝਾਯੋ ॥\
ਉਨ ਦਏ ਤੁਰਕ ਪਰ ਡਲੈ ਚਾਲ ॥ ਕਿਤ ਹੁਤੋ ਪੁਰਾਤਨ ਬੈਰ ਉਨ ਨਾਲ ॥੩॥

So Moman Khan put Ghumanda at the head of his troops,\
and he led them on a winding route somewhat away from the Singhs.\
He turned the Turks[^3] toward the village of Dalla,[^4]\
perhaps because he nursed some old grudge against its people. (3)

ਅਕੈ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੋ ਉਨ ਭਲਾ ਤਕਾਯੋ ॥ ਅਕੈ ਨਠ ਲੁਕਨ ਕੋ ਬਿਲਮ ਲਗਾਯੋ ॥\
ਉਨ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਵਲ ਦਈ ਖਬਰ ਘਲਾਇ ॥ ਤੂੰ ਤਾਰਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਅਬ ਨਠ ਜਾਇ ॥੪॥

Or else he meant to do the Singhs a good turn,\
since the delay in the troops' arrival would give the Singhs time to escape.\
(At the same time) he sent a message to the Singhs\
that Tara Singh should leave his place with his companions. (4)

ਤੁਰਕ ਫੌਜ ਮੁੜ ਜਾਊਗੁ ਲਹੌਰ ॥ ਆਇ ਬਹੀਂ ਤੂੰ ਫਿਰ ਇਤ ਠੌਰ ॥\
ਇਕ ਸਾਇਤ ਬਚੈ ਤੌ ਬਰਸ ਬਚਾਇ ॥ ਕਾਹੇ ਅਕਾਰਥ ਜਾਨ ਗਵਾਇ ॥੫॥

When the Turkish troops went back to Lahore empty-handed,\
Tara Singh could return and take his place again.\
If he escaped for a moment, he would be saved for years;\
why should he throw away his precious life for nothing? (5)

ਤੌ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਉਸ ਕੀਆ ਸਮੋਧ ॥ ਸ਼ਾਬਸ਼ ਭਾਈ ਤੇਰੀ ਬੋਧ ॥\
ਇਤਨਕ ਤੁਮ ਤੈ ਆਛੀ ਸਰੀ ॥ ਭਈਚਾਰੈ ਕੀ ਸਾਂਝ ਚਿਤ ਕਰੀ ॥੬॥

Hearing this message, Tara Singh Ji spoke to the messenger:\
he praised the good sense of his intentions,\
and said that he had indeed done a noble deed in warning him,\
for the sake of the bond of brotherhood.[^5] (6)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਜੋ ਤੁਮ ਕਹੀ ਸੁ ਭਲਿ ਕਹੀ ਹੈ ਯੌਂਹੀ ਸਚ ਬਾਤ ॥\
ਹਮਹ ਸ਼ਹੀਦੀ ਚਿਤ ਧਰੀ ਹਮ ਨਠ ਨਹਿਂ ਕਿਤ ਜਾਤ ॥੭॥

No doubt what he had said was meant for Tara Singh's good, and it was true as well;\
but since Tara Singh had set his heart on martyrdom, he would not run away anywhere from there. (7)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਜੌ ਹਮ ਅਬ ਨਠ ਜਾਨ ਬਚਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਫਿਰ ਚਾਹੈ ਹਮ ਈਹਾਂ ਹੀ ਆਵੈਂ ॥\
ਲਾਵੈ ਬੋਲੀ ਗਿਰਦ ਭਾਈਚਾਰੋ ॥ ਇਮ ਹਮ ਕਰਹਿਂ ਨ ਜਾਨ ਪਿਆਰੋ ॥੮॥

He could very well run away to save his life,\
and could easily come back to his place as well;\
but the brotherhood all around would taunt him as a coward,\
so he would not hold his life so dear. (8)

ਉਤ ਡਲਵਾਲਨ ਖਬਰਹਿ ਭਈ ॥ ਮਿਲੇ ਦੌੜ ਸੋ ਆਗੈ ਜਈ ॥\
ਹਮ ਰੱਯਤ ਹੈਂ ਖੁਦ ਪਤਿਸ਼ਾਹੀ ॥ ਹਮ ਮੈਂ ਰਾਠ ਸਿੰਘ ਚੋਰ ਕੋਊ ਨਾਹੀਂ ॥੯॥

Meanwhile, hearing that the troops had come,\
the people of Dalla ran out to meet them,\
assuring them that they were the loyal subjects of the Patshah himself,\
and that there was no fighting Singh[^6] and no thief among them. (9)

ਤੌ ਲੌ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਨਗਾਰਾ ਕਰਾਯਾ ॥ ਸੋਊ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਨੇ ਵਹਿ ਸੁਨ ਪਾਯਾ ॥\
ਤੌ ਇਮ ਲੀਨੋ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਜਾਨ ॥ ਆਹਿ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੋ ਅਗੈ ਮਕਾਨ ॥੧੦॥

By then the Singhs had their war drum[^7] beaten as a challenge,\
and the Turkish troops heard it from far off.\
With this, the Turks came to know\
that the Singhs' place lay further ahead. (10)

ਭਜਾਇ ਘੋੜੇ ਕੋਊ ਪੁਜਯੋ ਪਠਾਨ ॥ ਬੈਠੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਕਿਮ ਗਵਾਵਹੁ ਜਾਨ ॥\
ਦੌੜ ਲੁਕੋ ਅਬ ਝਾੜਨ ਮਾਂਹਿ ॥ ਕਰੀ ਖਬਰ ਮੈਂ ਆਨ ਤੁਸਾਂਹਿ ॥੧੧॥

A (friendly) Pathan[^8] too came galloping on horseback,\
warning the Singhs not to sit there and lose their lives in vain.\
They should run now and take refuge in the bush;\
he had come to warn them of the approaching troops. (11)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਤਾਰੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਉਸ ਕਹਯੋ ਸ਼ਾਬਸ਼ ਭਾਈ ਜੀ ਤੋਹਿ ॥\
ਅਬ ਹਮ ਨਠਕੈ ਕਿਤ ਲੁਕੈਂ ਹਮ ਸ਼ਹੀਦ ਈਹਾਂ ਹੀ ਹੋਹਿਂ ॥੧੨॥

Tara Singh Ji, praising his service, told the brotherly Pathan that he was grateful to him indeed;\
but where could he run and hide now? He would become a martyr at his own place. (12)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਅਗੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਟਹਲ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕਮਾਵਤ ॥ ਭਰ ਦੇਗੈ ਜਲ ਸ਼ਨਾਨ ਕਰਾਵਤ ॥\
ਔਰ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਸੋਂ ਕਹੀ ॥ ਤੁਮ ਬਹੁ ਟਹਲ ਅਗੈ ਕਰ ਲਈ ॥੧੩॥

At that time Tara Singh was serving the other Singhs,\
filling a big cauldron[^9] with water to bathe them.\
Then the other Singhs begged Tara Singh Ji,\
saying that he had already done plenty of service for them. (13)

ਅਬ ਹਮ ਟਹਲ ਚਹਿਂ ਕਰੀ ਤੁਮਾਰੀ ॥ ਹੁਇ ਪਵਿਤ੍ਰ ਅਬ ਦੇਹਿ ਹਮਾਰੀ ॥\
ਤੌ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਜੀ ਉਨ ਕਹੀ ਸੁਨਾਇ ॥ ਦੇਹਿ ਪਵਿਤ੍ਰ ਹੋਇ ਸ਼ਸਤ੍ਰ ਲਗਾਇ ॥੧੪॥

Now it was their turn to do some service for him,\
so that their own bodies might be made pure by serving him.\
At this, Tara Singh Ji told his companions\
that the body is made pure only when weapons strike it. (14)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਜੈਸ ਦੁਵਾਰਕਹਿ ਦਾਗ ਲਗ ਦੇਹਿ ਪਵਿੱਤਰ ਹੋਇ ॥\
ਤੈਸੇ ਸ਼ਸਤਰ ਕੇ ਲਗੇ ਪਾਪ ਦੇਹੋਂ ਜਾਇ ਧੋਇ ॥੧੫॥

As the devout have their bodies made pure by being branded at Dwarka,[^10]\
so, when a warrior is struck by a weapon in war, the sins are washed from his body. (15)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤੌ ਹੁਤਿ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਜਲ ਲੈਨ ਗਏ ॥ ਆਵਤ ਤੁਰਕ ਉਨ੍ਹੈਂ ਲਖ ਲਏ ॥\
ਮੁੜ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਸਭ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਸੁਨਾਈ ॥ ਜਿਨ ਨੱਠਨੋ ਸੋ ਨੱਠ ਜਾਹੁ ਭਾਈ ॥੧੬॥

As Tara Singh Ji went out to fetch water,[^11]\
he caught sight of the Turks advancing toward them.\
Coming back, he announced to all the Singhs once again,\
"Whoever wants to run, brothers, let him run now. (16)

ਅਕੈ ਸ਼ਸਤ੍ਰ ਫੜ ਬਾਹਰ ਹੋਵਹੁ ॥ ਛਾਡ ਓਟ ਰਣ ਭੂਮ ਖਲੋਵਹੁ ॥\
ਸੁਨਿ ਸਿੰਘ ਤੁਰੇ ਮੈਦਾਨੇ ਦਾਇ ॥ ਛੋਡ ਓਟ ਰਣ ਭੂਮ ਖੜੇ ਜਾਇ ॥੧੭॥

"Otherwise, come out with your weapons,\
leave your shelter and stand on the field of battle."\
Hearing this, the Singhs went out into the open;\
leaving their shelter, they took their stand on the battlefield. (17)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਉਤ ਤੇ ਤੁਰਕ ਤੌ ਆਇ ਪਏ ਬਜਾਏ ਧੌਂਸ ਨਗਾਰ ॥\
ਕਰਹਿਂ ਦੁਤਰਫੋਂ ਮਰ ਮਾਰ ਸੌਂਚੀ ਜੈਸ ਖਿਡਾਰ ॥੧੮॥

From the other side the Turks fell upon them, beating their great war drums;\
on both sides they struck and killed, like players in the game of sauṅchī.[^12] (18)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮ ਬੁਲਾਕਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਅਗਯੋਂ ਧਾਯੋ ॥ ਫੜ ਤੇਗੋ ਤਿਨ ਬਹੁ ਤਨ ਲਾਯੋ ॥\
ਅਗਯੋਂ ਲਗਯੋ ਤਿਸ ਛਾਤੀ ਤੀਰ ॥ ਭਯੋ ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮਹਿ ਸੋਈ ਬਹੀਰ ॥੧੯॥

Bulaka Singh was the first to charge the attackers;\
seizing his tegh,[^13] he wounded many of them.\
Then an enemy arrow struck him in the chest,\
and he was the first to depart this world. (19)

ਖੜ ਜਾਇ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਢੇਰੀ ਬੰਨ੍ਹ ਲਈ ॥ ਜਿਮ ਕਰ ਖੇਡ ਸੌਂਚ ਕੀ ਠਈ ॥\
ਉਤ ਤੇ ਤੁਰਕ ਹਲੋ ਕਰ ਆਵੈਂ ॥ ਇਤ ਤੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਉਨ ਮੋੜ ਹਟਾਵੈਂ ॥੨੦॥

Then the Singhs stood together in a ring with their backs to one another,[^14]\
as if they were taking part in a game of sauṅchī.\
As the Turks came charging at the ringed Singhs,\
the Singhs kept on driving them back. (20)

ਗਰਦ ਗੁਬਾਰ ਦੁਇ ਵਲ ਤੈ ਭਈ ॥ ਹੁਤੀ ਰਾਤ ਜਨ ਅਨੇਰੀ ਅਈ ॥\
ਉਤ ਕਰ ਹੱਲੇ ਤੁਰਕ ਘੋੜੈ ਚਲਾਵਨ ॥ ਮਾਰ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਚਹੇ ਫੇਟਨ ਗਿਰਾਵਨ ॥੨੧॥

So much dust was raised from both sides\
that it was as if a dust storm had broken in the night.\
The Turks, charging on their horses,\
meant to ride the Singhs down and crush them under the horses' hooves. (21)

ਅਗਯੋਂ ਉਨ ਸਿੰਘ ਮੁਖ ਕਟ ਡਾਰੇ ॥ ਗਿਰੇ ਤਹੀਂ ਬਹੁ ਥੱਕ ਕੈ ਹਾਰੇ ॥\
ਕਿਛਕੁ ਗਿਰੇ ਵਹਿ ਆਪਸ ਮਾਂਹਿ ॥ ਇਮ ਕਰ ਰੁਕ ਗਯੋ ਉਨ ਕੋ ਰਾਹੁ ॥੨੨॥

But the Singhs slashed the horses across the face,[^15]\
and many riders fell there, worn out and beaten.\
Some of the horsemen crashed into one another and fell,\
and in this way their own path was blocked. (22)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਤਿਹ ਦੁਇ ਵਲ ਤੇ ਅੜ ਭਿੜੈ ਗਿਰ ਗਿਰ ਰੁਕ ਗਈ ਠੌਰ\
ਕਰ ਕਰ ਹੱਲੇ ਜੇ ਅਵੈਂ ਪਰੈਂ ਉਨੈਂ ਪਰ ਧੌੜ ॥੨੩॥

As the fighters from both sides clashed, the ground was blocked with the falling bodies;\
as more and more men came charging, they too kept falling on the heaped bodies. (23)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਕਿਛ ਉਨ ਕੈ ਉਨ ਸ਼ਸਤ੍ਰ ਲਾਗੈਂ ॥ ਕਿਛ ਸਿੰਘ ਭੀ ਮਾਰੈਂ ਹੁਇ ਆਗੈ ॥\
ਦੋਊ ਤਰਫ਼ ਤੇ ਮਚ ਗਯੋ ਘਾਣ ॥ ਥੱਕ ਗਏ ਦੁਵੱਲੋਂ ਲਾ ਲਾ ਤਾਣ ॥੨੪॥

Some of the Turks were wounded by their own side's weapons,\
and the Singhs, pressing forward, struck down others.\
Such slaughter broke out on both sides\
that the men of both sides were worn out with the effort. (24)

ਧੀਰੀ ਧੀਰੀ ਵਲ ਦੋਊ ਤੈ ਹੋਈ ॥ ਛੋਟੀ ਹਥਯਾਰੀਂ ਆਵੈ ਨ ਕੋਈ ॥\
ਤੀਰ ਬੰਦੂਕ ਖੜ ਦੂਰੋਂ ਚਲਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਇਸ ਬਿਧ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਮਾਰ ਗਿਰਾਵੈਂ ॥੨੫॥

Then the attacks from both sides slowed down,\
and no one came forward any more with short weapons.\
The Turks fired muskets[^16] and shot arrows from a distance,\
and in this way began killing the Singhs from afar. (25)

ਹੁਤ ਬੰਦੂਕ ਤਮਾਚੋ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਪਹਿ ਨਾਂਹਿ ॥ ਪਰ ਉਨ ਕੀ ਗੋਲੀ ਲੰਘ ਉਨ ਪਰ ਪਾਂਹਿ ॥\
ਤੌ ਮੋਮਨ ਖਾਂ ਨੇੜੇ ਆਯਾ ॥ ਤੌ ਹੱਲਾ ਤਿਮ ਫੇਰ ਕਰਾਯਾ ॥੨੬॥

The Singhs had no muskets or pistols,[^17]\
but the Turks' bullets passed over them and struck their own men on the other side.\
Then Moman Khan himself came closer\
and had another charge made at the Singhs. (26)

ਤਊ ਮੁਗਲ ਤਕੀ ਘੋੜਾ ਚਲਾਯਾ ॥ ਕਹਾਂ ਤਾਰਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਪੁਛਤਾ ਆਯਾ ॥\
ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਕਹਯੋ ਚਲ ਆਉ ਅਗਾਰੀ ॥ ਬਿਸ਼ਕ ਚਲਾਉ ਤੂੰ ਪਹਿਲੋਂ ਵਾਰੀ ॥੨੭॥

At that the Mughal Taki[^18] rode forward on his horse,\
calling out loudly, "Where is Tara Singh?"\
Tara Singh Ji told him to come on a little further,\
and gave him the first blow, by all means. (27)

ਛੇੜ ਘੋੜਾ ਉਨ ਤੀਰ ਚਲਾਯਾ ॥ ਸੀਸ ਮੁੰਡਾਸੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਕੇ ਲਾਯਾ ॥\
ਝਟ ਕਰ ਘੋੜੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਫੜ ਲਈ ਬਾਗ ॥ ਤਿਸ ਹੁਤੋ ਨਗਾਤ ਸੰਜੋਇ ਬਿਨ ਨਾਂਗ ॥੨੮॥

Spurring his horse, Taki shot an arrow,\
and it struck the turban[^19] wrapped round Tara Singh Ji's head.\
In a flash Tara Singh caught hold of the horse's reins;\
but no part of Taki's body was bare of armour. (28)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਬਿਨ ਸੰਜੋਇ ਇਸ ਮੁਗ਼ਲਕਾ ਪਿੰਡਾ ਨਜ਼ਰ ਨ ਆਇ ॥\
ਇਕ ਮੁਖ ਨੰਗਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਲਖ ਉਤ ਪਿਪਲਾ ਦਯੋ ਧਸਾਇ ॥੨੯॥

Since the Mughal was covered in armour, no part of his body could be seen;\
but Tara Singh, spotting his bare face, drove the tip of his sword[^20] into it. (29)

ਮੁੜਯੋ ਤਕੀ ਮੁਖ ਜ਼ਖ਼ਮ ਖਾ ਮੋਮਨ ਲਯੋ ਪਛਾਨ ॥\
ਪੂਛੀ ਖਬਰ ਬੁਲਾਇ ਉਸ ਕਹਾਂ ਖਾਏ ਮੁਖ ਪਾਨ ॥੩੦॥

As Taki turned back with a wound in his face, Moman Khan recognised him;\
calling him over, he asked him where he had been chewing pān.[^21] (30)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤੌ ਤਕੀ ਨੈ ਮੋਮਨ ਸੁਨਾਈ ॥ ਅਗੈ ਤਾਰਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਬੈਠਾ ਬੀੜੇ ਬਨਾਈ ॥\
ਹੁਇ ਹਿੰਮਤ ਤੌ ਅਗੈ ਹੁਇ ਖਾਵੋ ॥ ਦੂਰ ਖੜੇ ਕਯਾ ਬਾਤ ਬਨਾਵੋ ॥੩੧॥

At this, Taki answered Moman Khan with a taunt,\
that Tara Singh was sitting up ahead, making up packets of pān;[^22]\
Moman Khan could go forward and have a taste of them too, if he dared,\
instead of making such remarks from a distance. (31)

ਇਕ ਹੁਤੋ ਚੇਲੋ ਉਸ ਪਹਿ ਖਲੋ ॥ ਸੁਨ ਵਹਿ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਪਰ ਧਾਇ ਚਲੋ ॥\
ਕਹਿ ਮਹਾਵਤ ਦਯੋ ਹਾਥੀ ਪਿਲਾਇ ॥ ਦੁਇ ਦੁਇ ਤੀਰ ਫੜ ਦਏ ਚਲਾਇ ॥੩੨॥

Hearing this, a disciple[^23] of Moman Khan who stood beside him\
rode out and charged at the Singhs.\
Telling the elephant driver to drive his elephant at them,\
this warrior shot his arrows two at a time. (32)

ਕਹਿ ਮਹਾਵਤ ਉਨ ਹਾਥੀ ਤੁਰਵਾਯੋ ॥ ਫੱਟੇ ਥਕੇ ਉਨ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਤਕਾਯੋ ॥\
ਉਨ ਜਾਨਯੋਂ ਮੈਂ ਊਚੇ ਥਾਇ ॥ ਦੇਉਂਗੁ ਹਾਥੀਓਂ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਮਿਧਾਇ ॥੩੩॥

He told the elephant driver to move the elephant further on,\
reckoning that the Singhs were wounded and exhausted.\
He thought that, perched up high on the elephant's back,\
he would have the Singhs crushed under the elephant's feet. (33)

ਦੇਖ ਥੋੜੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਲਾਲੀ ਆਈ ॥ ਗਿਰਦੇ ਦੇਖਯੋ ਉਨ ਹਾਥੀ ਘੁਮਾਈ ॥\
ਵਹੁ ਮਾਰੈ ਦੁਇ ਦੁਇ ਢੁਕ ਤੀਰ ॥ ਵਹਿ ਤੋ ਕਹਾਵਤ ਬਡ ਸੂਰਬੀਰ ॥੩੪॥

Seeing how few the Singhs were, his face flushed red with eagerness,\
and he looked them over, turning his elephant round about them.\
Coming close, he shot his arrows at the Singhs two at a time;\
he was known as a great warrior (among the Turkish troops). (34)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਤੌ ਭੀਮਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਕਰ ਖ਼ੁਸ਼ੀ ਪਰਯੋ ਸ਼ੇਰ ਜਿਮ ਧਾਇ ॥\
ਫੜ ਉਨ ਹਾਥੀ ਕੰਨ ਦਬਯੋ ਤੁਰਤਹਿ ਲਯੋ ਬਹਾਇ ॥੩੫॥

Then Bhima Singh (of Peshawar), in a joyful mood of valour, sprang upon his enemy like a lion;[^24]\
catching the elephant by the ear and pressing it hard, he made the elephant sit down in an instant. (35)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤੌ ਸਿੰਘ ਕੀਯੋ ਮਹਾਵਤ ਪਰ ਵਾਰ ॥ ਉਨ ਡਾਹਯੋ ਅੰਕੁਸ ਲਗ ਟੁਟੀ ਤਲਵਾਰ ॥\
ਅਧੀ ਰਹੀ ਸੰਗ ਮਾਰਯੋ ਮਹਾਵਤ ॥ ਸੋਊ ਗਯੋ ਗਸ਼ੀ ਅੰਕਸ ਲਾਵਤ ॥੩੬॥

Then the Singh struck at the elephant driver,\
but the driver thrust out his goad,[^25] and the sword broke against it.\
With the half that was left, he killed the elephant driver,\
but he himself fainted as the goad struck him. (36)

ਦੇਖ ਤਾਰੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਮਦਤ ਕਰਾਈ ॥ ਲਖਮੀਰ ਸਿੰਘ ਸੋ ਕਹਯੋ ਸੁਨਾਈ ॥\
ਜਦ ਅਸੀਂ ਤੁਸੀਂ ਹੁਤ ਮੰਝੀ ਚਰਾਵਤ ॥ ਤੂੰ ਹੁਤੋ ਮੰਝੀ ਦੁਇ ਦੁਇ ਟੱਪ ਜਾਵਤ ॥੩੭॥

Seeing Bhima Singh fall, Tara Singh rushed to his aid,\
and spoke to Lakhmir Singh in these words:\
when the two of them used to graze buffaloes,\
Lakhmir Singh used to leap over two buffaloes at a time. (37)

ਅਬ ਕੱਟਾ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਕਾ ਕਿਤਨਕ ਬਲਾਇ ॥ ਮਾਰ ਤੁਰਕ ਤੂੰ ਉਪਰ ਜਾਇ ॥\
ਅਗਯੋਂ ਲਖਮੀਰ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਸੋਂ ਕਹੀ ॥ ਮੇਰੇ ਭੀ ਮਨ ਥੀ ਗਲ ਯਹੀ ॥੩੮॥

Now this buffalo calf[^26] of the Turks was not so big;\
he should leap up and kill the Turk on top of it.\
Answering Tara Singh Ji, Lakhmir Singh said\
that he had been thinking along the same lines. (38)

ਮੋ ਪਰ ਤਨਕ ਆਗਯਾ ਜੇ ਹੋਇ ॥ ਮੈਂ ਮਾਰ ਦਿਖਾਊਂ ਤੁਰਕੈ ਸੋਇ ॥\
ਇਤਨਕ ਕਹਿ ਸਿੰਘ ਕੀਨੀ ਧਾਈ ॥ ਜਿਮ ਪਰੈ ਕੁੰਜਨ ਪਰ ਬਹਰੀ ਜਾਈ ॥੩੯॥

If Tara Singh would give him leave for it,\
he would surely show him that Turk killed.\
With these words Lakhmir Singh sprang at his enemy,\
as a falcon swoops on a flock of cranes.[^27] (39)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਫੜ ਨੇਜਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਕੁਦ ਪਰਯੋ ਸੌਂਹੈ ਚੇਲੇ ਕੋ ਲਲਕਾਰ ॥\
ਉਤ ਵਲ ਤੇ ਉਨ ਭੀ ਕਰੀ ਮਾਰੈ ਮਾਰ ਪੁਕਾਰ ॥੪੦॥

Seizing a spear,[^28] the Singh leapt forward, roaring a challenge to Moman Khan's disciple face to face;\
from the other side the Turk too attacked, shouting, "Kill him, kill him." (40)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤੌ ਲਖਮੀਰ ਸਿੰਘ ਜਾਇ ਸੰਭਾਰਾ ॥ ਜਾਇ ਚੇਲੇ ਕੋ ਮੋਢੇ ਮਾਰਾ ॥\
ਗਯੋ ਨੇਜਾ ਉਸ ਮੋਢੈ ਚੀਰ ॥ ਫੜ ਹੁਇ ਤਲਵਾਰ ਨ ਚਲੈ ਤੀਰ ॥੪੧॥

Then Lakhmir Singh closed in and took his aim,\
and struck Moman Khan's disciple in the shoulder.\
The spear cut right through his shoulder,\
so that he could neither wield his sword nor shoot his arrows. (41)

ਇਕ ਦੁਇ ਸਿੰਘ ਮਦਤ ਔਰ ਆਏ ॥ ਉਨ ਹਾਥੀ ਗਿਰਦਯੋਂ ਔਰ ਹਟਾਇ ॥\
ਤੌਂ ਲਖਮੀਰ ਸਿੰਘ ਕੋ ਦਾਉ ਲਗ ਗਯੋ ॥ ਫੜ ਰੱਸਾ ਹਾਥੀ ਪਰ ਗਯੋ ॥੪੨॥

As one or two more Singhs came to help Lakhmir Singh,\
they drove away the other men from around the elephant.\
Lakhmir Singh, seizing his chance,\
climbed onto the elephant by catching hold of a rope. (42)

ਮਾਰ ਤਲਵਾਰ ਚੇਲਾ ਡਾਰ ਦਯੋ ॥ ਹਾਥੀ ਤੋਰ ਵਲ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਦਯੋ ॥\
ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਕਹਯੋ ਹਾਥੀ ਕਿਹ ਕਾਮਨ ॥ ਹਮ ਅਨੇਕ ਬਖ਼ਸੈਂਗੇ ਗੁਲਾਮਨ ॥੪੩॥

Striking with his sword, he threw Moman Khan's disciple down,\
and drove the elephant away toward the Singhs.\
Tara Singh Ji remarked that one elephant was of no use to them;\
the Singhs would yet bestow many elephants on their servants.[^29] (43)

ਹਾਥੀ ਫਿਰ ਜਬ ਖਾਲੀ ਗਯੋ ॥ ਦਲ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਕੋ ਭਾਜ ਖਾਇ ਗਯੋ ॥\
ਤੌ ਮੋਮਨ ਖਾਂ ਪੂਰਬੀਏ ਬੁਲਾਏ ॥ ਹੁਤੇ ਹਜਾਰੀ ਖਿਤਾਬ ਜਿਨ ਪਾਏ ॥੪੪॥

When the elephant went back without its rider,\
the army of the Turks broke and fled.\
At this, Moman Khan summoned the (sturdy) Purbia soldiers,[^30]\
who had won the title of Hazari. (44)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਮਨਸਾ ਰਾਮ ਔ ਦਯਾ ਰਾਮ ਜਿਨ ਕੋ ਥੋ ਯਹਿ ਨਾਮ ॥\
ਭੇਜ ਨਾਲ ਬਡ ਫੌਜ ਹੁਤ ਕੁੰਦਲਪੁਰ ਘਰ ਧਾਮ ॥੪੫॥

Mansa Ram and Daya Ram were the names of these two warriors;\
they had been sent with a large force, and their home was at Kundalpur.[^31] (45)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਵੈ ਕਰ ਹੱਲਾ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਪਰ ਪਏ ॥ ਉਤੇ ਤੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਭੀ ਸਨਮੁਖ ਅਏ ॥\
ਮਨਸਾ ਰਾਮ ਔ ਦਇਆ ਰਾਮ ॥ ਮਾਰ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਸੁ ਉਸ ਹੀ ਥਾਮ ॥੪੬॥

As they charged and fell upon the Singhs,\
the Singhs too came face to face with them from the other side.\
Mansa Ram and Daya Ram (the renowned Purbia warriors)\
were slain by the Singhs on that very spot. (46)

ਤਿਹ ਫੌਜ ਗਈ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਪਰਾਈ ॥ ਰਹਯੋ ਮਦਾਨ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੈ ਪਾਈ ॥\
ਤੌ ਦਿਨ ਚੜ੍ਹਯ ਭਯੋ ਉਜਿਯਾਲਾ ॥ ਮੁੜ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਕੀਯੋ ਖੇਤ ਸੰਭਾਲਾ ॥੪੭॥

As the army of the Turks took to its heels,\
the field of battle was left in the hands of the Singhs.\
But as the day broke and the light grew,\
the Turks came back and took hold of the field once more. (47)

ਕੋਊ ਕਿਤੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਦਿਸੈ ਖੜਾ ॥ ਔਰ ਮਾਰਯੋ ਔ ਦੇਖਯੋ ਪੜਾ ॥\
ਵੈ ਦੂਰੋਂ ਹੀ ਬੰਦੂਕ ਚਲਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਉਠਿ ਨੇੜ ਢੁਕੈ ਤੇ ਸ਼ੀਹਆਣਾ ਆਵੈਂ ॥੪੮॥

While some Singhs could be seen standing here and there,\
others were lying dead or struck down with wounds.\
The Turks fired their muskets only from a distance,\
for they were too afraid[^32] to come near the Singhs. (48)

ਬਾਕੀ ਮਾਰ ਸਿੰਘ ਇਮ ਦਏ ॥ ਫਿਰ ਢੁਕ ਸਭਨ ਕੇ ਸਿਰ ਕਟ ਲਏ ॥\
ਇਮ ਤਾਰੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਸ਼ਹੀਦੀ ਪਾਈ ॥ ਸਿੰਘ ਨਿਭੇ ਉਸ ਸੰਗ ਸਭ ਬਾਈ ॥੪੯॥

In this way they killed all the Singhs who were left,\
and then came up and cut off all their heads.\
In this way Tara Singh attained martyrdom,\
along with all his twenty-two companions, who stood by him to the end.[^33] (49)

ਇਮ ਤਾਰੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੀ ਸਾਕੋ ਕੀਆ ॥ ਮਸ਼ਾਹੂਰ ਸਭ ਜਗ ਮੈਂ ਥੀਆ ॥\
ਗੁਰ ਕੌ ਵਾਕ ਜਿਸ ਐਸ ਕਮੱਯਾ ॥ ਸੀਸ ਦਯੋ ਪਰ ਸਿਰਰ ਨ ਦੱਯਾ ॥੫੦॥

Thus Tara Singh Ji, by his legendary deed,\
became famous (as a martyr) throughout the world.\
Following the Guru's word in letter and spirit,\
he gave his head but did not give up his resolve.[^34] (50)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਧੰਨ ਧੰਨ ਉਨ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੈ ਜਿਨਿ ਕਰ ਸਾਕਾ ਤਜੇ ਪਰਾਨ ॥\
ਰਹੇ ਨਾਮ ਥਿਰ ਕਰਮ ਕਾ ਹੈ ਜਗ ਆਵਨ ਜਾਨ ॥੫੧॥

Blessed, blessed are those Singhs (of the Khalsa Panth), who did this legendary deed and gave up their lives;\
the name won by deeds endures for ever, in a world of coming and going. (51)

### Notes

[^1]: The source prints ਘਵੰਡੋ; Kulwant Singh and BSS read Ghumanda. The words ਊਸੁ ਜਾ before his name are obscure · Uppal: a Jatt clan.
[^2]: Nihang: a Singh of the Khalsa's warrior order, dressed in blue. BSS: in earlier times every Sikh was a Nihang, and the talk of sardars, Nihangs and Khalsa as different kinds was started by those who wanted to split the Panth; he quotes a line describing the Panth of Nihangs and bhujaṅgīs with khandas at their necks and clubs in their hands.
[^3]: Turks: the poet's word for the Muslim rulers and their armies.
[^4]: Dalla: a village near Tara Singh's place (BSS).
[^5]: BSS: they were all of one clan, and Tara Singh praises the messenger for keeping the bond of kin in his heart.
[^6]: ਰਾਠ, a chief or fighting man; BSS: a warrior.
[^7]: nagārā. BSS: from the sound the Turks knew the Singhs' camp lay further on.
[^8]: BSS: a Pathan in the army who was Tara Singh's friend; he learned where the army was going, since soldiers were told only after they had set out.
[^9]: degh.
[^10]: Dwarka: Krishna's city on the coast of Gujarat, where Vaishnava pilgrims were branded with the marks of the conch and discus. BSS: so the way of the Khalsa is that every weapon that strikes the body makes it pure.
[^11]: BSS: some of the Singhs had gone for water and saw the Turks coming; when they came back, Tara Singh gave the order to all.
[^12]: ਧੌਂਸ ਨਗਾਰ, great war drums · sauṅchī: a Punjabi contest of strength between strong young men, in which a player strikes at the other side and must break away without being caught. BSS calls it sauṅchī pakkī.
[^13]: tegh: the broad curved sword · arrow: tīr.
[^14]: BSS: they stood back to back, facing all four sides.
[^15]: BSS: as the horses charged, the Singhs struck them in the mouth or forehead with spears.
[^16]: bandūk.
[^17]: tamāchā.
[^18]: ਤਕੀ; Kulwant Singh calls him Takki Khan, BSS Taki Beg.
[^19]: ਮੁੰਡਾਸਾ; BSS: his dumala · armour: ਸੰਜੋਇ, a coat of mail.
[^20]: ਪਿਪਲਾ, the tip of the sword.
[^21]: pān: betel leaf, chewed with lime and areca nut, which stains the mouth red. BSS: Moman Khan saw the stream of blood from his mouth.
[^22]: ਬੀੜੇ, folded packets of pān. BSS: Tara Singh sits there with a tray of pān, handing one to each man who goes forward.
[^23]: chelā. BSS: the army's five elephants carried its chiefs, Moman Khan's disciples; this one was a strong young man who set two arrows on his bow at once.
[^24]: BSS: Bhima Singh was a Singh from Peshawar · pressing the ear is the signal by which those who know elephants make them sit.
[^25]: ਅੰਕੁਸ, the elephant driver's iron hook · sword: talwār.
[^26]: ਕੱਟਾ, a young buffalo calf; Tara Singh's joke on the elephant.
[^27]: falcon: ਬਹਰੀ; BSS: a hunting bird like a hawk · cranes: ਕੁੰਜ.
[^28]: nejā.
[^29]: BSS: Tara Singh said it; when the Khalsa rules, they will give many elephants even to their servants.
[^30]: Purbias: soldiers from the east (Purab), Hindustanis in Mughal service · Hazari: a Mughal rank, commander of a thousand. BSS: some held the rank of five thousand or ten thousand.
[^31]: Kundalpur: unidentified.
[^32]: ਸ਼ੀਹਆਣਾ; BSS: fear.
[^33]: The twenty-two are counted in Episode 84, verse 57.
[^34]: resolve: ਸਿਰਰ. The line is Guru Gobind Singh's on Guru Tegh Bahadur, Bachittar Natak 5.14: ਸੀਸੁ ਦੀਆ ਪਰ ਸਿਰਰੁ ਨ ਦੀਆ.

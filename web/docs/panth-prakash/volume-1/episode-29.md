# Panth Prakash, Episode 29

## The Episode of the Meeting with Banda

### (tab un kahyo 'maiṅ baṅdā torā'...: Then he said, 'I am your servant'...)

>note:\
>1. What angered Banda?
>2. Mission given to Banda by the Guru
>3. The teaching about becoming a Sikh
>4. Significance of 5 arrows
>5. Names of 5 Singhs in command, assigned by Guru

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਪਹੁੰਚਯੋ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਜਿਹ ਸਮੇਂ ਗਯੋ ਬੰਦੋ ਕਹੂੰ ਔਰ ॥\
ਪਲੰਘ ਸ਼ਿੰਗਾਰਯੋ ਦੇਖਿਕੈ ਗੁਰ ਚੜ੍ਹ ਬੈਠੇ ਦੌਰ ॥੧॥

When the Satguru arrived, Banda[^1] had gone off somewhere else;\
seeing the bed decked out in finery, the Guru ran, climbed up, and sat upon it. (1)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਤਿਸ ਕੇ ਬਕਰੇ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਘਾਏ ॥ ਕਾਟ ਕੂਟ ਦਏ ਦੇਗਨ ਪਾਏ ॥\
ਤਿਸ ਚੇਲਨ ਜਾ ਕਰੀ ਪੁਕਾਰ ॥ ਕ੍ਰਾਇ ਕ੍ਰੋਧ ਲੈ ਆਏ ਨਾਰ ॥੨॥

The Singhs killed his goats,[^2]\
hacked them, chopped them, and put them in the deghs.[^3]\
His disciples went and raised the cry,\
stirred him to anger, and brought him back with them. (2)

ਦੇਖਤ ਪਲੰਘ ਸੁ ਅਤਿ ਰੁਹ ਭਰਯੋ ॥ ਕ੍ਰੋਧਤ ਬੀਰਨ ਹੁਕਮ ਕੋ ਕਰਯੋ ॥\
ਜਾ ਬੀਰਨ ਤਬ ਪਲੰਘ ਉਠਾਯਾ ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਤੀਰਹਿ ਸਾਥ ਦਬਾਯਾ ॥੩॥

When he saw the bed, he was filled with fury,\
and in his rage he gave his familiar spirits[^4] the order.\
The spirits went and lifted the bed,\
and the Satguru pressed it down with his arrows.[^5] (3)

ਫੇਰ ਮਵੱਕਲਨ ਲਾਯੋ ਤਾਨ ॥ ਤੌ ਫਿਰ ਉਠਯੋ ਨ ਅੰਗੁਲ ਸਮਾਨ ॥੪॥

Then the spirits[^6] strained with all their might,\
and still it would not rise a finger's breadth. (4)

ਪਲੰਘ ਅਚੱਲ ਲਖ ਹੁਇ ਗਯੋ ਕੋਇਲਾ ॥ ਕ੍ਰੋਧ ਸਾਥ ਫਿਰ ਮ੍ਵਕੱਲਨ ਬੋਲਾ ॥\
ਤੁਰਤ ਇਸੈ ਕੀ ਜਾਨ ਉਡਾਵੋ ॥ ਇਤੀ ਦੇਰ ਤੁਮ ਕਾਹੇ ਲਾਵੋ ॥੫॥

Seeing the bed would not move, he burned to a coal,\
and in his anger he spoke to the spirits again:\
"Take this man's life at once.\
Why are you taking so long?" (5)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਤਬੈ ਮ੍ਵਕੱਲਨ ਉਸ ਕਹਯੋ ਹਮ ਕਿਛ ਰਖਯੋ ਨ ਤਾਣ ॥\
ਜੌ ਇਨ ਕੋ ਹਮ ਦੁਖ ਦੇਵੈਂ ਸੋ ਪਵੈ ਹਮੈ ਪਰ ਆਣ ॥੬॥

Then the spirits said to him, "We have held back nothing of our strength;\
if we bring him harm, it comes down upon ourselves. (6)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਜੌ ਉਸ ਕੋ ਹਮ ਦੁਖ ਚਹੈਂ ਲਾਯਾ ॥ ਹੋਤ ਰਿਦੇ ਹਮ ਦੋਖ ਸਵਾਯਾ ॥\
ਜੌ ਹਮ ਇਸ ਕੇ ਨੇੜੈ ਜਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਤੇਜ ਅਗਨ ਸੋਂ ਹਮੈਂ ਜਲਾਵੈਂ ॥੭॥

"If we try to bring him any harm,\
the pain falls on our own hearts, and a quarter more;\
and if we go near him,\
he burns us in his blazing fire." (7)

ਤਦੈ ਮਵਕਲਨ ਐਸ ਉਚਾਰੀ ॥ ਇਨ ਕੈ ਆਗੇ ਹਮ ਭਏ ਹਾਰੀ ॥\
ਜਬ ਹਮ ਉਨ ਕੈ ਨੇੜੈ ਜਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਆਪ ਕੋਪ ਤੇ ਆਪ ਜਲਾਵੈਂ ॥੮॥

Then the spirits spoke these words:\
"Before him we are beaten.\
When we go near him,\
we burn ourselves in our own fury. (8)

ਛੁਟੈਂ ਤਬੈ ਜਬ ਸੀਸ ਨਿਵਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਹੋਹਿਂ ਸੀਤਲ ਤਬ ਅਤਿ ਸੁਖ ਪਾਵੈਂ ॥\
ਐਸੇ ਸੁਨੇ ਉਨੇਂ ਤੇ ਬੈਨ ॥ ਭਯੋ ਅਚੰਭੇ ਅਤਿ ਭੈ ਕੈਨ ॥੯॥

"We are free only when we bow our heads;\
then we grow cool, and know great ease."\
When he heard such words from them,\
he was astonished and filled with fear. (9)

ਜਬ ਉਨ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਨੈਨੀ ਦੇਖਾ ॥ ਅਪਨੇ ਬਲ ਤੇ ਅਧਿਕ ਬਹੁ ਲੇਖਾ ॥\
ਫਿਰ ਸਿੱਖਨ ਪ੍ਰਤਿ ਬੀਰ ਚਲਾਏ ॥ ਜਿਨ ਜਿਨ ਨੈ ਥੇ ਬਕਰੇ ਘਾਏ ॥੧੦॥

When he saw the Satguru with his own eyes,\
he reckoned him far greater than his own strength.\
Then he sent his spirits against the Sikhs,\
each and every one who had killed the goats. (10)

*Sortha* ਸੋਰਠਾ

ਸੋ ਫਿਰ ਆਏ ਬੀਰ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੈ ਢਿਗ ਸੁਨ ਢੁਕੈ ॥\
ਉਨ ਕੋ ਰੱਛਕ ਤੀਰ ਫਿਰੈ ਸੁਦਰਸ਼ਨ ਚੱਕ੍ਰ ਜਿਮ ॥੧੧॥

So the spirits came on, and hearing his order, closed in on the Singhs;\
but the Guru's arrows circled round to guard them, like the Sudarshan chakra.[^7] (11)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਬੀਰ ਫਿਰੇ ਜਬ ਸਿਖਨ ਤੇ ਤਬ ਉਨ ਨਿਸਚੋ ਆਇ ॥\
ਏ ਪੂਰੇ ਹੈਂ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੂ ਇਨ ਸਿਖਨ ਸਮ ਹਮ ਨਾਹਿ ॥੧੨॥

When the spirits turned back from the Sikhs, he knew for certain:\
*He is the perfect Satguru; I am not the equal of these Sikhs.* (12)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਤਬ ਅਧੀਨ ਹੁਇ ਚਰਨੀ ਪਰਯੋ ॥ ਤਬ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮੁਖ ਬਚਨ ਉਚਰਯੋ ॥\
ਕੋ ਤੂੰ ਪੰਥੀ ਕੋ ਗੁਰ ਤੇਰਾ ॥ ਕਰੈਂ ਮਸਕਰੀ ਸੰਤਨ ਕੇਰਾ ॥੧੩॥

Then he humbled himself and fell at his feet,\
and the Satguru spoke these words:\
"Whose path do you follow? Who is your guru,\
that you make a mockery of holy men? (13)

ਕਯਾ ਤੁਧ ਸਮਸਰ ਜਗ ਮੈਂ ਨਾਹੀਂ ॥ ਕਯਾ ਬਡ ਕਲਾ ਇਕ ਤੁਮਹੀ ਮਾਂਹੀ ॥੧੪॥

"Is there no one in the world your equal?\
Is all the great power in you alone? (14)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਕਰੇ ਮਸਕਰੀ ਸੰਤ ਜੋ ਸੋਊ ਹੋਹਿ ਖੁਵਾਰ ॥\
ਭਾਂਡ ਭਗਤੀਏ ਡੂਮ ਲੁੱਚੇ ਇਨ ਕੀ ਹੈ ਯਹ ਕਾਰ ॥੧੫॥

"Whoever makes a mockery of holy men is brought to ruin.\
Jesters,[^8] play-actors, Doms and rogues: this is their trade." (15)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਤਬ ਉਨ ਕਹਯੋ ਮੈਂ ਬੰਦਾ ਤੋਰਾ ॥ ਮੈਂ ਤੁਹਿ ਸਿਖ ਗੁਰੂ ਤੂੰ ਮੋਰਾ ॥\
ਪਿਛਲੀ ਬਾਤ ਮਾਫ ਹਮ ਕੀਜੈ ॥ ਅਬ ਹੁਇ ਫਿਰ ਤੌ ਜੀ ਆਯੋ ਕੀਜੈ ॥੧੬॥

Then he said, "I am your servant;[^9]\
I am your Sikh, and you are my Guru.\
Forgive me what is past;\
and if it happens again, do with me as you will. (16)

ਅਬ ਮੈਂ ਭਯੋ ਤੁਮਾਰੋ ਦਾਸ ॥ ਸੋ ਮੈਂ ਕਰੋਂ ਹੁਕਮ ਹੋਇ ਖਾਸ ॥\
ਹਮ ਕੋ ਕੀਜੈ ਅਪਨੌ ਦਾਸ ॥ ਦਾਸ ਜਾਨ ਰਾਖੋ ਨਿਜ ਪਾਸ ॥੧੭॥

"Now I have become your servant;\
whatever you command, that I will do.\
Make me your own servant;\
know me as your servant and keep me by your side." (17)

**The Guru's Grace on Banda, and Sending Him toward the Punjab** ਬੰਦੇ ਪਰ ਗੁਰੂ ਜੀ ਦੀ ਮੇਹਰ ਤੇ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਵਲ ਤੋਰਨਾ

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਜਬ ਐਸੇ ਉਨ ਬਚ ਕਹੇ ਹਾਥ ਜੋੜ ਸਿਰ ਨਿਯਾਇ ॥\
ਭਏ ਪ੍ਰਸੰਨ ਤਬ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੂ ਲੀਨੋ ਚਰਨਨ ਲਾਇ ॥੧॥

When he had spoken such words, his hands joined and his head bowed,\
the Satguru was pleased and took him to his feet. (18)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕਹੀ ਸਿਖ ਬਨਨੋ ਔਖਾ ॥ ਮਨ ਤਨ ਧਨ ਰਖ ਹੋਇ ਨਾ ਤੋਖਾ ॥\
ਸਿੱਖ ਬਨੈ ਜਬ ਖੋਏ ਭਰਮ ॥ ਸਿੱਖ ਬਨਨ ਕੈ ਕਠਨ ਸੁ ਕਰਮ ॥੨॥

The Satguru said, "To become a Sikh is hard:\
he lays down mind and body and wealth, and holds no misgiving.\
A man becomes a Sikh when he has lost his delusion;\
the works that make a Sikh are hard. (19)

ਸਿੱਖ ਹੋਇ ਜੋ ਆਪਾ ਖੋਵੈ ॥ ਮੇਟ ਕੁਲੈ ਨਿਜ ਗੁਰ ਕੁਲ ਹੋਵੈ ॥\
ਜਿਮ ਕਰ ਕੀਟ ਨਿਜ ਕੁਲੈ ਤਿਆਗੈ ॥ ਭਵੈ ਭਰਿੰਗੀ ਉਸ ਕੁਲ ਲਾਗੈ ॥੩॥

"He is a Sikh who loses his self,\
who wipes out his own lineage and enters the lineage of his Guru,\
as the grub forsakes its own lineage,\
becomes a wasp,[^10] and is joined to the wasp's lineage. (20)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਵਾਲੋਂ ਨਿਕੀ ਖੰਨਯੋ ਤਿੱਖੀ ਹੈ ਸਿੱਖੀ ਕੀ ਰੀਤਿ ॥\
ਸਿਰ ਤੇ ਪਰੇ ਸੇਵਾ ਕਰੇ ਤਊ ਡਰੇ ਮਧ ਚੀਤ ॥੪॥

"Finer than a hair and sharper than the khanda[^11] is the way of the Sikhs;\
he serves with his head already given, and still he fears within his heart." (21)

ਜੋ ਆਖੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਉਸੈ ਸੋ ਉਨ ਲੀਨੀ ਮਾਨ ॥\
ਧਰਯੋ ਸੁ ਬੰਦਾ ਨਾਮ ਤਿਸ ਤਿਨ ਕਰ ਲੀਯੋ ਪ੍ਰਮਾਨ ॥੫॥

All that the Satguru told him, he accepted;\
the Guru gave him the name Banda,[^12] and he took it for his own. (22)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਬਾਰ ਬਾਰ ਬੰਦੋ ਪਗ ਪਰਯੋ ॥ ਤੌ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਿਰ ਪੈ ਹਥ ਧਰਯੋ ॥\
ਬੰਦੈ ਕਹੀ ਕਿਛ ਆਗਯਾ ਦੇਹੁ ॥ ਖਿਜਮਤ ਹਮੈ ਕੋਊ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ੇਹੁ ॥੬॥

Again and again Banda fell at his feet,\
and then the Satguru laid his hand upon his head.\
Banda said, "Give me some command;\
grant me some service to do." (23)

ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕਹੀ ਤੁਮ ਜੋਗ ਯਹਿ ਕਾਰ ॥ ਜਾ ਸਾਹਿਬਜ਼ਾਦਨ ਘਾਤੀ ਮਾਰ ॥\
ਸ੍ਰਿਹੰਦ ਕੋ ਮਾਰ ਫਿਰ ਮਾਰੀਂ ਪਹਾਰ ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੌਂਪੀ ਤਿਸ ਯਹ ਕਾਰ ॥੭॥

The Satguru said, "This is the work fit for you:\
go, strike down the killers of the Sahibzadas;[^13]\
strike Sirhind, and then strike the hills."[^14]\
This was the work the Satguru gave into his hands. (24)

ਸਿੱਖਨ ਰੱਖ ਅਸਿੱਖ ਸੰਘਰੀਓ ॥ ਦਾਸ ਹੋੋਇ ਤਾਂ ਉਸੈ ਉਬਰੀਓ ॥\
ਦਿਲੀਓਂ ਲਗ ਕਰ ਦੰਗਾ ਮਚਾਵੋ ॥ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਪ੍ਰਬਤ ਸਭ ਧੂੜ ਮਿਲਾਵੋ ॥੮॥

"Protect the Sikhs, and destroy those who are anti-Sikhs;\
but if a man becomes your servant, then spare him.\
Stir up fighting from Delhi onward,\
and lay the Punjab and the hills in the dust. (25)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਬਿਰਛਨ ਕੇ ਸਿਰ ਜੋ ਲਗੇ ਕਹਿ ਬੰਦੋ ਉਸ ਜੱਗ ॥\
ਹਮਰੋ ਬੰਦੋ ਤੂੰ ਬਨ ਸੋ ਤੂੰ ਜਾਇਂ ਪਤਸ਼ਾਹੀ ਲੱਗ ॥੯॥

"What fastens on the crowns of trees, the world calls the baṅdā;[^15]\
become my baṅdā, then, and go and fasten on the Patshahi." (26)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਆਖੀ ਜੋ ਬੰਦੈ ਸੁ ਉਨ ਕਰੀ ਮਨਜ਼ੂਰ ॥\
ਪਰਗਟ ਹੋਈ ਜਗਤ ਗਲ ਕੀਯੋ ਬੰਦਾ ਭਰਪੂਰ ॥੧੦ ॥

What the Satguru told Banda, he accepted,\
and word went out through the world that the Guru had filled Banda with power. (27)

ਬੰਦੇ ਗੁਰ ਖੰਡਾ ਦਯੋ ਲਯੋ ਉਨੈਂ ਗਲ ਪਾਇ ॥\
ਖਾਲਸੋ ਦੇਖ ਸੁ ਵਿਟਰਿਓ ਤਿਨ ਖੰਡੋ ਲਯੋ ਛਿਨਾਇ ॥੧੧॥

The Guru gave Banda a khanda, and he slung it from his shoulder;\
the Khalsa saw it and flared up, and snatched the khanda away.[^16] (28)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਤਬ ਫਰਯਾਦ ਉਨ ਗੁਰ ਪਹਿ ਕਰੀ ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਹਸ ਹਸ ਚਿਤ ਬਿਚਰੀ ॥\
ਚੀਜ ਆਪਨੀ ਇਨ੍ਹੈਂ ਸੰਭਾਰੀ ॥ ਭਯੋ ਖਾਲਸੋ ਅਬ ਕਲ ਧਾਰੀ ॥੧੨॥

Then he made his complaint to the Guru,\
and the Satguru laughed and laughed, and thought it over:\
*They have taken back what is their own;\
now the Khalsa has come into its power.* (29)

ਬਖਸ਼ੈ ਬੰਦੇ ਕੋ ਪੰਜ ਤੀਰ ॥ ਕਹਯੋ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਇਹ ਪੰਜ ਹੈਂ ਬੀਰ ॥\
ਪੜੈ ਲੋੜ ਜਿਤ ਵਲ ਤੁਹਿ ਆਇ ॥ ਇਨ ਮੈਂ ਤੇ ਇਕ ਦਈਂ ਚਲਾਇ ॥੧੩॥

He gave Banda five arrows as a gift.\
The Satguru said, "These five are spirits.\
Wherever need comes upon you,\
shoot one of them. (30)

ਪੌਣ ਵਗੈ ਬਹੁ ਧੂੜ ਉਡਾਇ ॥ ਦੁਸ਼ਮਨ ਦ੍ਰਿਸ਼ਟ ਅੰਧ ਹੁਇ ਆਇ ॥\
ਉਲਟੈ ਫੌਜ ਪੌਨ ਪਲਟਾਇ ॥ ਦੁਸ਼ਮਨ ਮਾਰ ਫਤੇ ਜੰਗ ਪਾਇ ॥੧੪॥

"A wind will blow and raise great dust;\
the enemy's sight will go blind;\
the wind will turn and throw their army back;\
you will kill the enemy and win the battle. (31)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਜੋਇ ਸਿਖ ਤੁਮ ਆ ਮਿਲੈ ਤਿਨ ਤੂੰ ਲਈਂ ਮਿਲਾਇ ॥\
ਆਦਰ ਦੈ ਦੈ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਭਲ ਆਗੇ ਦਯੋ ਚੜ੍ਹਾਇ ॥੧੫॥

"Whatever Sikhs come to join you, take them in with you;\
honour them again and again in every good way, and send them forward. (32)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਸਿੰਘ ਮਝੈਲਨ ਲਈਂ ਬੁਲਾਇ ॥ ਉਨ ਕੋ ਮੁਹਰੇ ਲੀਜੋ ਲਾਇ ॥\
ਓਇ ਹੈਂ ਮਹਾਂ ਸੂਰ ਅਤਿ ਹਠੀ ॥ ਉਨ ਤੇ ਫੌਜ ਤੁਰਕ ਜਾਇ ਨਠੀ ॥੧੬॥

"Call the Majhail[^17] Singhs to you," he said,\
"and put them in the front.\
They are mighty heroes, utterly unyielding;\
the army of the Turks[^18] runs from them. (33)

ਓਇ ਹੈਂ ਸੂਰ ਮਹਾਂ ਬਲਵਾਨ ॥ ਉਨ ਪੈ ਹੈਂ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮਿਹਰਵਾਨ ॥\
ਓਇ ਹੈਂ ਗੁਰ ਨਿਜ ਪ੍ਰਣ ਕੇ ਪੂਰੇ ॥ ਓਇ ਸੂਰਨ ਮੈਂ ਹੈਂ ਅਤਿ ਸੂਰੇ ॥੧੭॥

"They are heroes of great strength,\
and the Satguru is gracious to them.\
They keep their vow to the Guru to the full;\
among heroes they are the greatest heroes. (34)

ਓਂਇ ਨਿਜ ਅਪਨੇ ਗੁਰ ਕੇ ਭਗਤੁ ॥ ਉਨ ਮੈਂ ਹੈਂ ਸਭ ਹਮਰੀ ਸ਼ਕਤਿ ॥\
ਉਨਕੋ ਹਮ ਬਖ਼ਸੀ ਗੁਰਿਆਈ ॥ ਹਮ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ੀ ਉਨ ਲਈ ਸੰਭਾਈ ॥੧੮॥

"They are devotees of their own Guru;\
all my power is in them.\
To them I have given the Guruship;\
I gave it, and they have taken it up and kept it. (35)

ਹਮ ਮੈ ਉਨ ਮੈਂ ਭੇਦ ਕਿਛ ਨਾਹਿ ॥ ਉਨ ਮੈਂ ਹਮ ਓਇ ਹਮਰੇ ਮਾਂਹਿ ॥\
ਐਸੇ ਬੰਦਾ ਦਯੋ ਸਮਝਾਇ ॥ ਚੰਗੀ ਵਿਧ ਵਹਿ ਚਹੈ ਕਮਾਇ ॥੧੯॥

"Between me and them there is no difference;\
I am in them, and they are in me."\
So he made Banda understand,\
that he might carry it out in the right way. (36)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਜਬ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਆਗਯਾ ਦਈ ਬੰਦੇ ਪੰਜਾਬੇ ਜਾਣ ॥\
ਸਹਿਬਜ਼ਾਦੈ ਜਿਨ ਘਏ ਤਿਨ ਕੋ ਕਰਨੈ ਘਾਣ ॥੨੦॥

The Satguru commanded Banda to go to the Punjab\
and crush like seed in the oil press those who had slain the Sahibzadas. (37)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਜੋ ਜੋ ਆਗਯਾ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਦਈ ॥ ਸੋ ਬੰਦੈ ਕਾਗਜ਼ ਲਿਖ ਲਈ ॥\
ਹਮਰੋ ਸੰਗ ਭੇਜੀਓ ਸੋਊ ॥ ਬਤਾਵੈ ਸਿਖ ਅਸਿਖੈ ਜੋਊ ॥੨੧॥

Every command the Satguru gave,\
Banda wrote down on paper.\
"Send with me," he said, "those men\
who can tell a Sikh from one who is not." (38)

ਤਬ ਗੁਰ ਸਿੰਘ ਮਝੈਲ ਰਲਾਏ ॥ ਬਾਬੇ ਬਿਨੋਦ ਸਿੰਘ, ਕਾਨ੍ਹ ਸਿੰਘ ਚੜ੍ਹਾਏ ॥\
ਦਯਾ ਮਿੰਘ ਔਣਿਨ ਸਿੰਘ ਭਲੇ ॥ ਭੇ ਹਜੂਰ ਭੁਜੰਗੀ ਬੰਦੈ ਘਲੇ ॥੨੨॥

Then the Guru joined Majhail Singhs to him;\
he sent along Baba Binod Singh and Kahn Singh,[^19]\
Daya Singh and Aunin Singh of the Bhallas, [^20]\
and from his own presence he sent bhujaṅgīs[^21] with Banda. (39)

ਬਾਜ ਸਿੰਘ ਬਲ ਮੀਰ ਪੁਰ ਪੱਟੀ ਵਾਰੋ ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੰਗ ਦਏ ਭਾਈ ਚਾਰੋਂ ॥\
ਔਰ ਚੜ੍ਹਾਏ ਬਡ ਸਿਦਕ ਵਾਰੇ ॥ ਰਹਤ ਹਜੂਰ ਥੇ ਗੁਰੂ ਪਿਆਰੇ ॥੨੩॥

With him went Baj Singh, a Bal of Mirpur by Patti;[^22]\
the Satguru sent the four brothers along,\
and others too, men of great and steadfast faith,\
who lived in his presence and were dear to the Guru. (40)

ਮਝੈਲ ਸਿੱਖਨ ਕੋ ਲਿਖੀ ਅਰਦਾਸ ॥ ਦੇਖਤ ਆਯੋ ਬੰਦੈ ਕੇ ਹੀ ਪਾਸ ॥\
ਜੋ ਹੈਂ ਦੋਖੀ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਭਾਰੇ ॥ ਜਿੰਨੇ ਸ਼ਾਹਬਜ਼ਾਦੈ ਹੈਂ ਮਾਰੇ ॥੨੪॥

To the Majhail Sikhs the Guru wrote an ardās:[^23]\
"The moment you see this, come straight to Banda.\
The Khalsa's great enemies\
are those who have killed the Sahibzadas. (41)

ਕਰਯੋ ਖੋਟ ਜਿਨ ਪਰਬਤ ਵਾਰਨ ॥ ਤਿਨਕੋ ਲੂਟੋ ਖੂਬ ਪਹਾਰਨ ॥\
ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮ ਬਜੀਦੈ ਲੀਜੋ ਮਾਰ ॥ ਬਹੁਤ ਮੁਰਦਾ ਤਿਹਿ ਕਰਯੋ ਖੁਵਾਰ ॥੨੫॥

"As for the men of the hills who dealt falsely,\
plunder those hillmen well.\
First of all kill Bajid,[^24]\
and bring his corpse to utter disgrace." (42)

### Notes

[^1]: Banda: the poet gives him this name before the Guru does (verse 22). He is Narain Das, the poet's name for Madho Das, the Bairagi mahant (a Vaishnava ascetic and the head of his own house) of Episode 28, which ends with the Guru at his bed.
[^2]: Baba Santa Singh Akali's account: the goats roamed the mahant's garden, held by his spell so that no bird pecked a fruit and no goat touched a plant; the Singhs caught them, washed them at the Persian wheel and killed them by jhatka, the single stroke.
[^3]: deghs: the great cooking pots of the Guru's kitchen.
[^4]: familiar spirits: the poet's bīr, a spirit bound by spells to serve its master.
[^5]: Baba Santa Singh's account: the Guru took four arrows from his quiver and laid one on each leg of the bed.
[^6]: spirits: here the poet's muwakkil, an Arabic word for a spirit set to serve; the same spirits as in verse 3.
[^7]: Sudarshan chakra: the whirling discus of Vishnu, wielded by Krishna.
[^8]: jesters, play-actors: the poet's bhāṅd and bhagtīe, the buffoons and the players of the sāṅg, the village farce · Dom: a caste of hereditary singers and drummers; Baba Santa Singh calls them Mirasis.
[^9]: servant: the word is baṅdā, a servant or a slave. The Guru makes it his name at verse 22.
[^10]: the wasp: the poet's bhariṅgī, the mason wasp of the old saying, which seals a grub in a cell of mud and hums over it until the grub becomes a wasp like itself.
[^11]: khanda: the double-edged sword. The line is an old Sikh saying.
[^12]: Banda: servant (verse 16). Baba Santa Singh's account is that the Guru named him Banda Singh, and the Khalsa later added Bahadur, the brave, for the valour he showed in battle after battle.
[^13]: Sahibzadas: the Guru's four sons. The two elder fell at Chamkaur; the two younger were put to death at Sirhind.
[^14]: the hills: the hill rajas, with whom, in Baba Santa Singh's reading, the whole quarrel began.
[^15]: baṅdā: a rootless plant that grows on the crown of a tree and spreads over it until the tree dries up (Baba Santa Singh: the amar vel, the dodder). The same word means servant, and is Banda's name.
[^16]: Baba Santa Singh's account: the Singhs, who had served the Guru and kept his watches, took it ill that a newcomer should walk off with the khanda, and took it from him as soon as he stepped away from the Guru.
[^17]: Majhail: of the Majha, the country between the Beas and the Ravi around Amritsar, where the earlier Gurus founded Khadur, Goindwal, Amritsar and Tarn Taran.
[^18]: Turks: the poet's word for the Muslim rulers and their armies, here the Mughals.
[^19]: Baba Santa Singh's account: Binod Singh of Khadur was of Guru Angad's line, the Trehan clan, and Kahn Singh of Goindwal of Guru Amar Das's line, the Bhallas.
[^20]: Bhallas: the Khatri clan of Guru Amar Das; Baba Santa Singh counts these two of his line as well. Alternatively, the names are also understood as being Baj Singh, and Fateh Singh.
[^21]: bhujaṅgīs: young Singhs, literally serpents; Baba Santa Singh: warriors like black cobras, who stayed close to the Guru.
[^22]: Bal: a Jatt clan · Mirpur: a village by Patti, a town of the Majha south of Amritsar · four brothers: the verse does not name them; Kulwant Singh names Ram Singh as Baj Singh's brother.
[^23]: ardās: the Sikh prayer of petition; here a written request from the Guru, which Baba Santa Singh calls a hukamnama.
[^24]: Bajid: the poet's form of Wazir Khan, the commander of Sirhind, who had the Guru's two younger sons put to death.

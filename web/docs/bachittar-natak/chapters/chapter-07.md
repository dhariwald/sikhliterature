# Bachittar Natak, Chapter 7

## ਅਥ ਕਬਿ ਜਨਮ ਕਥਨੰ ॥ The Telling of the Poet's Birth

>**Questions**
>
>City where the Guru was born (prakash asthan)?

#### ਅਥ ਕਬਿ ਜਨਮ ਕਥਨੰ ॥

ਚੌਪਈ ॥ *Chaupai*

ਮੁਰ ਪਿਤ ਪੂਰਬ ਕੀਯਸਿ ਪਯਾਨਾ ॥ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਕੇ ਤੀਰਥਿ ਨਾਨਾ ॥\
ਜਬ ਹੀ ਜਾਤ ਤ੍ਰਿਬੇਣੀ ਭਏ ॥ ਪੁੰਨ ਦਾਨ ਦਿਨ ਕਰਤ ਬਿਤਏ ॥੧॥

My father[^1] set out for the east\
and bathed at places of pilgrimage of every kind;\
when he reached the Triveni,[^2]\
he spent his days in works of merit and in giving. (1)

ਤਹੀ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਸ ਹਮਾਰਾ ਭਯੋ ॥ ਪਟਨਾ ਸਹਰ ਬਿਖੈ ਭਵ ਲਯੋ ॥\
ਮੱਦ੍ਰ ਦੇਸ ਹਮ ਕੋ ਲੇ ਆਏ ॥ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਦਾਈਅਨਿ ਦੁਲਰਾਏ ॥੨॥

It was there that my light appeared,[^3]\
and in the city of Patna[^4] I took birth;\
they brought me to the Madra country,[^5]\
and nurses cherished me in every way. (2)

ਕੀਨੀ ਅਨਿਕ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਤਨ ਰੱਛਾ ॥ ਦੀਨੀ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਕੀ ਸਿੱਛਾ ॥\
ਜਬ ਹਮ ਧਰਮ ਕਰਮ ਮੋ ਆਏ ॥ ਦੇਵ ਲੋਕ ਤਬ ਪਿਤਾ ਸਿਧਾਏ ॥੩॥

My body was guarded in many ways,\
and I was taught in every way;\
when I came of age for the duties of dharam,[^6]\
my father departed for the world of the gods.[^7] (3)

---
ਇਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਬਚਿਤ੍ਰ ਨਾਟਕ ਗ੍ਰੰਥੇ ਕਬਿ ਜਨਮ ਕਥਨੰ ਨਾਮ ਸਪਤਮੋ ਧਿਆਇ ਸਮਾਪਤ ਮਸਤੁ ਸੁਭ ਮਸਤੁ ॥੭॥ ਅਫਜੂ ॥੨੮੨॥

*Here ends the seventh chapter of the holy book Bachittar Natak, called the Telling of the Poet's Birth. Let it be complete, and let it be blessed. Chapter 7; verses so far, 282.*

*Sri Dasam Granth, Bachittar Natak, Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji. Gurmukhi text of record from the Shabad OS database, Sri Dasam Granth, section 54; the closing line divided ਸਮਾਪਤ ਮਸਤੁ ਸੁਭ ਮਸਤੁ (samāptam astu, shubham astu). Punjabi commentary from the steek of Narain Singh. Cite as Bachittar Natak 7.2.*
---
### Notes

[^1]: Guru Tegh Bahadur, who travelled east preaching the faith.
[^2]: The Triveni (ਤ੍ਰਿਬੇਣੀ), the meeting of the Ganges, the Jamuna and the unseen Saraswati at Prayag (Allahabad).
[^3]: Narain Singh: the Guru was conceived at Prayag and born after his parents reached Patna.
[^4]: Patna, in Bihar, where Takht Sri Patna Sahib now stands.
[^5]: The Madra country (ਮੱਦ੍ਰ ਦੇਸ), the old name for the Punjab; Narain Singh: Anandpur in the Punjab.
[^6]: Dharam (ਧਰਮ), duty and the right way; the word stays dharam wherever it appears.
[^7]: The world of the gods (ਦੇਵ ਲੋਕ). Narain Singh: his father was martyred in Delhi.

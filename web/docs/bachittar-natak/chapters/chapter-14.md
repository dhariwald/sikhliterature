# Bachittar Natak, Chapter 14

## The Prayer to Sarab Kal

>**Questions**

>1. Who the Guru describes as his Father and Mother

ਚੌਪਈ ॥ *Chaupai*

ਸਰਬ ਕਾਲ ਸਭ ਸਾਧ ਉਬਾਰੇ ॥ ਦੁਖੁ ਦੈ ਕੈ ਦੋਖੀ ਸਭ ਮਾਰੇ ॥\
ਅਦਭੁਤਿ ਗਤਿ ਭਗਤਨ ਦਿਖਰਾਈ ॥ ਸਭ ਸੰਕਟ ਤੇ ਲਏ ਬਚਾਈ ॥੧॥

Sarab Kal[^1] saved all the holy\
and struck down all their slanderers with pain;\
he showed his devotees his wondrous ways\
and saved them from every trouble. (1)

ਸਭ ਸੰਕਟ ਤੇ ਸੰਤ ਬਚਾਏ ॥ ਸਭ ਕੰਟਕ ਕੰਟਕ ਜਿਮ ਘਾਏ ॥\
ਦਾਸ ਜਾਨ ਮੁਹਿ ਕਰੀ ਸਹਾਇ ॥ ਆਪ ਹਾਥ ਦੈ ਲਯੋ ਬਚਾਇ ॥੨॥

He saved the saints from every trouble\
and destroyed all their tormentors as thorns are destroyed;\
knowing me his servant, he came to my help,\
gave me his own hand and saved me. (2)

ਅਬ ਜੋ ਜੋ ਮੈਂ ਲਖੇ ਤਮਾਸਾ ॥ ਸੋ ਸੋ ਕਰੋ ਤੁਮੈ ਅਰਦਾਸਾ ॥\
ਜੋ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਕਿਰਪਾ ਕਟਾਛ ਦਿਖੈਹੈ ॥ ਸੋ ਤਵ ਦਾਸ ਉਚਾਰਤ ਜੈਹੈ ॥੩॥

Now every wonder I have seen,\
I set before you, one by one, as my humble prayer;\
whatever Prabhu shows me in his glance of mercy,\
your servant will go on telling. (3)

ਜਿਹ ਜਿਹ ਬਿਧ ਮੈ ਲਖੇ ਤਮਾਸਾ ॥ ਚਾਹਤ ਤਿਨ ਕੋ ਕੀਯੋ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਸਾ ॥\
ਜੋ ਜੋ ਜਨਮ ਪੂਰਬਲੇ ਹੇਰੇ ॥ ਕਹਿਹੋ ਸੁ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਪਰਾਕ੍ਰਮ ਤੇਰੇ ॥੪॥

In whatever ways I have seen these wonders,\
I wish to bring them to light;\
every former birth I have looked upon\
I shall tell, Prabhu, as your mighty deeds. (4)

ਸਰਬ ਕਾਲ ਹੈ ਪਿਤਾ ਅਪਾਰਾ ॥ ਦੇਬਿ ਕਾਲਿਕਾ ਮਾਤ ਹਮਾਰਾ ॥\
ਮਨੂਆ ਗੁਰ ਮੁਰਿ ਮਨਸਾ ਮਾਈ ॥ ਜਿਨ ਮੋ ਕੋ ਸੁਭ ਕ੍ਰਿਆ ਪੜ੍ਹਾਈ ॥੫॥

Sarab Kal is our boundless father,\
the goddess Kalika[^2] our mother;\
the mind is my guru, and Mansa my mother,[^3]\
who taught me to do good. (5)

ਜਬ ਮਨਸਾ ਮਨ ਮਯਾ ਬਿਚਾਰੀ ॥ ਗੁਰ ਮਨੂਆ ਕਹ ਕਹਿਯੋ ਸੁਧਾਰੀ ॥\
ਜੇ ਜੇ ਚਰਿਤ ਪੁਰਾਤਨ ਲਹੇ ॥ ਤੇ ਤੇ ਅਬ ਚਹੀਅਤ ਹੈ ਕਹੇ ॥੬॥

When Mansa thought of mercy in her heart,[^4]\
she spoke to the guru, the mind, and set it right:\
"All the deeds of old that you have come to know,\
every one of them should now be told." (6)

ਸਰਬ ਕਾਲ ਕਰਣਾ ਤਬ ਭਰੇ ॥ ਸੇਵਕ ਜਾਨ ਦਯਾ ਰਸ ਢਰੇ ॥\
ਜੋ ਜੋ ਜਨਮ ਪੁਰਬਲੋ ਭਯੋ ॥ ਸੋ ਸੋ ਸਭ ਸਿਮਰਣ ਕਰ ਦਯੋ ॥੭॥

Then Sarab Kal was filled with compassion\
and, knowing his servant, poured out the nectar of mercy;\
every former birth there had been,\
he brought it all back to memory.[^5] (7)

ਮੋ ਕੌ ਇਤੀ ਹੁਤੀ ਕਹ ਸੁੱਧੰ ॥ ਜਸ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਦਈ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਕਰਿ ਬੁੱਧੰ ॥\
ਸਰਬ ਕਾਲ ਤਬ ਭਏ ਦਇਆਲਾ ॥ ਲੋਹ ਰਛ ਹਮ ਕੋ ਸਬ ਕਾਲਾ ॥੮॥

Where did I have such awareness\
as Prabhu gave me in his mercy?\
Then Sarab Kal grew merciful,\
and the Iron[^6] protects us at all times. (8)

ਸਰਬ ਕਾਲ ਰੱਛਾ ਸਬ ਕਾਲ ॥ ਲੋਹ ਰੱਛ ਸਰਬਦਾ ਬਿਸਾਲ ॥\
ਢੀਠ ਭਯੋ ਤਵ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾ ਲਖਾਈ ॥ ਐਂਡੋ ਫਿਰੋ ਸਭਨ ਭਯੋ ਰਾਈ ॥੯॥

Sarab Kal protects at all times;\
the mighty Iron protects for ever;\
having seen your mercy I have grown bold,\
and I go about with my head high, as king over all.[^7] (9)

ਜਿਹ ਜਿਹ ਬਿਧ ਜਨਮਨ ਸੁਧਿ ਆਈ ॥ ਤਿਮ ਤਿਮ ਕਹੇ ਗਿਰੰਥ ਬਨਾਈ ॥\
ਪ੍ਰਥਮੇ ਸਤਿਜੁਗ ਜਿਹ ਬਿਧਿ ਲਹਾ ॥ ਪ੍ਰਥਮੇ ਦੇਬਿ ਚਰਿਤ੍ਰ ਕੋ ਕਹਾ ॥੧੦॥

In whatever way the memory of those births came back,\
in just that way I have told them, making them into a book;\
first, as I came to know the Satyuga,[^8]\
I first told the deeds of the Goddess. (10)

ਪਹਿਲੇ ਚੰਡੀ ਚਰਿਤ੍ਰ ਬਨਾਯੋ ॥ ਨਖ ਸਿਖ ਤੇ ਕ੍ਰਮ ਭਾਖ ਸੁਨਾਯੋ ॥\
ਛੋਰ ਕਥਾ ਤਬ ਪ੍ਰਥਮ ਸੁਨਾਈ ॥ ਅਬ ਚਾਹਤ ਫਿਰਿ ਕਰੋਂ ਬਡਾਈ ॥੧੧॥

First I made the Chandi Charitra[^9]\
and told it in order, from head to toe;\
at first I told the story in short,\
and now I wish to sing its praise again. (11)

---
ਇਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਬਚਿਤ੍ਰ ਨਾਟਕ ਗ੍ਰੰਥੇ ਸਰਬ ਕਾਲ ਕੀ ਬੇਨਤੀ ਬਰਨਨੰ ਨਾਮ ਚੌਦਸਮੋ ਧਿਆਇ ਸਮਾਪਤ ਮਸਤੁ ਸੁਭ ਮਸਤੁ ॥੧੪॥ ਅਫਜੂ ॥੪੭੧॥

*Here ends the fourteenth chapter of the holy book Bachittar Natak, called the Account of the Prayer to Sarab Kal. All is true, all is blessed. Chapter 14; verses so far, 471.*

Sri Dasam Granth, Bachittar Natak, Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji. Gurmukhi text of record from the Shabad OS database, Sri Dasam Granth, section 54. ### Notes

[^1]: Sarab Kal (ਸਰਬ ਕਾਲ), Kal over all: the Lord as all-conquering Time, the death of all, the name the Bachittar Natak gives the Supreme Being; Narain Singh reads Vahiguru.
[^2]: Kalika (ਕਾਲਿਕਾ), the goddess Kali. Steek: Prabhu has no body or form; he is pure Name and power, the god of gods and the death of death, and that power is the mother. Narain Singh: the great power of Akal Purakh.
[^3]: Mansa (ਮਨਸਾ), intention, the will of the mind. Narain Singh reads the pure mind as the guru and good understanding as the mother.
[^4]: Steek: when the will runs after things it becomes a bond, but when it turns to thoughts of mercy, it sets the mind straight as a guru does. The Steek cites Gurbani to the same effect: hope and desire bind the self-willed, while for the one who follows the Guru they become a way to awakening.
[^5]: Steek: this shows that Guru Gobind Singh saw past, present and future, and knew earlier and later births.
[^6]: The Iron (ਲੋਹ), the Lord in the form of iron and the sword, as in the name Sarab Loh; Narain Singh reads the Lord of light.
[^7]: Narain Singh: by your mercy I have become so fearless that I go about unafraid, as king over all.
[^8]: Satyuga, the first and truest of the four ages.
[^9]: Chandi Charitra, the Dasam Granth's account of the goddess Chandi; Narain Singh reads that others had told the story before, and the Guru tells it from beginning to end. The Dasam Granth holds two Chandi Charitras, the shorter and the longer.

# Panth Prakash, Episode 42

## The Episode of the Ramraias and the Bhujangis

### (rāmraīāṅ dī ghuṛāṇī vikhe sudhāī: The Ramraias are punished at Ghudani)

>**Questions**
>
>1. Bulaka Singh's complaint?
>2. Banda's response?

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਬੁਲਾਕਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਇਕ ਸਿੰਘ ਹੁਤੋ ਰਹਿ ਆਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਪਾਸ ॥\
ਹਾਥ ਜੋੜ ਠਾਂਢੋ ਭਯੋ ਕਰ ਬੰਦੇ ਪੈ ਅਰਦਾਸ ॥੧॥

There was a Singh named Bulaka Singh, who lived in the company of Ali Singh, staying at his side;\
he stood up before Banda with his hands joined, and made his ardās,[^1] his humble plea, to him. (1)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਪਾਇਲ ਪਾਸ ਘੁਡਾਣੀ ਗ੍ਰਾਮ ॥ ਤਹਾਂ ਬਹੁਤ ਹੈਂ ਖਤ੍ਰੀਅਨ ਧਾਮ ॥\
ਰਾਮਰਯਨ ਕੇ ਮਸੰਦ ਕਹਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਕਰੈਂ ਮਸਕਰੀ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਖਿਝਾਵੈਂ ॥੨॥

"Near the town of Payal[^2] there is a village by the name of Ghudani,\
and a great many households of Khatris[^3] have their homes there;\
they call themselves the masands[^4] of the Ramraias,[^5]\
and they mock the Khalsa and keep goading the Singhs to anger. (2)

ਮੈਂ ਊਹਾਂ ਸ਼ਬਦ ਚੌਂਕੀ ਠਈ ॥ ਬੋਲੋ ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਅਰਦਾਸ ਯੌਂ ਕਹੀ ॥\
ਸੁਨਤ ਦੁਤਾਰੋ ਉਨ ਭੰਨ ਦਯੋ ॥ ਮੁਖ ਤੇ ਵਾਕ ਤਿਨ ਖੋਟੇ ਕਹਯੋ ॥੩॥

"I held a sitting of shabad singing[^6] there among them,\
and when I said the ardās, I called out like this: 'Say, Vahiguru';\
as soon as they heard it, they smashed my two-stringed lute[^7] to pieces,\
and from their mouths they poured out vile and wicked words. (3)

ਔ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੀ ਨਿੰਦਯਾ ਕਰੀ ॥ ਹਮ ਕੌਂ ਨਹਿਂ ਵਹਿ ਬਿਸਰਤ ਘਰੀ ॥\
ਥੀ ਹਮ ਕੌ ਬਹੁ ਕੀਨੀ ਮਾਰ ॥ ਸੋ ਤੋ ਹਮਨੈ ਦਈ ਵਿਸਾਰ ॥੪॥

"And they slandered the Satguru, speaking of him with scorn;\
that hour I can never forget, not for a single moment;\
they beat me badly as well, and gave me many blows,\
but that I have put out of my mind and let it go. (4)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਪੁਤ੍ਰ ਮੁਏ ਆਪੈ ਮੁਯੋ ਅਬ ਮੁਯੋ ਨ ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਬੋਲ ॥\
ਯੋ ਕਹਿ ਦੁਤਾਰੋ ਭੰਨਯੋ ਤੌ ਮਾਰਯੋ ਕੇਸ ਮੁਹਿ ਖੋਲ ॥੫॥

"'His sons are dead, and he himself is dead, and still this cry of Vahiguru has not died,' they said;\
and saying this, they smashed my lute to pieces, and they tore my hair loose and beat me. (5)

ਤੁਮ ਜੱਟਨ ਹੁਇ ਏਕਠੇ ਲਯੋ ਥੋ ਗੁਰੂ ਬਣਾਇ ॥\
ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਸਿੰਘ ਕਹੈਂ ਕਹੈਂ ਸਚੋ ਪਾਤਿਸ਼ਾਹਿ ॥੬॥

"'You Jatts[^8] banded together, all of you, and set him up and made a Guru out of him;\
you go about saying Gobind, Gobind Singh, and calling him the True Patshah.'" (6)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਯੋ ਸੁਨ ਬੰਦੇ ਗੁੱਸਾ ਕੀਯੋ ॥ ਪਾਇਲ ਠਾਣਾ ਤਿਸ ਲਿਖ ਦੀਯੋ ॥\
ਜਾ ਕਰ ਤਿਸ ਤੇ ਬਦਲੇ ਲੇਹੁ ॥ ਮਾਰ ਲੂਟ ਤਿਸ ਪਿੰਡ ਫੜੇਹੁ ॥੭॥

Hearing this, Banda's anger blazed up against them,\
and he wrote out an order giving Bulaka Singh the post[^9] at Payal:\
"Go, and take your revenge on them for what they have done;\
strike them down, plunder them, and seize hold of that village." (7)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਪਾਇਲ ਠਾਣਾ ਪਾਇਕੈ ਲੀਨੇ ਮਸੰਦ ਫੜਾਇ ॥\
ਕਿਛ ਕੂਟੇ ਸਭ ਲੁੁੱਟ ਲਏ ਦੀਨੇ ਪਿੰਡੋਂ ਕਢਾਇ ॥੮॥

Taking over the post at Payal, Bulaka Singh had the masands seized and dragged in;\
some of them were beaten, all of them were plundered of everything, and they were driven out of their village. (8)

### Notes

[^1]: ardās: the Sikh's prayer of petition, here a plea laid before Banda.
[^2]: Payal: a town in Ludhiana district, with the village of Ghudani close by.
[^3]: Khatris: the trading and writing caste to which the Gurus belonged.
[^4]: masands: the agents who once collected the offerings of the Sikhs for the Gurus; Guru Gobind Singh did away with them.
[^5]: Ramraias: the followers of Ram Rai, the elder son of Guru Har Rai, whom his father cast out; his seat was at Dehradun. The Bhujangis of the title are the young Singhs of the Khalsa.
[^6]: a sitting of shabad singing: the poet's shabad chauṅkī, a session of kīrtan. BSS: he held it at the hour before dawn.
[^7]: two-stringed lute: the poet's dutārā.
[^8]: Jatts: the great farming caste of the Punjab. BSS: the masands were Khatris, and would not accept Guru Gobind Singh as Guru at all.
[^9]: post: the poet's ṭhāṇā, a post of armed men that held the country and kept order. BSS: Banda gave Bulaka Singh the command of the post at Payal, under which Ghudani fell. Kulwant Singh: Banda sent written orders to the officer at Payal.

# Panth Prakash, Episode 39

## The Episode of the Preparations on Both Sides

### (phaṛhe majhaul su laiṇge...: The Majhails will win the victory)

>**Questions**
>
>1. Banda's earlier prediction?
>2. Name Banda's 2 aims, in order.

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਬਜੀਰੇ ਮਨ ਅਤਿ ਚਿੰਤਾ ਧਾਰੀ ॥ ਨਜੀਕ ਆਈ ਹਮ ਮਰਨੇ ਵਾਰੀ ॥\
ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੇ ਦਿਲ ਭਈ ਵਧਾਈ ॥ ਪਹਿਲੀ ਫਤੇ ਖਾਲਸੇ ਪਾਈ ॥੧॥

Wazira's[^1] mind was filled with heavy worry and with dread:\
*My own turn to die has now come very close to me.*\
But in the hearts of the Singhs there was rejoicing and gladness,\
for this was the first victory that the Khalsa had won. (1)

ਡੇਰਾ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਊਹਾਂ ਲਗਾਯੋ ॥ ਤਬ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਨੇ ਭੋਜਨ ਪਾਯੋ ॥\
ਭੋਜਨ ਪਾਇ ਕੀਯੋ ਫਿਰਿ ਕੂਚੁ ॥ ਬੰਦਾ ਮਿਲਿਓ ਅਗੈ ਪਹੂਚ ॥੨॥

The Singhs pitched their camp right there on the field of battle,\
and then the Singhs sat down together and ate their food;\
when they had eaten, they struck their camp and marched on again,\
and further ahead along the road, Banda came and met them. (2)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਬੰਦੈ ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮੈਂ ਥੋ ਕਹਯੋ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਊਚ ਸੁਨਾਇ ॥\
ਮਝੈਲ ਫਤੇ ਸੁ ਲੈਣਗੇ ਮਲੇਰੀਅਨ ਉੱਤੇ ਪਾਇ ॥੩॥

Banda had said this beforehand, announcing it loudly so that all the Singhs could hear him:\
"The Majhails[^2] will win the victory, and they will come out on top of the men of Maler."[^3] (3)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਬੰਦੈ ਆਦਰ ਬਹੁ ਮਝੈਲਨ ਕੀਯਾ ॥ ਬਹੁਤ ਖਰਚ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੋ ਦੀਯਾ ॥\
ਸਿੰਘਨ ਫਤੇ ਸੁ ਗੁਰੂ ਬੁਲਾਈ ॥ ਹੁਏ ਰਾਜੀ ਮਿਲਿ ਭਾਈਅਨ ਭਾਈ ॥੪॥

Banda showed the Majhails great honour and welcomed them warmly,\
and he gave the Singhs a great deal of money for their needs;\
the Singhs called out the Guru's Fateh,[^4] greeting one another,\
and brothers met with brothers, and all of them were glad of heart. (4)

ਬੰਦੈ ਕੇ ਦਿਲ ਭਯੋ ਉਛਾਹਿ ॥ ਕਹਯੋ ਬੰਦੇ ਕਰੌ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਕੜਾਹੁ ॥\
ਜੋ ਤੁਮ ਔ ਗੁਰ ਹਮਹਿ ਫੁਰਮਾਯਾ ॥ ਕਰੋ ਸੋਈ ਅਬ ਵਖਤ ਹੈ ਆਯਾ ॥੫॥

Banda's heart rose high with eagerness and with joy,\
and Banda said, "Make kaṛāh prashad[^5] in the Satguru's name;\
what the Guru commanded, both to all of you and to me,\
do that now, for the time to do it has come at last. (5)

ਅਬ ਤੌ ਲਈਐ ਬਜੀਰੈ ਮਾਰ ॥ ਫੇਰ ਕਰੈਂਗੇ ਪਰਬਤ ਕਾਰ ॥\
ਸਿੰਘਨ ਲੀਨੀ ਮਾਨ ਸਲਾਹਿ ॥ ਮਝੈਲਨ ਕਿਯੋ ਡੇਰਾ ਇਕ ਦਾਇ ॥੬॥

"Now let us strike Wazira down and kill him first of all,\
and after that we shall turn to the work that waits in the hills."\
The Singhs accepted this counsel and agreed to do it,\
and the Majhails pitched their camp on one side, by themselves. (6)

ਤਬ ਭਯੋ ਪ੍ਰਾਤ ਸੁ ਬੰਦਾ ਚੜ੍ਹਾ ॥ ਮਾਰ ਛੱਤ ਫਿਰ ਡੇਰਾ ਕਰਾ ॥\
ਲੁਟਯੋ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਤੌ ਭੁੱਖ ਮਿਟਾਈ ॥ ਤੁਰਕ ਕਟੇ ਲਏ ਹਿੰਦੂ ਬਚਾਈ ॥੭॥

Then, when the morning came, Banda rode out to attack,\
struck the town of Chhat,[^6] and then made his camp beyond it;\
he plundered the town, and so the hunger of his men was satisfied;\
the Turks[^7] there were cut down, but the Hindus were spared. (7)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਬੰਦੈ ਡੇਰਾ ਕੂਚ ਕਰ ਅਗੈ ਦੀਨੋਂ ਪਾਇ ॥\
ਉਚੀ ਰੋੜੀ ਦੇਖਕੈ ਬੈਠੋ ਆਸਨ ਲਾਇ ॥੮॥

Then Banda struck his camp and moved on, and pitched it once again further ahead along the road,\
and seeing a high mound of earth and stones, he went and sat down upon it, taking his seat there as a holy man does. (8)

### Notes

[^1]: Wazira: the poet's scornful short form for Wazir Khan, the nawab of Sirhind.
[^2]: Majhails: the Singhs of the Majha, the country between the Beas and the Ravi, around Amritsar and Lahore.
[^3]: men of Maler: the Pathans of Malerkotla (Episode 38).
[^4]: Fateh: the Khalsa's greeting, Vahiguru ji ka Khalsa, Vahiguru ji ki Fateh (the Khalsa belongs to Vahiguru, the victory belongs to Vahiguru).
[^5]: kaṛāh prashad: the sacred pudding of flour, ghee and sugar, made and shared after the ardās. BSS: Banda ordered great pans of it made, the ardās offered, and the attack made ready.
[^6]: Chhat: a small town beside Banur, south of present-day Chandigarh.
[^7]: Turks: the poet's word for the Muslim rulers and their armies, and for Muslims generally.

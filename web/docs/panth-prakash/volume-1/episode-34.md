# Panth Prakash, Episode 34

## The Episode of the Singhs of Salaudi

### (ālī siṅgh mālī siṅgh dā bande nūṅ milṇā: Ali Singh and Mali Singh meet Banda)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਸ੍ਰਿਹੰਦ ਢਿਗ ਇਕ ਸਿਲੌਦੀ ਗ੍ਰਾਮ ॥ ਹੁਤੇ ਉਹਾਂ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੇ ਧਾਮ ॥\
ਤਿਨ ਮੈਂ ਇਕ ਆਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਨਾਮ ॥ ਚਾਕ੍ਰੀ ਕਰਤ ਵਜ਼ੀਰੇ ਧਾਮ ॥੧॥

Close to the city of Sirhind there was a village by the name of Salaudi,[^1]\
where the Singhs of the Guru had their homes.\
Among them was a Singh by the name of Ali Singh,\
who took his pay in the service of Wazira,[^2] the nawab of Sirhind, and worked in his household. (1)

ਨਬਾਬ ਸ੍ਰਿਹੰਦੀ ਉਸੇ ਬੁਲਾਯੋ ॥ ਸੱਦ ਪੁਛਯੋ ਤੁਮ ਗੁਰ ਔਰ ਆਯੋ ॥\
ਤੁਮਕੋ ਚਾਹੀਅਤ ਉਸ ਪਹਿ ਜਾਓ ॥ ਉਸਕੋ ਤੁਮ ਈਹਾਂ ਲੈ ਆਓ ॥੨॥

One day the nawab of Sirhind sent for Ali Singh;\
he summoned him to his court and asked him, "Has another Guru of yours come into the land?\
Then you ought to go to him and join his camp;\
go, then, and bring this Guru of yours here before me. (2)

ਈਹਾਂ ਆਇ ਦੇਊਂ ਅਲਖ ਮਿਟਾਇ ॥ ਰਲੈ ਪੁਤ੍ਰਨ ਗੁਰ ਸ਼ਿਤਾਬੀ ਜਾਇ ॥\
ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕਹੀ ਜਿ ਹੁਇ ਗੁਰ ਪੂਰਾ ॥ ਤੌ ਆਪੈ ਆਉਗੁ ਕਯਾ ਤੁਮ ਪੈ ਜ਼ਰੂਰਾ ॥

"Let him only come here, and I shall wipe him out so that no trace of him is left,\
and I shall send him quickly to join the Guru's sons in death."\
The Singhs answered him, "If he is truly the perfect Guru,\
he will come to you of his own accord; why should we need to bring him?" (3)

ਫਿਰ ਉਨ ਕਹਯੋ ਤੁਮ ਕੈਦੈ ਪਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਤਬ ਛੋਡੈਂ ਜਬ ਹਮੈਂ ਦਿਖਾਵੈਂ ॥\
ਨਹਿ ਆਵੈ ਮੈਂ ਤੁਮ ਦੇਉਂਗੁ ਮਾਰ ॥ ਬੇੜੀ ਪਾਈ ਇਤੈ ਕਰਾਰ ॥੪॥

Then the nawab said, "Until then I shall keep you both in prison,\
and I will set you free only when you show me this Guru of yours.\
And if he does not come, then I shall have you put to death."\
With this vow he had shackles put upon the feet of the two Singhs. (4)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਬੇੜੀ ਮੈਂ ਪਗ ਨਹਿਂ ਰਹੈ ਤੁਰਕ ਸੁ ਤੰਗ ਕਰਾਇ ॥\
ਆਇ ਬੀਰ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕਹੈ ਤੁਮ ਤੁਰੋ ਬੰਦੇ ਕੇ ਦਾਇ ॥੫॥

But the shackles would not stay on their feet; however tight the Turks[^3] made them, their feet slipped free again;\
and then spirits[^4] came to the Singhs and told them, "Go now; set out for Banda's camp." (5)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤਬ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਕੋ ਮਨ ਪਤਿਆਯਾ ॥ ਕਹਿ ਨਬਾਬ ਤੇ ਹਿਸਾਬ ਕਰਾਯਾ ॥\
ਨਬਾਬ ਕਹੀ ਹਮ ਹਸ ਕਰ ਕਹੀ ॥ ਅਬ ਨਹਿ ਰਹੈਂ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਉਨ ਕਹੀ ॥੬॥

Then the Singhs were convinced in their hearts (of Banda's power);\
they went to the nawab and told him to settle their account and pay them what he owed.\
The nawab said, "I only said those things in jest."\
But the Singhs told him plainly, "We will not stay in your service now." (6)

ਤੁਹੀਂ ਕਹੈ ਇਹ ਮਰਨੋ ਡਰੈ ॥ ਊਹਾਂ ਹੀ ਗਯੋ ਹਮੈ ਅਬ ਸਰੈ ॥\
ਫੇਰ ਮਿਲੈਂ ਤਬ ਗੁਰ ਕੇ ਸਾਥ ॥ ਗੁਰ ਫਤੇ ਬੁਲਾਈ ਸਿਰ ਧਰ ਹਾਥ ॥੭॥

"It was you yourself who said that we were afraid to die;\
now nothing will do for us but to go and join him there.\
We shall meet you again, then, when we come back with the Guru."\
And raising their joined hands above their heads, they called out the Guru's Fateh[^5] and left him. (7)

ਸੇਹਰ ਖੰਡੇ ਰਾਹ ਪੁਛ ਲਯੋ ॥ ਖਖਰੋਡ ਪਰਗਣੇ ਮੈਂ ਥੋ ਭਯੋ ॥\
ਦਿੱਲੀ ਤੇ ਵਹਿ ਤੀਹ ਕੁ ਕੋਸ ॥ ਤੁਰੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਦਿਲ ਕਰ ਇਮ ਹੋਸ ॥੮॥

They asked the way to Sehar Khand,[^6] where Banda had made his camp,\
a village that lay in the district of Khakhrod,[^7]\
some thirty kos[^8] or so from Delhi;\
and the Singhs set out on that road, turning a thought over in their hearts. (8)

ਬੰਦੇ ਕੀ ਪਰਤੱਗਯਾ ਲਯੈ ॥ ਬਜੀਰੈ ਮਾਰੈ ਇਮ ਚਿੱਤ ਪਰਤਯੈ ॥\
ਜੋ ਹਮ ਕੌ ਵਹੁ ਲੈਹੇ ਨਾਮ ॥ ਤਬ ਸਾਰੈਗੋ ਸਭ ਗੁਰ ਕਾਮ ॥੯॥

*We will put Banda to the test before we trust him,*\
*and so our hearts will know for certain whether he will kill Wazira:*\
*if he greets us by our own names when we arrive,*\
*then we shall know that the Guru will carry all his work through.* (9)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਦਸ ਭੁਜੰਗੀ ਥੇ ਤੁਰੇ ਆਲੀ ਮਾਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਸਾਥ ॥\
ਜਾਇ ਦੂਰਉਂ ਬੰਦੈ ਦੇਖਕੈ ਧਰਯੋ ਮਥੇ ਪਰ ਹਾਥ ॥੧੦॥

Ten young bhujaṅgī,[^9] fighting Singhs, set out on the road with Ali Singh and his brother Mali Singh;\
and when they drew near and saw Banda in the distance, they raised their hands to their foreheads in greeting. (10)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਕੀ ਫਤੇ ਬੁਲਾਈ ॥ ਅਕਾਲ ਅਕਾਲ ਕੀ ਵਾਜ ਸੁਣਾਈ ॥\
ਅਗੈ ਬੰਦੈ ਯੋ ਬਚਨ ਸੁਣਾਏ ॥ ਚਾਹਤ ਅਪਨੇ ਨਾਮ ਬਤਾਏ ॥੧੧॥

They called out the Fateh of Vahiguru, as the Khalsa greets the Khalsa,\
and raised the great cry of "Akal, Akal."[^10]\
Then Banda answered their greeting with these words:\
"You have come here wanting to hear your own names spoken by me. (11)

ਆਲੀ ਮਾਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਭਸੌੜੀਏ ਆਏ ॥ ਕਰਾਮਾਤ ਘਰੋਂ ਢੂੰਢਤ ਧਾਏ ॥\
ਕਰਾਮਾਤ ਉਸ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਮਾਹੀਂ ॥ ਭੇਜਣ ਵਾਰੋ ਲਾਜ ਰਖਾਹੀਂ ॥੧੨॥

"Ali Singh and Mali Singh have come, and with them the Singhs of Bhasaur;[^11]\
you set out from your homes to go looking for a miracle in me.\
But the miracle is not mine; it lives in that Satguru alone,\
and the One who sent me here will keep my honour." (12)

ਯੌ ਕਹਿ ਉਨ ਕੋ ਨਾਮ ਸੁਨਾਯੋ ॥ ਤੌ ਉਨ ਮਨ ਠੀਕ ਪਤਿਆਯੋ ॥\
ਮਾਲੀ ਆਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਬੰਦੈ ਕਹੀ ॥ ਉਨ ਮੈਂ ਤੁਮ ਮੈਂ ਭੇਦੋ ਨਹੀਂ ॥੧੩॥

As he said this, he spoke each one of their names aloud,\
and then their minds were fully satisfied, and all their doubt was gone.\
Then Mali Singh and Ali Singh said to Banda,\
"There is no difference at all between the Guru and you. (13)

ਅਬਹ ਕਾਹੇ ਕੋ ਦੇਰ ਲਗਾਈ ॥ ਕਰੋ ਕਾਰ ਕਰਨ ਆਇ ਜਾਹੀ ॥\
ਫਿਰ ਬੰਦੇ ਨੈ ਉਨ ਸੋਂ ਕਹੀ ॥ ਸਿੰਘ ਆਵਤ ਹੈਂ ਮਿਲਨੇ ਕਈ ॥੧੪॥

"Why then do you delay now, and why have you not yet struck?\
Do the work you came here to do, the work the Guru gave you."\
Then Banda answered them and said,\
"Many more Singhs are on the road, coming to join us here. (14)

ਉਨ ਕੌ ਚਹਿਯੈ ਸੰਗ ਰਲਾਏਂ ॥ ਇਮ ਕਰਿਕੈ ਹਮ ਦੇਰ ਲਗਾਏਂ ॥\
ਜਿਨ ਪੈ ਅਗੇ ਚਿਠੈ ਥੇ ਗਏ ॥ ਸੋ ਅਬ ਆਵਤ ਦੂਰੋਂ ਧਏ ॥੧੫॥

"It is right that we should take them in with us before we strike;\
that is the only reason we have waited here so long.\
All those to whom our letters went out before\
are hurrying toward us now from far away. (15)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਉਨ ਕੈ ਦਿਲ ਕੀ ਹਮ ਬੁਝੈਂ ਵੈ ਚਹੈਂ ਫਤੇ ਲਈ ਆਪ ॥\
ਦੱਖਣ ਪੂਰਬ ਪੱਛਮੋਂ ਪਰਬਤਿ ਆਏ ਥਾਪ ॥੧੬॥

"We know what is in their hearts: each one of them wants to win this victory with his own hand;\
from the south and the east and the west, and down from the hills, they have set out with their minds made up." (16)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤੌ ਲੌ ਮਾਲਵਯੋਂ ਖਾਲਸੋ ਆਯੋ ॥ ਫਤੇ ਸਿੰਘ ਭਾਈ ਕਾ ਲਯਾਯੋ ॥\
ਕਰਮ ਧਰਮ ਸਿੰਘ ਰੂਪੇ ਵਾਰੇ ॥ ਆਏ ਤੇ ਭੀ ਗੁਰੂ ਪਿਆਰੇ ॥੧੭॥

While he was still speaking, the Khalsa of the Malwa[^12] arrived;\
Fateh Singh of Bhai Ka[^13] was the one who led them in,\
and Karam Singh and Dharam Singh of Rupa[^14] came as well,\
they too beloved of the Guru, and devoted Singhs. (17)

ਰਾਮਾ ਤਿਲੋਕਾ ਆਪ ਨ ਆਏ ॥ ਦੇਕੇ ਖਰਚ ਉਨ ਭੇਜੇ ਪਰਾਏ ॥\
ਮਹਿਰਾਜ ਵਾਲੇ ਕਿਛ ਲੁੱਟਣ ਆਏ ॥ ਫੂਲ ਪੁਤ੍ਰਨ ਤੇ ਖਰਚ ਲੈ ਧਾਏ ॥੧੮॥

Rama and Tiloka,[^15] the sons of Phul, did not come themselves;\
instead they paid the costs of other men and sent them in their place.\
Some men of Mehraj[^16] came as well, but they came looking for plunder,\
hurrying in on the money they had been given by the sons of Phul. (18)

ਕੋਟ ਕਪੂਰੀਏ ਆਏ ਨ ਕੋਈ ॥ ਇਮ ਰਹਯੋ ਕਪੂਰੋ ਖਾਲੀ ਤੋਈ ॥੧੯॥

Not a single man came to Banda from Kot Kapura,[^17]\
and so Kapura (who had failed the Guru once before) was left with nothing again. (19)

Kulwant Singh is named in full in the notes; Baba Santa Singh Akali is BSS. Cite as Panth Prakash 34.4.*

### Notes

[^1]: Salaudi: the poet writes Silaudi in the verse and Salaudi in the title; a village near Sirhind. BSS: it is still called Salaudi of the Singhs, and Baba Banta Singh Nihang is of this family.
[^2]: Wazira: the poet's scornful short form (also Bajira) for Wazir Khan, the nawab of Sirhind, who had the Guru's two younger sons put to death.
[^3]: Turks: the poet's word for the Muslim rulers and their men.
[^4]: spirits: the poet's bīr, spirit servants. BSS: they were Banda's; the Turks could not see them, but they showed themselves to the Singhs.
[^5]: Fateh: the Khalsa greeting, Vahiguru ji ka Khalsa, Vahiguru ji ki Fateh (the Khalsa is Vahiguru's; the victory is Vahiguru's).
[^6]: Sehar Khand: the poet's Sehar Khanda, the village in the Bangar where Banda had camped (Episode 33).
[^7]: Khakhrod: as printed; probably Kharkhauda, near Sonipat in Haryana (unverified).
[^8]: kos: about 3 km.
[^9]: bhujaṅgī: young Singhs (the word means young serpents).
[^10]: Akal: the Timeless One. BSS: they raised the cry of Sat Sri Akal.
[^11]: Bhasaur: a village of the Malwa. BSS: men of Bhasaur came with the two brothers; Kulwant Singh reads the brothers themselves as men of Bhasaur.
[^12]: the Malwa: the country south of the Satluj.
[^13]: Bhai Ka: as printed. Kulwant Singh reads a village, Bhaika; BSS: Bhai ke Chakk. BSS also says Fateh Singh led the fighting at Samana and was put over the army (Episode 35).
[^14]: Rupa: the village of Bhai Rupa in the Malwa. Kulwant Singh reads one man, Karam Singh.
[^15]: Rama and Tiloka: Ram Singh and Tilok Singh, sons of Phul, the Malwa chief from whom the houses of Patiala, Nabha and Jind descend. BSS: they held great estates, and sent other Singhs to the war with money for the road.
[^16]: Mehraj: the village of Phul's family, in the Malwa.
[^17]: Kot Kapura: a fort town of the Malwa, whose chief Kapura had refused Guru Gobind Singh shelter. BSS: Kapura had already drawn the Guru's curse, and now he let this chance pass as well.

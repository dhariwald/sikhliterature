# Panth Prakash, Episode 30

## The Khalsa's Ardās

### (jo tūṅ siṅghan khush rakhaiṅ tau rahaiṅ sadā tūṅ sukhi: If you keep the Singhs content, you shall live in peace forever)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਤਬੈ ਖਾਲਸੇ ਕਰੀ ਅਰਦਾਸ ॥ ਹਾਥ ਜੋੜ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪਾਸ ॥\
ਹਜੂਰੋਂ ਪਤਿਸ਼ਾਹੀ ਖਾਲਸੇ ਦਈ ॥ ਹਜੂਰੋਂ ਮੁਖੋਂ ਕਈ ਵਾਰ ਬਖ਼ਸ਼ਈ ॥੧॥

Then the Khalsa made ardās[^1]\
with hands joined before the Sri Satguru:\
"From your presence the Patshahi was given to the Khalsa;\
from your own lips you have granted it many times. (1)

ਖਾਲਸੋ ਮਾਰੈ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਤੁਮ ਤਾਣੈ ॥ ਤੁਰਕ ਗਏ ਹਮ ਦਰਗਾਹੋਂ ਜਾਣੈ ॥\
ਜੌ ਬੰਦਾ ਪਾਤਸ਼ਾਹੀ ਚਾਹੈ ॥ ਤਬ ਹਮ ਚਲੈ ਨ ਇਨ ਕੈ ਰਾਹੈ ॥੨॥

"By your strength the Khalsa will strike down the Turks;[^2]\
we know the Turks are finished in the Court of God.\
If Banda[^3] should want the Patshahi,\
then we will not walk in his way." (2)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸੁਨੀਓ ਖੁਸ਼ ਭਏ ਸੋਊ ਬੰਦੇ ਕਹਿ ਦੀਨ ॥\
ਤੂੰ ਨਾ ਮੰਨੈ ਜਬ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਤਬ ਹੁਵੈਗੀ ਤੁਮਰੀ ਹੀਨ ॥੩॥

The Satguru heard it and was glad, and told Banda the same:\
"When you do not heed the Khalsa, then you shall be brought low. (3)

ਜੋ ਤੂੰ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਖੁਸ਼ ਰਖੈਂ ਤੌ ਰਹੈਂ ਸਦਾ ਤੂੰ ਸੁੱਖਿ ॥\
ਜੇ ਤੂੰ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਦੁਖ ਦੇਵੈਂ ਤੌ ਤੂੰ ਪਾਵੈਂ ਦੁੱਖ ॥੪॥

"If you keep the Singhs content, you shall live in peace forever;\
if you bring the Singhs pain, then pain shall be yours." (4)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਤਬ ਬੰਦੈ ਮਨਜੂਰ ਸੁ ਕਰੀ ॥ ਤੁਰਯੋ ਖਾਲਸੋ ਉਸ ਹੀ ਘਰੀ ॥\
ਚਿੱਠੇ ਹਜ਼ੂਰੀ ਲਏ ਲਿਖਾਇ ॥ ਮੱਧ ਪੰਜਾਬ ਜੁ ਸਿੰਘ ਰਹਾਇਂ ॥੫॥

Then Banda accepted it,\
and the Khalsa set out that very hour.\
Banda had letters written from the Guru's presence\
to the Singhs who lived in the heart of the Punjab.[^4] (5)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਜੋ ਆਇ ਮਿਲੈ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਉਹ ਸੁਖ ਸਾਰਾ ਲੇਹਿ ॥\
ਜੋ ਆਇ ਰਲੈ ਨ ਖਾਲਸੈ, ਰੱਯਤ ਤਿਸੈ ਬਣੇਹੁ ॥੬॥

"Whoever comes and joins the Khalsa shall have every comfort;\
whoever does not come and join the Khalsa, make him your subject." (6)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਤਬ ਹੀ ਬੰਦੈ ਬੰਧੇ ਹਾਥਿ ॥ ਹਮ ਮਾਨੇਗੇ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਬਾਤ ॥\
ਭੀੜ ਪੜੈ ਜਹਿਂ ਹਮ ਪਰ ਆਇ ॥ ਤਹ ਤੁਮ ਕਰਨੀ ਬਨਗੁ ਸਹਾਇ ॥੭॥

Then Banda joined his hands:\
"I will heed the word of the Singhs.\
Wherever hard straits fall upon me,\
there you must come to my help. (7)

ਰਿੱਧਿ ਸਿੱਧਿ ਕਿਛ ਹਮ ਭੀ ਦੱਯੈ ॥ ਜਿਮ ਪਤਿਆਰੋ ਜਗ ਮੈਂ ਪੱਯੈ ॥\
ਜੌ ਮੈਂ ਕਰੋਂ ਸਿੰਘਨ ਸੋਂ ਧੋਹੁ ॥ ਤੌ ਮੈਂ ਲਯੋ ਅਗਲੀ ਖੋਹੁ ॥੮॥

"Give me too some riches and some powers,[^5]\
so that the world will put its trust in me.\
If ever I betray the Singhs,\
then strip me even of what I had before." (8)

ਤਿਸ ਕੌ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਯੌ ਪੁਨ ਕਹੀ ॥ ਸੌਂਪ ਸਭੀ ਹਮ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਦਈ ॥\
ਹਮਨੈ ਖਾਲਸੇ ਤੂੰ ਭੀ ਰਲਾਯਾ ॥ ਤੈਂ ਭੀ ਖਾਲਸਯੋ ਵੰਡਾ ਪਾਯਾ ॥੯॥

Then the Satguru said to him,\
"I have given everything over to the Khalsa.\
I have made you one with the Khalsa too;\
you too have your share from the Khalsa. (9)

ਪੰਜ ਸਿੰਘ ਲੈ ਕੇ ਤੂੰ ਸਾਥ ॥ ਕਰ ਅਰਦਾਸ ਪੂਰੀ ਹੋਇ ਬਾਤ ॥\
ਯੌ ਸੁਨ ਬੰਦੇ ਕਰੀ ਚੜ੍ਹਾਈ ॥ ਡੇਰਾ ਤੋਰਯੋ ਸਿਰਹੰਦੈ ਦਾਈ ॥੧੦॥

"Take five Singhs[^6] with you,\
make ardās, and your purpose will be fulfilled."\
Hearing this, Banda set out on his campaign\
and moved his camp toward Sirhind.[^7] (10)

Institute of Sikh Studies, Chandigarh, volume 1, episodes 1 to 81, with the English translation of
Kulwant Singh. Punjabi commentary from the Prachin Panth Prakash Steek, part 1, of Singh Sahib Baba
Santa Singh Akali, Jathedar of the 96 Krori Budha Dal. Both commentators are named in full in the
notes; the project docs abbreviate them KS and BSS. Cite as Panth Prakash 30.4.*

### Notes

[^1]: ardās: the Sikh prayer of petition, made standing with hands joined.
[^2]: Turks: the poet's word for the Muslim rulers and their armies, here the Mughals.
[^3]: Banda: Banda Singh, whom the Guru named and sent to the Punjab in Episode 29.
[^4]: the heart of the Punjab: Baba Santa Singh Akali names the Malwa, the Majha and the Doaba.
[^5]: riches and powers: the poet's riddhi and siddhi, prosperity and miraculous powers.
[^6]: five Singhs: in the Khalsa's practice five Singhs together stand for the Guru, as the Five Beloved did when the Khalsa was first made.
[^7]: Sirhind: the Mughal city where the Guru's two younger sons were put to death · Baba Santa Singh's account is that five leading Singhs went with Banda, and twenty-five more.

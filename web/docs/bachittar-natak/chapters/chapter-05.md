# Bachittar Natak, Chapter 5

## The Account of the Patshahis

ਨਰਾਜ ਛੰਦ ॥ *Naraj Chhand*

ਬਹੁਰ ਬਿਖਾਦ ਬਾਧਿਯੰ ॥\
ਕਿਨੀ ਨ ਤਾਹਿ ਸਾਧਿਯੰ ॥\
ਕਰੰਮ ਕਾਲ ਯੌਂ ਭਈ ॥\
ਸੁ ਭੂਮਿ ਬੰਸ ਤੇ ਗਈ ॥੧॥

Then strife took hold once more,\
and no one could settle it;\
so fate and time worked out\
that the land passed from the line (of the Bedis).[^1] (1)

ਦੋਹਰਾ ॥ *Dohra*

ਬਿਪ੍ਰ ਕਰਤ ਭਏ ਸੂਦ੍ਰ ਬ੍ਰਿਤਿ ਛਤ੍ਰੀ ਬੈਸਨ ਕਰਮ ॥\
ਬੈਸ ਕਰਤ ਭਏ ਛਤ੍ਰਿ ਬ੍ਰਿਤਿ ਸੂਦ੍ਰ ਸੁ ਦਿਜ ਕੋ ਧਰਮ ॥੨॥

Brahmins took up the work of Sudras, and Kshatriyas the work of Vaishyas;[^2]\
Vaishyas took up the Kshatriya's trade, and Sudras the dharam[^3] of the Brahmin. (2)

ਚੌਪਈ ॥ *Chaupai*

ਬੀਸ ਗਾਵ ਤਿਨ ਕੇ ਰਹਿ ਗਏ ॥ ਜਿਨ ਮੋ ਕਰਤ ਕ੍ਰਿਸਾਨੀ ਭਏ ॥\
ਬਹੁਤ ਕਾਲ ਇਹ ਭਾਂਤਿ ਬਿਤਾਯੋ ॥ ਜਨਮ ਸਮੈ ਨਾਨਕ ਕੋ ਆਯੋ ॥੩॥

Twenty villages were left to them,\
and there they lived by farming;\
a long time passed in this way,\
and then the hour came for Nanak to be born. (3)

ਦੋਹਰਾ ॥ *Dohra*

ਤਿਨ ਬੇਦੀਅਨ ਕੀ ਕੁਲ ਬਿਖੈ ਪ੍ਰਗਟੇ ਨਾਨਕ ਰਾਇ ॥\
ਸਭ ਸਿੱਖਨ ਕੋ ਸੁਖ ਦਏ ਜਹ ਤਹ ਭਏ ਸਹਾਇ ॥੪॥

In that family of the Bedis Nanak Rai[^4] appeared;\
he gave peace to all his Sikhs and stood by them wherever they were. (4)

ਚੌਪਈ ॥ *Chaupai*

ਤਿਨ ਇਹ ਕਲ ਮੋ ਧਰਮੁ ਚਲਾਯੋ ॥ ਸਭ ਸਾਧਨ ਕੋ ਰਾਹੁ ਬਤਾਯੋ ॥\
ਜੋ ਤਾਂ ਕੇ ਮਾਰਗ ਮਹਿ ਆਏ ॥ ਤੇ ਕਬਹੂੰ ਨਹੀ ਪਾਪ ਸੰਤਾਏ ॥੫॥

He set dharam going in this Kaliyuga[^5]\
and showed the way to all holy men;\
those who came onto his path\
were never troubled by sin. (5)

ਜੇ ਜੇ ਪੰਥ ਤਵਨ ਕੇ ਪਰੇ ॥ ਪਾਪ ਤਾਪ ਤਿਨ ਕੇ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਹਰੇ ॥\
ਦੂਖ ਭੂਖ ਕਬਹੂੰ ਨ ਸੰਤਾਏ ॥ ਜਾਲ ਕਾਲ ਕੇ ਬੀਚ ਨ ਆਏ ॥੬॥

All who joined his Panth,\
Prabhu[^6] took away their sins and their sufferings;\
pain and hunger never troubled them,\
and they never fell into the net of Kal.[^7] (6)

ਨਾਨਕ ਅੰਗਦ ਕੋ ਬਪੁ ਧਰਾ ॥ ਧਰਮ ਪ੍ਰਚੁਰ ਇਹ ਜਗ ਮੋ ਕਰਾ ॥\
ਅਮਰਦਾਸ ਪੁਨਿ ਨਾਮੁ ਕਹਾਯੋ ॥ ਜਨ ਦੀਪਕ ਤੇ ਦੀਪ ਜਗਾਯੋ ॥੭॥

Nanak took on the body of Angad\
and spread dharam far through this world;\
then he took the name of Amar Das,\
as one lamp is lit from another. (7)

ਜਬ ਬਰਦਾਨ ਸਮੈ ਵਹੁ ਆਵਾ ॥ ਰਾਮਦਾਸ ਤਬ ਗੁਰੂ ਕਹਾਵਾ ॥\
ਤਿਹ ਬਰਦਾਨ ਪੁਰਾਤਨ ਦੀਆ ॥ ਅਮਰਦਾਸ ਸੁਰਪੁਰਿ ਮਗੁ ਲੀਆ ॥੮॥

When the time of that boon came,[^8]\
Ram Das was called the Guru;\
the old boon was granted to him,\
and Amar Das took the road to the city of the gods.[^9] (8)

ਸ੍ਰੀ ਨਾਨਕ ਅੰਗਦਿ ਕਰਿ ਮਾਨਾ ॥ ਅਮਰਦਾਸ ਅੰਗਦ ਪਹਿਚਾਨਾ ॥\
ਅਮਰਦਾਸ ਰਾਮਦਾਸ ਕਹਾਯੋ ॥ ਸਾਧਨਿ ਲਖਾ ਮੂੜ੍ਹ ਨਹਿ ਪਾਯੋ ॥੯॥

Sri Nanak was known as Angad;\
Angad was recognised as Amar Das;\
Amar Das was called Ram Das:\
the holy saw it, but the fools never grasped it. (9)

ਭਿੰਨ ਭਿੰਨ ਸਭਹੂੰ ਕਰ ਜਾਨਾ ॥ ਏਕ ਰੂਪ ਕਿਨਹੂੰ ਪਹਿਚਾਨਾ ॥\
ਜਿਨ ਜਾਨਾ ਤਿਨ ਹੀ ਸਿਧ ਪਾਈ ॥ ਬਿਨ ਸਮਝੇ ਸਿਧ ਹਾਥਿ ਨ ਆਈ ॥੧੦॥

Everyone took them for separate men;\
only a few knew them as one form;\
those who knew it reached the goal,[^10]\
and without that understanding the goal never came to hand. (10)

ਰਾਮਦਾਸ ਹਰਿ ਸੋ ਮਿਲ ਗਏ ॥ ਗੁਰਤਾ ਦੇਤ ਅਰਜਨਹਿ ਭਏ ॥\
ਜਬ ਅਰਜਨ ਪ੍ਰਭੁ ਲੋਕ ਸਿਧਾਏ ॥ ਹਰਿਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਤਿਹ ਠਾਂ ਠਹਿਰਾਏ ॥੧੧॥

Ram Das merged with Hari,\
and gave the Guruship to Arjan;\
when Arjan left for the world of Prabhu,\
Hargobind was set in his place. (11)

ਹਰਿਗੋਬਿੰਦ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਲੋਕ ਸਿਧਾਰੇ ॥ ਹਰੀਰਾਇ ਤਿਹ ਠਾਂ ਬੈਠਾਰੇ ॥\
ਹਰੀਕ੍ਰਿਸਨ ਤਿਨ ਕੇ ਸੁਤ ਵਏ ॥ ਤਿਨ ਤੇ ਤੇਗ ਬਹਾਦਰ ਭਏ ॥੧੨॥

Hargobind left for the world of Prabhu,\
and Har Rai was seated in his place;\
Har Krishan was his son,\
and after him came Tegh Bahadur. (12)

ਤਿਲਕ ਜੰਞੂ ਰਾਖਾ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਤਾ ਕਾ ॥ ਕੀਨੋ ਬਡੋ ਕਲੂ ਮਹਿ ਸਾਕਾ ॥\
ਸਾਧਨ ਹੇਤਿ ਇਤੀ ਜਿਨਿ ਕਰੀ ॥ ਸੀਸੁ ਦੀਆ ਪਰ ਸੀ ਨ ਉਚਰੀ ॥੧੩॥

The master guarded their tilak and sacred thread,[^11]\
and did a great saka[^12] in the Kaliyuga;\
for the sake of holy men he went this far:\
he gave his head, and never let out a cry of pain.[^13] (13)

ਧਰਮ ਹੇਤ ਸਾਕਾ ਜਿਨਿ ਕੀਆ ॥ ਸੀਸੁ ਦੀਆ ਪਰ ਸਿਰਰੁ ਨ ਦੀਆ ॥\
ਨਾਟਕ ਚੇਟਕ ਕੀਏ ਕੁਕਾਜਾ ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਲੋਗਨ ਕਹ ਆਵਤ ਲਾਜਾ ॥੧੪॥

He did this saka for dharam:\
he gave his head, but not his resolve;[^14]\
shows and conjuring tricks are foul deeds,\
and the men of Prabhu are ashamed to do them.[^15] (14)

ਦੋਹਰਾ ॥ *Dohra*

ਠੀਕਰਿ ਫੋਰਿ ਦਿਲੀਸਿ ਸਿਰਿ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਪੁਰ ਕੀਯਾ ਪਯਾਨ ॥\
ਤੇਗ ਬਹਾਦਰ ਸੀ ਕ੍ਰਿਆ ਕਰੀ ਨ ਕਿਨਹੂੰ ਆਨ ॥੧੫॥

He broke his clay pot on the head of the lord of Delhi[^16] and set out for the city of Prabhu;\
no one else has ever done a deed like Tegh Bahadur's. (15)

ਤੇਗ ਬਹਾਦਰ ਕੇ ਚਲਤ ਭਯੋ ਜਗਤ ਕੋ ਸੋਕ ॥\
ਹੈ ਹੈ ਹੈ ਸਭ ਜਗ ਭਯੋ ਜੈ ਜੈ ਜੈ ਸੁਰ ਲੋਕ ॥੧੬॥

When Tegh Bahadur departed, grief fell on the world;\
all the world cried, "Alas, alas, alas," and the world of the gods, "Victory, victory, victory." (16)

---
ਇਤਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਬਚਿਤ੍ਰ ਨਾਟਕ ਗ੍ਰੰਥੇ ਪਾਤਸ਼ਾਹੀ ਬਰਨਨੰ ਨਾਮ ਪੰਚਮੋ ਧਿਆਇ ਸਮਾਪਤ ਮਸਤੁ ਸੁਭ ਮਸਤੁ ॥੫॥ ਅਫਜੂ ॥੨੧੫॥

*Here ends the fifth chapter of the holy book Bachittar Natak, called the Account of the Patshahis. Let it be complete, and let it be blessed. Chapter 5; verses so far, 215.*

*Sri Dasam Granth, Bachittar Natak, Sri Guru Gobind Singh Ji. Gurmukhi text of record from the Shabad OS database, Sri Dasam Granth, section 54; the closing line divided ਸਮਾਪਤ ਮਸਤੁ ਸੁਭ ਮਸਤੁ (samāptam astu, shubham astu). Punjabi commentary from the steek of Narain Singh. Cite as Bachittar Natak 5.13.*

### Notes

[^1]: The Bedis, Guru Nanak's Khatri clan. Narain Singh, on chapter 4: the Bedis, who had learned the Vedas at Kashi, were given the kingdom of Lahore by the Sodhi raja, who went to the forest to do tapasya.
[^2]: The four orders (varna): Brahmin (priest), Kshatriya (ਛਤ੍ਰੀ, warrior), Vaishya (ਬੈਸ, trader and farmer) and Sudra (labourer). In the verse each order does another's work.
[^3]: Dharam (ਧਰਮ), the duty and right way of each order; the word stays dharam wherever it appears.
[^4]: Nanak Rai: Guru Nanak; Rai (ਰਾਇ), king, a title of honour.
[^5]: Kaliyuga (ਕਲ, ਕਲੂ), the fourth and darkest age of the world, the present one.
[^6]: Prabhu (ਪ੍ਰਭ), Hari (ਹਰਿ): names of the Lord.
[^7]: Kal (ਕਾਲ), Time and Death; in the Bachittar Natak also the Lord as all-conquering Time. The net of Kal is death and birth again.
[^8]: Narain Singh, on chapter 4: when the Sodhi raja gave up his kingdom, the Bedis promised him that when they came as Guru Nanak, they would make him Guru in the third body after theirs, so that the fourth Guru would be a Sodhi.
[^9]: The city of the gods (ਸੁਰਪੁਰਿ); Narain Singh: Baikunth.
[^10]: The goal (ਸਿਧ), success, perfection; Narain Singh: liberation.
[^11]: Tilak (ਤਿਲਕ), the mark of the Hindu faith on the forehead; the sacred thread (ਜੰਞੂ, janeu), worn by Brahmins, Kshatriyas and Vaishyas. Narain Singh: Brahmins suffering under Aurangzeb's oppression came to Guru Tegh Bahadur pleading for help, and he went to give his life to end the suffering of the Hindus.
[^12]: Saka (ਸਾਕਾ), a deed of sacrifice remembered for ever.
[^13]: A cry of pain (ਸੀ), the hiss of breath drawn in through the teeth.
[^14]: Resolve (ਸਿਰਰੁ), steadfastness, sounding against ਸੀਸੁ, head.
[^15]: Narain Singh: Aurangzeb pressed the Guru to show a miracle, and he would not, holding that a miracle goes against the will of Prabhu.
[^16]: To break a clay pot (ਠੀਕਰਿ) on someone's head is to leave the blame on him; Narain Singh reads the pot as the Guru's own body. The lord of Delhi (ਦਿਲੀਸਿ) is Aurangzeb.

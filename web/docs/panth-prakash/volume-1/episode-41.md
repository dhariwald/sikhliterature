# Panth Prakash, Episode 41

## Banda Enters the City

### (shahar bāvan madh bāvanī tahiṅ turak dae bhajāi: In the city and through all the Bavani, the Turks were driven out)

>**Questions**
>
>1. Banda's diwan of Sirhind?
>2. The Guru's command Banda forgot?
>3. What came of it?

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਸ਼ਹਰ ਮਾਂਹਿ ਦਾਖਲ ਕੀਯੋ ਬਾਜ ਸਿੰਘ ਜਿਹ ਨਾਮ ॥\
ਜਾਤਿ ਬੱਲ ਜਟ ਬੰਸ ਥੌ ਮੀਰ ਪੁਰ ਪੱਟੀ ਉਸ ਗਾਮ ॥੧ ॥

Banda sent his men into the city of Sirhind under a Singh whose name was Baj Singh;\
by caste he was a Jatt of the Bal clan,[^1] of that line, and his own village was Mirpur Patti.[^2] (1)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਥੋ ਬੰਦੇ ਨੇ ਦਿਵਾਨ ਬਣਾਯਾ ॥ ਲਭਯੋ ਮਾਲ ਸਭ ਉਸ ਸੌਂਪਾਯਾ ॥\
ਚਾਰੇ ਭਾਈ ਥੇ ਅਤਿ ਸੂਰੇ ॥ ਦੁਇ ਠਾਣੀ ਦੁਇ ਰਹੈਂ ਹਜੂਰੇ ॥੨॥

Banda had made Baj Singh his diwan, his chief minister,\
and all the wealth that was found, he handed over into his keeping;\
there were four brothers of them, and every one a mighty warrior;\
two held the posts[^3] of the country, and two stayed in attendance on Banda. (2)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਬਾਜ ਸਿੰਘ ਔ ਰਾਮ ਸਿੰਘ ਕੋਇਰ ਸਿੰਘ ਸਿਆਮ ॥\
ਤਿਨ ਕੋ ਬਾਵਨ ਸਭ ਦਈ ਔ ਦਯੋ ਬੰਦੈ ਸਭ ਕਾਮ ॥੩॥

Baj Singh, and Ram Singh, and Koir Singh, and Shyam Singh, these four were the brothers;\
to them Banda gave the whole of the Bavani,[^4] and he gave them all the work of governing it. (3)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਆਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਨਾਇਬ ਠਹਿਰਾਯਾ ॥ ਮੁਲਕ ਬਾਵਨੀ ਉਨ ਉਗਰਾਯਾ ॥\
ਬੰਦਾ ਸ੍ਰਿਹੰਦੈ ਦਾਖਲ ਭਯੋ ॥ ਬਜੀਰੈ ਮਾਲ ਸੁ ਬੰਦੁਬਸਤ ਕਯੋ ॥੪॥

Ali Singh[^5] was appointed to serve as his deputy,\
and he gathered in the revenue of all the Bavani country;\
then Banda himself came in and entered the city of Sirhind,\
and set in order all of Wazira's[^6] wealth and goods. (4)

ਸ਼ਹਰ ਰੁਕਯੋ ਸੋ ਲੁੱਟਯੋ ਨਾਹੀ ॥ ਚੂਕ ਗਯੋ ਸੁ ਵਖਤ ਪਛੁਤਾਹੀ ॥\
ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਬਚਨ ਥੋ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਉਜੜਾਵਨ ॥ ਲੂਟ ਕੂਟ ਭੁਇ ਸੰਗ ਮਿਲਾਵਨ ॥੫॥

Banda held the city of Sirhind, but he did not plunder it;\
the right moment slipped past him, and afterward he was sorry for it;\
for the Satguru's command had been to lay that city waste,\
to plunder it, to pound it to pieces, and to level it with the ground. (5)

ਸੋਰਠਾ *Sortha*

ਭੂਲ ਗਯੋ ਵਹਿ ਕਾਰ ਸਤਿਗੁਰਿ ਭੇਜਯੋ ਜਿਮ ਕਹੀ ॥\
ਤੌ ਹੀ ਭਯੋ ਖੁਆਰ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਸ੍ਰਾਪਯੋ ਉਨ ਰੱਖਿਯੋ ॥੬॥

He forgot that task, the very task for which the Satguru had sent him, just as the Guru had told him;\
and for that he came to ruin and disgrace, because he spared the city that lay under the Guru's curse. (6)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਬੰਦੇ ਸ਼ਹਿਰ ਸੁ ਰਖਯੋ ਬਚਾਈ ॥ ਦਈ ਟਕਿਅਨ ਉਗਰਾਹੀ ਪਾਈ ॥\
ਹਿੰਦੂ ਕਾਰ ਸੁ ਦਾਰ ਬਨਾਏ ॥ ਮੁਸਲਮਾਨ ਲੁਕਿ ਛੱਪਿ ਬਚਾਏ ॥੭॥

So Banda kept the city safe and spared it from destruction,\
and laid a levy of coin upon it, to be gathered in;\
he made Hindus his officers, to run the city for him,\
while the Muslims saved their lives by hiding and lying low. (7)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਬਾਜ ਸਿੰਘ ਘੋੜੈ ਚੜ੍ਹੈ ਪਗੁ ਤੁਰਕੈ ਸੀਸ ਟਿਕਾਇ ॥\
ਸ਼ਹਰ ਬਾਵਨ ਮਧ ਬਾਵਨੀ ਤਹਿਂ ਤੁਰਕ ਦਏ ਭਜਾਇ ॥੮॥

Baj Singh, whenever he mounted his horse, would set his foot upon a Turk's[^7] head as he climbed into the saddle;\
in the city and all through the Bavani, the Turks there were driven out and put to flight. (8)

ਛੱਤੀ ਲੱਖ ਕੋ ਪਰਗਣੋ ਪਲ ਮੈਂ ਲਿਯੋ ਛੁਡਵਾਇ ॥\
ਬੰਦੇ ਕਹਯੋ ਸਦ ਖਾਲਸੇ ਲੁਟ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਕੌ ਲਿਓ ਖਾਇ ॥੯॥

A district of thirty-six lakh[^8] was set free from the Turks in a single moment;\
and Banda called the Khalsa together and said, "Plunder the Turks, and live on what you take." (9)

Punjabi commentary from the Prachin Panth Prakash Steek, part 1, of Singh Sahib Baba Santa Singh Akali, Jathedar of the 96 Krori Budha Dal. Kulwant Singh is named in full in the notes; Baba Santa Singh Akali is BSS. Cite as Panth Prakash 41.4.*

### Notes

[^1]: Jatt: the great farming caste of the Punjab; Bal is one of its clans.
[^2]: Mirpur Patti: Mirpur, by Patti in the Majha. Kulwant Singh: Baj Singh was sent with Banda by Guru Gobind Singh, and died a martyr with him at Delhi.
[^3]: posts: the poet's ṭhāṇe, the posts of armed men that held the country and kept order. BSS: much like a district police chief today.
[^4]: Bavani: the fifty-two, the district of Sirhind. Kulwant Singh: a province of fifty-two villages; BSS: a district bringing in fifty-two lakh a year in revenue.
[^5]: Ali Singh: of Salaudi (Episode 34).
[^6]: Wazira: the poet's scornful short form for Wazir Khan, the nawab of Sirhind, killed at Chappar Chiri (Episode 40).
[^7]: Turk: the poet's word for the Muslim rulers and their armies, and for Muslims generally. BSS: Baj Singh made it his rule to strike down one Turk every day before he mounted.
[^8]: lakh: a hundred thousand. BSS: thirty-six lakh rupees of revenue a year; Kulwant Singh: thirty-six lakh people. BSS faults Banda for keeping the royal treasure and leaving the Khalsa to plunder, when he should have shared it all out.

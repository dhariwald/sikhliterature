# Panth Prakash, Episode 15

## The Sākhī of the Origin of the Panth of the Khalsa at Sri Kesgarh[^1]

### (amrit saṅskār, pañj bhujaṅgī jo bhae...: the rite of amrit; the five who became bhujaṅgīs...)

>note:\
>Names of the 5 chosen bhujangis\
>Practices the Guru said to follow, and what to avoid\

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਇਤਨੀ ਬਾਤ ਵਿਚਾਰ ਕਰ ਹੁਤੀ ਬਾਤ ਲਈ ਚੀਨ ॥\
ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਬਹਿ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਕੇਸਗੜ ਉਦਮ ਸੋਊ ਕੀਨ ॥੧॥

Having thought the matter through, he had settled on what was to be done,\
and the Satguru sat at Sri Kesgarh[^2] and set about that very work. (1)

ਸਦ ਪੰਡਤ ਬਡ ਜੋਤਕੀ ਮਹੂਰਤ ਖੂਬ ਕਢਾਇ ॥\
ਕੀਏ ਭੁਜੰਗੀ ਛਾਂਟ ਪੰਜ ਪੰਜੇ ਜਾਤ ਗਿਣਾਇ ॥੨॥

He called pandits and great astrologers and had them find a truly good hour,\
and he picked out five bhujaṅgīs,[^3] one from each of five jātis.[^4] (2)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਪੰਜ ਭੁਜੰਗੀ ਲਏ ਉਠਾਇ ॥ ਚਾਰੈ ਬਰਨ ਇਕ ਕੀਏ ਭਰਾਇ ॥\
ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮ ਦਯਾ ਸਿੰਘ ਸੋਫਤੀ ਜਾਤ ॥ ਬਸਤ ਲਾਹੌਰ ਹੁਤੇ ਬਖਯਾਤ ॥੩॥

He raised up the five bhujaṅgīs\
and made the four varnas[^5] one, as brothers.\
First was Daya Singh, of the Sobti[^6] jāti,\
who lived in Lahore and was well known. (3)

ਦੂਜੋ ਧਰਮ ਸਿੰਘ ਹਸਤਨਾਪੁਰ ਬਾਸੀ ॥ ਹੁਤੋ ਜੱਟ ਸਿਵ ਜਟੋ ਪ੍ਰਕਾਸ਼ੀ ॥\
ਤੀਜੋ ਮੁਹਕਮ ਸਿੰਘ ਦ੍ਵਾਰਕਾ ਜਾਯੋ ॥ ਨਾਮਦੇਵ ਅਵਤਾਰ ਸੁ ਆਯੋ ॥੪॥

Second was Dharam Singh, who lived in Hastinapur,[^7]\
a Jatt, of the stock that sprang from Shiva's matted hair.[^8]\
Third was Mohkam Singh, born at Dwarka,[^9]\
who had come as an avatar of Namdev.[^10] (4)

ਚੌਥੇ ਨਾਈ ਸਾਹਿਬ ਸਿੰਘ ਜੋਊ ॥ ਸੈਣ ਭਗਤ ਜਹ ਰਹਤੇ ਸੋਊ ॥\
ਪੰਚਮ ਹਿੰਮਤ ਸਿੰਘ ਝੀਵਰ ਕਹੀਐ ॥ ਪੰਜ ਭੁਜੰਗੀ ਪੰਜ ਜਾਤ ਲਹੀਐ ॥੫॥

Fourth was the Nai,[^11] Sahib Singh,\
from where Sain Bhagat[^12] lived.\
Fifth was Himmat Singh, called a Jhinwar:[^13]\
five bhujaṅgīs from five jātis. (5)

ਪਾਹੁਲ ਖੰਡੇ ਤਯਾਰ ਕਰਵਾਈ ॥ ਜਲ ਮੱਧ ਆਨ ਮਿਠਾਈ ਪਾਈ ॥\
ਤਿਹ ਮੱਧ ਰਾਖੀ ਕਰਦ ਗਡਾਇ ॥ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਰਾਖੀ ਕਰਦ ਧਾਰੇ ਦਾਇ ॥੬॥

He had the pāhul[^14] of the khanda[^15] made ready;\
sweets were brought and put into the water.[^16]\
He kept a kard[^17] planted in it,\
and the Satguru held the kard with its edge foremost, in his right hand. (6)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਸ੍ਰੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸਤਿਨਾਮ ਕਹਿਓ ਕਰਤਾ ਪੁਰਖ ਉਚਾਰ ॥\
ਅਕਾਲ ਅਕਾਲਹ ਜਾਪ ਕਰ ਤੋਰੀ ਕ੍ਰਿਪਾਨਿ ਸਧਾਰ ॥੭॥

Sri Satguru said "Satnam"[^18] and uttered "Karta Purakh",\
and repeating "Akal, Akal", he stirred it with the kirpan[^19] held firm. (7)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮ ਭਗੌਤੀ ਵਾਰ ਜੁ ਪੌੜੀ ॥ ਪੜ੍ਹ ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮੈਂ ਆਪ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਜੋੜੀ ॥\
ਨੌਂ ਪਤਸਾਹੀਅਨ ਲਯੋ ਧਿਆਇ ॥ ਅਬ ਦਸਮਨ ਪਰ ਹੋਈਂ ਸਹਾਇ ॥੮॥

First he read the pauri of the Var of Bhagauti,[^20]\
which the Satguru himself had just composed;\
he called the nine Patshahis[^21] to mind,\
and asked them now to stand by the Tenth. (8)

ਬਹੁਰ ਸਵੱਯੇ ਬੱਤੀ ਉਚਾਰੇ ॥ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਮੁੱਖੋਂ ਸਵਾਰੇ ॥\
ਤੇਜ ਤੇਜ ਜੋ ਚੰਡੀ ਬਾਣੀ ॥ ਸੋਉ ਪਾਹੁਲ ਕੇ ਮੱਧੈ ਠਾਣੀ ॥੯॥

Then the thirty-two savaiyas[^22] were recited,\
which Sri Satguru had fashioned with his own blessed mouth;\
and the sacred verse of Chandi,[^23] full of fierce fire,\
that too he set into the pāhul. (9)

*Tribhangi Chhand* ਤ੍ਰਿਭੰਗੀ ਛੰਦ

> *Sri Dasam Granth, Bachittar Natak, chapter 1, verse 2.*
>
> ਖਗ ਖੰਡ ਬਿਹੰਡੰ ਖਲ ਦਲ ਖੰਡੰ ਅਤਿ ਰਣ ਮੰਡੰ ਬਰ ਬੰਡੰ ॥\
> *khag khaṅd bihaṅdaṅ khal dal khaṅdaṅ, ati raṇ maṅdaṅ bar baṅdaṅ*\
> The Sword[^24] that cuts and hews, that cuts down the armies of the wicked, glory of the battlefield, mighty one;
>
> ਭੁਜਦੰਡ ਅਖੰਡੰ ਤੇਜ ਪ੍ਰਚੰਡੰ ਜੋਤਿ ਅਮੰਡੰ ਭਾਨ ਪ੍ਰਭੰ ॥\
> *bhujdaṅd akhaṅdaṅ tej prachaṅdaṅ, joti amaṅdaṅ bhān prabhaṅ*\
> Arm that cannot be broken, splendour blazing, light undimmed, radiant as the sun;
>
> ਸੁਖ ਸੰਤਾ ਕਰਣੰ ਦੁਰਮਤਿ ਦਰਣੰ ਕਿਲਵਿਖ ਹਰਣੰ ਅਸਿ ਸਰਣੰ ॥\
> *sukh saṅtā karṇaṅ durmati darṇaṅ, kilvikh harṇaṅ asi sarṇaṅ*\
> You bring peace to the saints, you crush evil thinking, you take away sin: I have come to your refuge, Sword.
>
> ਜੈ ਜੈ ਜਗ ਕਾਰਣ ਸ੍ਰਿਸਿ ਉਬਾਰਣ ਮਮ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਰਣ ਜੈ ਤੇਗੰ ॥\
> *jai jai jag kāraṇ srisi ubāraṇ, mam pratipāraṇ jai tegaṅ*\
> Hail, hail, cause of the world, saviour of creation, my sustainer: hail to the Sword.

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮ ਅੰਚਰੀ ਭਰੀ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਉਨ ਲਲਕਾਰ ॥\
ਸਨਮੁਖ ਨੇਤ੍ਰ ਰਖਵਾਇ ਕਰ ਦਈ ਮੱਧ ਮੈਂ ਡਾਰ ॥੧੦॥

First Sri Satguru filled his cupped hands and called out to them;\
he had them keep their eyes on him and threw it into their eyes. (10)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਅਕਾਲ ਅਕਾਲ ਕਰ ਜਾਪ ਜਪਾਈ ॥ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਵਾਹਿਗੁਰੂ ਗੁਰ ਫਤ੍ਹੇ ਬੁਲਾਈ ॥\
ਪੁਨ ਪੰਜ ਪੰਜ ਸੀਸ ਅੰਚਰੀ ਪਾਇ ॥ ਪੰਚ ਪੰਚ ਪੰਚਨ ਦਈ ਛਕਾਇ ॥੧੧॥

He had them repeat "Akal, Akal"\
and call out the Guru's Fateh[^25] of Sri Vahiguru.\
Then he poured five handfuls on each one's head,\
and had each of the five drink five. (11)

ਇਕੈ ਸੁਨਹਿਰੇ ਸਭੀ ਰਲਾਏ ॥ ਭਿੰਨ ਭੇਤ ਕਛੁ ਰਖਨ ਨ ਪਾਏ ॥\
ਚਾਰ ਬਰਨ ਔ ਆਸ਼੍ਰਮ ਚਾਰ ॥ ਜਨੇਊ ਟਿੱਕੈ ਦਿਯੋ ਉਤਾਰ ॥੧੨॥

He mixed them all together in one sunehria,[^26]\
and let no difference of any kind be kept.\
The four varnas and the four ashramas:[^27]\
he had the sacred thread and the tilak[^28] taken off. (12)

ਪੁਨ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਉਨ ਸਿੱਛਯਾ ਦਈ ॥ ਮੀਣੇ ਮਸੰਦਨ ਬਰਤਯੋ ਨਹੀਂ ॥\
ਕੁੜੀਮਾਰ ਰਮਰਈ ਨੜੀਮਾਰ ॥ ਜੋ ਇਨ ਮਿਲੇ ਸੁ ਹੁਇ ਹੈ ਖ੍ਵਾਰ ॥੧੩॥

Then the Satguru gave them teaching:\
"Have no dealings with the Minas or the masaṅds,[^29]\
or with those who kill their daughters, the Ramraias,[^30] or those who smoke;\
whoever mixes with these will come to grief. (13)

ਜਥਾ ਸ਼ਕਤ ਗੁਰ ਗੋਲਕ ਪਾਯੋ ॥ ਕੜਾਹ ਕਰਾਇ ਖਾਲਸੇ ਛਕਾਯੋ ॥\
ਪਹਿਰ ਕਛਹਿਰੇ ਸਿਰ ਬੰਧਯੋ ਪਾਗ ॥ ਗੁਰ ਗ੍ਰੰਥ ਬਚਨ ਪਰ ਰਹਯੋ ਲਾਗ ॥੧੪॥

"Put what you can into the Guru's offering box;\
have kaṛāh[^31] made and feed it to the Khalsa.\
Wear the kachhehra[^32] and tie a turban on your head,\
and hold fast to the word of the Guru Granth." (14)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਯੌ ਕਹਿਕੈ ਸ੍ਰੀ ਸਤਿਗੁਰੁ ਗਲ ਤੇਗੋ ਦੀਨੋ ਪਾਇ ॥\
ਕਰਦ ਚਕਰ ਸਿਰ ਪਰ ਧਰੇਂ ਮੁਖੋਂ ਅਕਾਲ ਜਪਾਇ ॥੧੫॥

Having said this, Sri Satguru hung a tegh at each one's neck;\
they set kard and chakkar[^33] on their heads and repeated "Akal" aloud. (15)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਔਰ ਕਹੀ ਗੁਰਬਾਣੀ ਪੜ੍ਹਾਯੋ ॥ ਜਪੁ ਜਾਪ ਦੋਇ ਵੇਲੇ ਜਪਾਯੋ ॥\
ਔ ਅਨੰਦ ਰਹਿਰਾਸ ਜਪੱਯੋ ॥ ਚੰਡੀ ਬਾਣੀ ਖੜੇ ਪੜੱਯੋ ॥੧੬॥

He told them further to read the Guru's sacred verse:\
"Recite Japu and Jap[^34] at both times of day,\
and recite Anand and Rehras,[^35]\
and read the sacred verse of Chandi standing. (16)

ਦੋਇ ਵੇਲੇ ਉਠ ਬੰਧਯੋ ਦਸਤਾਰੇ ॥ ਪਹਰ ਆਠ ਰਖੱਯੋ ਸ਼ਸਤ੍ਰ ਸੰਭਾਰੇ ॥\
ਪੀਓ ਸੁਧਾ ਔ ਖੇਲੋ ਸ਼ਿਕਾਰ ॥ ਸ਼ਸਤ੍ਰ ਵਿਦਯਾ ਜਿਮ ਹੋਇ ਸੰਭਾਰ ॥੧੭॥

"Rise at both times of day and tie your turban;\
keep your arms by you and cared for at every hour of the day and night.\
Drink the nectar[^36] and go hunting,\
so that your skill in arms is kept up. (17)

ਕਰ ਝਟਕੈ ਬਕਰਨ ਕੋ ਖੱਯੋ ॥ ਮੁਰਦੈ ਕੁੱਠੈ ਨਿਕਟ ਨ ਜੱਯੋ ॥\
ਕੇਸਨ ਕੀ ਕੀਜੋ ਪ੍ਰਤਿਪਾਲ ॥ ਨਹਿ ਉਸਤ੍ਰਨ ਸੋ ਕਟਯੋ ਬਾਲ ॥੧੮॥

"Kill goats with jhatka[^37] and eat them;\
do not go near carrion or meat killed in the Muslim way.\
Take care of your hair,\
and never cut a hair with a razor. (18)

ਪਿਤ੍ਰਨ ਕਰਮ ਸੋ ਛੋਰਨ ਕਰਨੋਂ ॥ ਰਖਯੋ ਧਯਾਨ ਸੁ ਮਧ ਗੁਰ ਚਰਨੋਂ ॥\
ਪਰੈ ਔਰ ਜੋ ਖੋਟੇ ਰਾਹਿ ॥ ਤਿਨ ਕੋ ਲਾਯੋ ਜ਼ਰੂਰ ਤਨਖਾਹਿ ॥੧੯॥

"Give up the rites for the ancestors,[^38]\
and keep your mind on the Guru's feet.\
And anyone else who takes a false road,\
be sure to lay a tankhāh[^39] on him." (19)

*Dohra* ਦੋਹਰਾ

ਕਰੀ ਜੁ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮ ਬਿਧ ਸੋਈ ਪੁਨ ਬਿਧ ਕੀਨ ॥\
ਪੰਜ ਭੁਜੰਗੀ ਜੋ ਭਏ ਗੁਰ ਉਨਤੇ ਪਾਹੁਲ ਲੀਨ ॥੨੦॥

The rite the Satguru had first performed, that same rite was performed again:\
from the five who had become bhujaṅgīs, the Guru took the pāhul. (20)

*Chaupai* ਚੌਪਈ

ਵਹੀ ਵਰਤਾਰੋ ਭੁਜੰਗਨ ਵਰਤਾਯੋ ॥ ਆਪਸ ਗੁਰ ਚੇਲਾ ਕਹਿਵਾਯੋ ॥\
ਯਹੀ ਆਦ ਹੁਤ ਆਯੋ ਵਰਤਾਰਾ ॥ ਜਿਮ ਨਾਨਕ ਗੁਰ ਅੰਗਦ ਧਾਰਾ ॥੨੧॥

The bhujaṅgīs gave him the same rite in turn,\
and he had himself called both Guru and disciple.\
This had been the practice from the very beginning,\
as Nanak took Angad for his Guru.[^40] (21)

ਪੀਯੋ ਪਾਹੁਲ ਗੁਰ ਬਚਨ ਨਿਵਾਰੀ ॥ ਭਯੋ ਖਾਲਸੋ ਵਡ ਕਲਧਾਰੀ ॥\
ਸਾਲ ਬਾਵਨ ਪਰ ਸੱਤ੍ਵੇ ਸਈ ॥ ਬੁਧਵਾਰ ਬਸਾਖ ਪੰਥ ਉਤਪਤਿ ਭਈ ॥੨੨॥

The Guru drank the pāhul, keeping to his own word,\
and the Khalsa became great, a bearer of power.\
In the year 1752 (1695 CE),\
on a Wednesday in Vaisakh,[^41] the Panth came into being. (22)

### Notes
[^1]: sākhī: an account of an event.
[^2]: Kesgarh: the fort at Anandpur where the Khalsa was created.
[^3]: bhujaṅgī: literally the young of a snake; the poet's name for the Guru's first initiated Singhs.
[^4]: jāti: caste group by birth.
[^5]: varna: the four orders of Brahmin, Kshatriya, Vaishya and Sudra.
[^6]: Sobti: a Khatri subcaste.
[^7]: Hastinapur: the ancient Kaurava capital, near Meerut; Baba Santa Singh Akali takes it to mean Delhi.
[^8]: Jatt: the landholding farmer jāti; the poet gives the old folk account that the Jatts sprang from Shiva's jaṭā, his matted locks.
[^9]: Dwarka: Krishna's city on the coast of Gujarat.
[^10]: Namdev: the Bhagat of Maharashtra, of the Chhimba jāti, whose hymns are in the Guru Granth Sahib; tradition makes Mohkam Singh a Chhimba too.
[^11]: Nai: the barber jāti.
[^12]: Sain: the Bhagat, a barber, whose hymn is in the Guru Granth Sahib; Baba Santa Singh Akali places his home at Bidar.
[^13]: Jhinwar: the water-carrier jāti. · Baba Santa Singh Akali's reading is that Himmat Singh was an avatar of the hunter whose arrow killed Krishna.
[^14]: pāhul: the initiation drink, the poet's word for amrit.
[^15]: khanda: the double-edged sword.
[^16]: Tradition has Mata Sahib Devan bring the patāse, sugar drops, and put them in.
[^17]: kard: a knife or short blade. · Baba Santa Singh Akali rejects as idle talk the story that goats were tied in the tent and killed in place of the five.
[^18]: Satnam: the True Name. · Karta Purakh: the Creator Being. · Akal: the Timeless One.
[^19]: kirpan: the sword of the Khalsa.
[^20]: The Var of Bhagauti: Guru Gobind Singh's ballad also called Chandi di Var; its first pauri, which calls on Bhagauti, the sword as divine power, and then the nine Gurus, opens the Sikh ardās. · Baba Santa Singh Akali's reading is that the Guru composed it at this moment.
[^21]: The nine Patshahis: the nine Gurus before Guru Gobind Singh.
[^22]: savaiya: a verse metre; the rite today recites ten savaiyas of Guru Gobind Singh.
[^23]: Chandi: the goddess Durga; the verses in praise of her battles are by Guru Gobind Singh.
[^24]: khag, asi, tegh: names of the sword, tegh the broad one; Guru Gobind Singh addresses the sword as the power of Akal.
[^25]: Fateh: the greeting Vahiguru ji ka Khalsa, Vahiguru ji ki Fateh, the Khalsa is Vahiguru's and so is the victory.
[^26]: sunehria: a large metal bowl.
[^27]: ashrama: the four stages of a Brahmanical life, student, householder, forest dweller and renouncer.
[^28]: tilak: the mark on the forehead.
[^29]: Minas: the followers of Prithi Chand, elder brother of Guru Arjan, and his line. · masaṅd: the Guru's collectors of offerings, removed by Guru Gobind Singh.
[^30]: Ramraias: the followers of Ram Rai, elder son of Guru Har Rai, put out of the Sikh fold for altering Gurbani before Aurangzeb.
[^31]: kaṛāh: the sweet of flour, ghee and sugar offered and shared in the saṅgat.
[^32]: kachhehra: the short breeches of the Khalsa.
[^33]: chakkar: a steel throwing quoit, worn on the turban.
[^34]: Japu: Guru Nanak's Japji. · Jap: Guru Gobind Singh's Jap Sahib.
[^35]: Anand: Guru Amar Das's Anand Sahib. · Rahiras: the evening prayer.
[^36]: sudha: nectar.
[^37]: jhatka: an animal killed with a single stroke.
[^38]: The shraddh and other rites for the dead of the family.
[^39]: tankhāh: a penalty laid by the Khalsa on one who breaks its discipline.
[^40]: Guru Nanak installed Guru Angad and bowed to him as Guru.
[^41]: Vaisakh runs from mid-April to mid-May; the date usually given for the creation of the Khalsa is Vaisakhi of 1756, 1699 CE.

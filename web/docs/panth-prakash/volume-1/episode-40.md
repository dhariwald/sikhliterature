# Panth Prakash, Episode 40

## The Story of the Killing of Bajid

### (itī bajīrā mārio jeṭh sudī ke māṅhi: So Wazira was killed in the bright fortnight of Jeth)

>**Questions**
>
>1. The Singhs' plea, and Banda's reply?
>2. What Banda's arrow did?
>3. Wazira's end?

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਬੰਦੇ ਕਾ ਡੇਰਾ ਸੁਨਿ ਆਯਾ ॥ ਸਹੌਂ ਬਜੀਰਾ ਲੜਨੈ ਧਾਯਾ ॥\
ਤੋਪ ਜੰਬੂਰੇ ਮੂਹਰੇ ਧਰੇ ॥ ਤਿਸ ਕੇ ਸਾਥ ਪਿਆਦੇ ਕਰੇ ॥੧॥

Wazira[^1] heard where Banda had pitched his camp, and came out against him,\
marching straight out to meet him face to face and fight him;\
he set his cannon and his swivel guns out in front of his army,\
and close beside the guns he placed his foot soldiers. (1)

ਇਕ ਵਲ ਕੀਏ ਸ਼ੁਤਰ ਹਥਨਾਰ ॥ ਇਸ ਬਿਧ ਕਰੀ ਲੜਨ ਕੀ ਕਾਰ ॥\
ਢੁਕ ਬੰਦੇ ਢਿਗ ਸ਼ਲਕ ਚਲਾਈ ॥ ਜਨ ਗੋਲਨ ਬਰਖਾ ਬਰਖਾਈ ॥੨॥

On one flank he set the swivel guns mounted on camels,[^2]\
and in this way he made his whole army ready for battle;\
drawing close to Banda, they let loose a volley all together,\
as if a rain of iron balls were pouring down upon the Singhs. (2)

ਤੋਪ ਜੰਬੂਰੇ ਰਹਕਲ ਭਾਰੇ ॥ ਚਲਵਾਏ ਬਜੀਰੈ ਜੋ ਥੇ ਸਾਰੇ ॥\
ਗੋਲੇ ਚਲੇ ਜੰਜੀਰਹਿ ਜੋੜ ॥ ਲਗੈਂ ਬਿਰਛ ਉਸ ਸੁੱਟੈਂ ਤੋੜ ॥੩॥

Cannon, swivel guns, and heavy field guns, every one of them,\
all the guns Wazira had, he ordered to fire at once;\
the cannonballs flew joined one to another with chains,[^3]\
and any tree they struck, they broke apart and threw to the ground. (3)

ਗੋਲੀ ਬਰਸੈ ਜੈਸੇ ਮੇਹਿਂ ॥ ਖੜੇ ਬਹਨ ਕਿਮ ਪੈਣ ਨ ਦੇਹਿਂ ॥\
ਧੂਓਂ ਉਠ ਭਈ ਦਿਨ ਤੇ ਰਾਤ ॥ ਘੋੜੇ ਪਗ ਬਹੁ ਧੂੜ ਉਡਾਤ ॥੪॥

Bullets came pouring down upon them like rain from the sky,\
and would not let a man stand up, or sit, or even lie down;\
the smoke rose up so thick that the day was turned into night,\
and the hooves of the horses kicked up great clouds of dust. (4)

ਜਨ ਕਰ ਭਈ ਹਨੇਰੀ ਰਾਤ ॥ ਤੋੜੇ ਚਮਕ ਟਿਟਾਣੇ ਵਾਤ ॥\
ਧੂਓਂ ਧਾਰ ਉਠ ਹੋ ਗਈ ਘਟਾ ॥ ਦਮਕਤ ਪਯਾਲੇ ਜਨੁ ਬਿਜ ਛਟਾ ॥੫॥

It was as if a pitch-dark night had fallen on the field;\
the burning matches[^4] of the muskets glowed in it like fireflies;\
the smoke rose up in streams and gathered into a heavy cloud,\
and the priming pans[^5] flashed out in it like streaks of lightning. (5)

ਚੇਲੇ ਪਾਸੋਂ ਨਠ ਨਠ ਜ਼ਾਹਿਂ ॥ ਬੰਦੇ ਉਹਲੇ ਹੋਇ ਲੁਕਾਹਿਂ ॥\
ਲੁਟੇਰੇ ਲੋਕ ਜਿ ਬੰਦੇ ਰਖੇ ॥ ਲਰਨ ਮਰਨ ਤਜ ਹੁਇ ਗਏ ਵਖੇ ॥੬॥

Banda's disciples ran away from his side, running and running,\
and hid themselves behind Banda, keeping him between them and the enemy;\
the plundering folk whom Banda had taken on to go with him\
gave up all fighting and dying, and went off by themselves. (6)

ਕਹੈਂ ਫਕੀਰ ਕਹਾਂ ਹਮ ਗਾਰੈ ॥ ਕਰਤ ਮਜੂਰੀ ਖਾਹਿਂ ਸੁਖਾਰੈ ॥\
ਚੇਲੇ ਬੰਦੇ ਸਭ ਨਠ ਗਏ ॥ ਸਿੰਘ ਸੂਰਮੇਂ ਠਾਂਢੇ ਰਹੇ ॥੭॥

They said, "Where has this faqir[^6] brought us, to be ground to ruin?\
When we worked for our wages, we ate our bread in comfort and in peace."\
So all of Banda's disciples ran away and were gone,\
but the Singhs, the true warriors, stood their ground and stayed. (7)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਭਯੋ ਮੁਕਾਬਲਾ ਦੁਇ ਵਲੋਂ ਸਭ ਸਿਰਤੰਤ ਵਜੀਰੈ ਪਾਸ ॥\
ਜੁ ਲੁਟੇਰੇ ਬੰਦੇ ਸਾਥ ਥੇ ਜ਼ਖੀਰੋ ਕੀਯੋ ਨ ਤਾਸ ॥੮॥

So the battle was joined from both sides, and all the stores and gear of war were with Wazira,\
while the plunderers who had come along with Banda had laid up no stores of any kind. (8)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਉਤੈ ਬਜੀਰੈ ਸਭ ਕੁਛ ਲੀਆ ॥ ਬੰਦੈ ਨੇ ਕੁਛ ਸੰਜਮ ਨ ਕੀਆ ॥\
ਨਹਿਂ ਕਿਛ ਦਾਰੂ ਸਿੱਕੋ ਬ੍ਰਤਾਯੋ ॥ ਨਹਿਂ ਕਿਛ ਤੋਪ ਜੰਬੂਰਨ ਆਹਯੋ ॥੯॥

Over there, Wazira had taken care to provide himself with everything,\
but Banda had laid nothing by and kept nothing back in store;\
he had handed out no powder and no lead to his fighting men,\
and he had brought no cannon and no swivel guns at all. (9)

ਉਤੇ ਸਿਰਤੰਤ ਅਯੋ ਪਤਸ਼ਾਹੀ ॥ ਇਤ ਲੜ ਡੰਗ ਖਾਨ ਕੋ ਨਾਂਹੀ ॥\
ਉਤਵਲ ਮੋਹਰ ਰੁਪਏ ਤੁੜਾਏ ॥ ਇਤ ਲੁਟਿ ਮਿਲੈ ਤੌ ਡੰਗ ਲੰਘਾਏ ॥੧੦॥

Over there were all the stores and gear of the Patshahi,\
but here, while they fought, there was not even one meal to eat;\
over there they changed gold coins[^7] and silver rupees into small money to spend,\
but here, only if plunder came their way, could they get through a single meal. (10)

ਦੇਖੋ ਕਲਾ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਕੀ ਨਯਾਰੀ ॥ ਜਿੱਤੇ ਫਕੀਰ ਸ਼ਾਹਿ ਜਾਇ ਹਾਰੀ ॥\
ਪ੍ਰਭ ਚਾਹੇ ਮੇਰੁ ਤ੍ਰਿਣੋ ਉਠਾਇ ॥ ਪ੍ਰਭ ਚਾਹੈ ਸਮੁੰਦ੍ਰ ਘੜਯੋਂ ਸੁਕਾਇ ॥੧੧॥

See how wondrous is the power of Prabhu,[^8] beyond all reckoning:\
the faqir won the day, and the Shah went down in defeat.\
If Prabhu wills it, he lifts up Mount Meru[^9] as if it were a blade of grass;\
if Prabhu wills it, he dries up the whole ocean within a few short minutes. (11)

ਆਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਬਾਜ ਸਿੰਘ ਹਥ ਜੋੜੋ ॥ ਬੈਠੋ ਹੋ ਕਿਮ ਚੜੋ ਨ ਘੋੜੇ ॥\
ਤਬ ਬੰਦੇ ਉਨ ਸਬਹਨ ਕਹਯੋ ॥ ਏਕ ਪਹਿਰ ਤੁਮ ਤਕੜੇ ਰਹਯੋ ॥੧੨॥

Ali Singh and Baj Singh came to Banda with their hands joined and pleaded,\
"Why are you sitting here? Why do you not mount your horse and ride?"\
Then Banda said to all of them who were gathered there,\
"Hold firm for three hours more, and stand fast for that long, (12)

ਤੌ ਮਾਲਕ ਫਿਰ ਹਮ ਵਲ ਕਰੈ ॥ ਸਭ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਕੌ ਮਾਰ ਸੁ ਡਰੈ ॥\
ਤਬ ਸਿੱਖਨ ਮਿਲ ਬਿਨਤੀ ਕਰੀ ॥ ਹਮ ਤੇ ਸਰਤ ਨ ਕਾਚੀ ਘਰੀ ॥੧੩॥

"and then the Master will turn and come over to our side,\
and all the Turks[^10] will be struck down and filled with terror."\
Then the Sikhs together pleaded with him once again,\
"We cannot hold out for even the shortest stretch of time." (13)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਬਾਜ ਸਿੰਘ ਔ ਸ਼ਯਾਮ ਸਿੰਘ ਸਤਿਗੁਰ ਦਏ ਜੁ ਸਾਥ ॥\
ਸਬਹਿਨ ਮਿਲਕੈ ਯੌ ਕਹਯੋ ਜੋੜ ਜੋੜ ਤੋ ਹਾਥ ॥੧੪॥

Baj Singh and Shyam Singh, the two whom the Satguru himself had sent along with Banda,\
and all the rest with them spoke to him like this, joining their hands again and again. (14)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤੁਮ ਬਿਨ ਹਮ ਤੇ ਲਰਯੋ ਨ ਜਾਈ ॥ ਸੀਸ ਸੁ ਤੁਮ ਢਿਗ ਦੇਹਿਂ ਲਗਾਈ ॥\
ਖੜਯੋ ਖਾਲਸੋ ਔਰ ਗਏ ਪਰਾਈ ॥ ਭਾਵੈਂ ਰਾਖੋ ਭਾਵੈਂ ਦੇਹੁ ਮਰਾਈ ॥੧੫॥

"Without you we cannot fight; we cannot do it on our own;\
we shall lay our heads down here beside you, at your side.\
The Khalsa alone has stood its ground; all the rest have run away;\
keep us alive if that is your will, or have us killed if that is your will. (15)

ਹਮ ਭਜ ਜਾਵਨ ਨਹਿਂ ਕਹੂੰ ਠੌਰ ॥ ਹਮ ਮਰ ਰਹੈਂਗੇ ਤੁਮ ਪਗ ਕੌਰ ॥੧੬॥

"We have no place anywhere in the world to run to and hide ourselves;\
we shall stay right here and lay down our lives at your feet." (16)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਫਿਰ ਬੰਦੈ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਕਹਯੋ ਤੁਮ ਘੜੀਆਂ ਚਾਰ ਲੜਾਉ ॥\
ਮਾਲਕ ਤੇ ਹਥ ਜੋੜਕੈ ਔਰ ਮੈਂ ਲੈਗੁ ਬਖਸ਼ਾਇ ॥੧੭॥

Then Banda said to the Khalsa, "Keep up the fight for an hour and a half, and hold the enemy off till then;\
after that, I shall join my hands before the Master and win his grace and favour for you all." (17)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਸਿੱਖਨ ਕਹਯੋ ਘੜੀ ਇਕ ਭਾਰੀ ॥ ਹਮ ਤੋ ਪਲ ਨਹਿ ਸਕੈਂ ਸੰਭਾਰੀ ॥\
ਮਾਲਕ ਹਥ ਬਿਨ ਸਿਰ ਹਮ ਆਏ ॥ ਹਮ ਕਿਮ ਬਚੈਂ ਤੁਰਕ ਆਗੇ ਪਾਏ ॥੧੮॥

The Sikhs said, "Even one short stretch of time is too heavy a weight for us;\
we cannot hold out against them for a single moment more.\
Did we come here without the Master's hand upon our heads?\
How can we survive, now that we have been thrown down before the Turks? (18)

ਹਮ ਪਰਯੋ ਜਹਾਜ ਸਮੁੰਦਰ ਮਾਂਹੀ ॥ ਭਾਮੈਂ ਰਾਖੋ ਭਾਮੈਂ ਦੇਹੁ ਡੁਬਾਈ ॥\
ਤੁਮ ਮਾਲਕ ਹੁਇ ਰਖੋ ਬਚਾਈ ॥ ਤੁਰਕ ਆਟਾ ਹਮ ਲੂਣੋਂ ਨਾਹੀਂ ॥੧੯॥

"Our ship has been caught out in the middle of the sea;\
keep it afloat if you will, or let it sink beneath the waves;\
you have been made our master, and it is for you to keep us safe;\
the Turks are as many as the flour in the kneading pan, and we are not even the pinch of salt in it. (19)

ਕਿਮ ਹਮ ਇਨ ਸੰਗ ਪੁਜੈ ਲਰਾਈ ॥ ਮਾਰੇ ਤੋਪਨ ਕੇ ਅਬ ਉਡ ਜਾਂਹੀ ॥\
ਚਾਰ ਘੜੀ ਹਮ ਕਬ ਇਨ ਠੱਲ੍ਹੈਂ ॥ ਤੁਮ ਬਿਨ ਬਚੈਂ ਨ ਹਮ ਇਕ ਪਲੈ ॥੨੦॥

"How can our fighting ever stand up to theirs and match it?\
Struck by their cannon, we shall be blown away right now.\
How long could we hold them back, even for an hour and a half?\
Without you we cannot survive for even a single moment. (20)

ਤੁਮ ਬਿਨ ਘੜੀ ਨ ਇਕ ਹਮ ਲੜੈਂ ॥ ਤੁਮ ਬਿਨ ਏਕ ਘੜੀ ਮੈਂ ਮਰੈਂ ॥\
ਤੁਮ ਬਿਨ ਹਮ ਲਰ ਸਕੈਂ ਸੁ ਨਾਹੀਂ ॥ ਤੋਪ ਜੰਬੂਰਨ ਸੋਂ ਉਡ ਜਾਹੀਂ ॥੨੧॥

"Without you we cannot fight for even one short stretch of time;\
without you we shall all be dead within that one short time;\
without you we cannot fight them, no, we cannot;\
we shall be blown away by their cannon and their swivel guns. (21)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਜਿਮ ਤੁਮ ਪ੍ਰਿਥਮੋ ਥੋ ਕਹਯੋ ਹਮ ਤੋਪਨ ਲਗਨ ਨ ਦੇਹਿਂ ॥\
ਉਨ ਕੇ ਸ਼ਸਤ੍ਰ ਸੁ ਉਨ ਲਗੈਂ ਉਨ ਕੋ ਨਾਸ਼ ਕਰੇਂਹਿ ॥੨੨॥

"You told us before, 'I shall never let their cannon touch you;\
their own weapons shall strike them back, and their own weapons shall destroy them.'" (22)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਸਿੰਘ ਮਝੈਲਨ ਬੰਦੈ ਕਹਿਓ ॥ ਹਮ ਲਰਹੈਂ ਤੁਮ ਬੈਠੇ ਰਹਿਓ ॥\
ਬੰਦੈ ਛਡਕੇ ਚੇਲੇ ਭਾਗੇ ॥ ਲੜੇ ਖਾਲਸੇ ਹੁਇ ਹੁਇ ਆਗੇ ॥੨੩॥

Then the Majhail Singhs[^11] spoke up and said to Banda,\
"We shall do the fighting ourselves; you stay seated here."\
Banda's disciples abandoned him there and fled from the field,\
but the men of the Khalsa fought on, pressing forward to the front, again and again. (23)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਉੱਚੀ ਰੋੜੀ ਦੇਖਿਕੈ ਬੰਦਾ ਬੈਠਯੋ ਜਾਇ ॥\
ਬਜੀਰੈ ਗੋਲਨਦਾਜ ਸੱਦਿ ਆਖਯੋ ਦੇਹੁ ਉਡਾਇ ॥੨੪॥

Seeing a high mound of earth and stones, Banda went over to it and sat himself down there upon the top;\
Wazira sent for his gunner, called him to his side, and said, "Blow that man away." (24)

ਚੁਪ ਕਰ ਬੰਦਾ ਬਹਿ ਰਹਯੋ ਪਰਯੋ ਲੜਾਈ ਗਾਢ ॥\
ਗੋਲੀ ਗੋਲੈ ਬਹੁ ਪਵੈਂ ਬੰਦਾ ਭਯੋ ਨ ਠਾਢ ॥੨੫॥

Banda kept silent and stayed seated there, while the battle raged thick and hard around him;\
bullets and cannonballs fell all about him in great numbers, but Banda did not rise to his feet. (25)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤਬ ਲੋਕਨ ਕੋ ਚਿੰਤਾ ਪਰੀ ॥ ਬੰਦਾ ਬੈਠਾ ਇਹਾਂ ਰਹਿ ਮਰੀ ॥\
ਚੇਲੇ ਨਠਿ ਯੋ ਟਿਬੈ ਲੁਕੈ ॥ ਗੋਲੈ ਸ਼ੂੰਕਤ ਸੁਨਤ ਨ ਸਕੈਂ ॥੨੬॥

Then the people there grew anxious and afraid for him:\
*Banda will sit on here, and he will die where he sits.*\
His disciples ran and hid themselves behind the mound,\
for they could not bear to hear the cannonballs whistling past. (26)

ਕੋਊ ਕਹੈ ਇਹ ਇਹਾਂ ਮਰ ਜਾਉਗ ॥ ਕੋਊ ਕਹੈ ਯਾ ਫਕੀਰ ਲੁਕ ਜਾਉਗ ॥\
ਕੋਊ ਕਹੈ ਹਮ ਹੀ ਕੋ ਖ੍ਵਾਰੀ ॥ ਜਿਨਕੇ ਆਹਿਂ ਅਡੰਬਰ ਭਾਰੀ ॥੨੭॥

Some said, "He will die here, this man, right where he sits";\
some said, "This faqir will slip away somewhere and hide himself";\
some said, "It is we who shall be ruined and disgraced,\
we who keep up great households and a grand display." (27)

ਤਬੈ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਉਪਜਯੋ ਰੋਹਿ ॥ ਲੜੈ ਖਾਲਸੋ ਆਗੈ ਹੋਹਿ ॥\
ਕਹਯੋ ਖ਼ਾਲਸੈ ਤੁਮ ਟਿਕ ਬਹਿ ਰਹੋ ॥ ਹਮ ਜੀਵਤ ਤੁਮ ਚਿੰਤ ਨ ਗਹੋ ॥੨੮॥

Then a fury rose up in the Khalsa, burning hot,\
and the Khalsa fought on, pressing forward to the front;\
the Khalsa said to Banda, "Stay seated, firm in your place;\
while we are alive, do not trouble yourself with any worry." (28)

ਤਬ ਉਨ ਕੀਯੋ ਦਗ਼ਾ ਦਗ਼ਬਾਜ ॥ ਡਾਰ ਲੂਟ ਚਲਯੋ ਪਿੱਛੈ ਭਾਜ ॥\
ਘੋੜੇ ਤੇ ਗਿਰ ਸੋ ਮਰਿ ਗਯੋ ॥ ਉਨ ਜੋ ਕੀਤੋ ਸੋ ਉਨ ਪਯੋ ॥੨੯॥

Then that double-dealer,[^12] the traitor, played false and showed his treachery;\
he threw himself into plunder, and then ran off toward the rear;\
but he fell from his horse as he fled, and so he died there;\
what he had done to others came back upon his own head. (29)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਹਸਿ ਬੰਦੇ ਘੋੜੋ ਮੰਗਯੋ ਕਹਿ ਮਾਲਕ ਪਹੁੰਚਯੋ ਆਇ ॥\
ਮਾਰੋ ਲੇਵੋ ਲੂਟ ਕਹਿ ਦਯੋ ਸੁ ਤੀਰ ਚਲਾਇ ॥੩੦॥

Laughing, Banda called for his horse, saying, "The Master has arrived; he has come";\
and saying, "Strike them down, and take the plunder," he loosed the arrow[^13] (that the Guru had given him). (30)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਚਲਤ ਤੀਰ ਉਨ ਘੱਟਾ ਉਠਾਯੋ ॥ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਕੀ ਸੋ ਅੱਖੀਂ ਪਾਯੋ ॥\
ਉਸੀ ਘੱਟੇ ਸੋਂ ਅੰਧੇ ਭਏ ॥ ਆਪਸ ਮੈਂ ਲਰਿ ਕੈ ਮਰਿ ਗਏ ॥੩੧॥

As the arrow flew, it raised up a great storm of dust,\
and drove that dust straight into the eyes of the Turks;\
blinded by that very dust, they could not tell friend from foe,\
and they fought among themselves and died at each other's hands. (31)

ਤਹੀਂ ਹਕਾਰ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਦਯੋ ॥ ਜਨ ਸਿਧਿ ਇਜੜ ਸ਼ੇਰ ਸੁ ਭਯੋ ॥\
ਜੋ ਤੁਰਕਨ ਕੇ ਲਭੇ ਸਰਦਾਰ ॥ ਤੇਊ ਖਾਲਸੈ ਦੀਨੇ ਮਾਰ ॥੩੨॥

Right there the Khalsa raised its war cry and charged,\
like a lion that has fallen upon a flock of sheep;\
and the Khalsa killed every leader of the Turks they found on the field,\
and struck them all down, every one of them, where they stood. (32)

ਤਹਾਂ ਬਜੀਰਾ ਸਸਕਿਤ ਪਯਾ ॥ ਕਿਲਾ ਗਡ ਕੋਊ ਅਗੈ ਗਯਾ ॥\
ਬੰਦੇ ਵਹੜਨ ਸਾਥ ਘਿਸੜਾਯਾ ॥ ਫੇਰ ਅਗਨ ਮੈ ਉਸੇ ਸੜਾਯਾ ॥੩੩॥

There Wazira was found lying on the field, sobbing and gasping for breath;\
someone drove a stake into his body and then went on ahead;\
Banda had him dragged along the ground behind young bullocks,\
and after that he had him thrown into the fire and burned.[^14] (33)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਇਤੀ ਬਜ਼ੀਰਾ ਮਾਰਿਓ ਜੇਠ ਸੁਦੀ ਕੇ ਮਾਹਿ ॥\
ਸੱਤ੍ਰਾ ਸੈਅ ਸਤਾਸਠ ਚਪੜ ਚਿੜੀ ਕੇ ਪਾਹਿ ॥੩੪॥

This is how Wazira was killed, struck down at last, in the bright fortnight of the month of Jeth,[^15]\
in the year Sammat 1767 (1710 CE), at the place called Chappar Chiri.[^16] (34)

### Notes

[^1]: Wazira: the poet's scornful short form for Wazir Khan, the nawab of Sirhind; the title calls him Bajid.
[^2]: swivel guns mounted on camels: the poet's shutar hathnār, the shutarnāl, a light gun fired from a camel's back (the hathnāl, fired from an elephant, may be meant as well). BSS: camels carrying small guns and long-barrelled muskets.
[^3]: BSS reads the line as the cannonballs flying in a line, one after another, like the links of a chain. Kulwant Singh: chain firing.
[^4]: matches: the poet's toṛe, the slow-burning cords that fired the matchlock.
[^5]: priming pans: the poet's pyāle, cups. BSS: the small cups at the touchhole of a gun, where the priming powder is fired.
[^6]: faqir: a holy beggar, a wandering ascetic. BSS: they said this sadhu had brought them there to be killed.
[^7]: gold coins: the poet's mohar.
[^8]: Prabhu: the Lord, a name of the divine.
[^9]: Meru: the golden mountain at the centre of the world in the Puranas.
[^10]: Turks: the poet's word for the Muslim rulers and their armies.
[^11]: Majhail Singhs: the Singhs of the Majha, the country between the Beas and the Ravi, who had joined Banda after the battle at Ropar (Episodes 38 and 39).
[^12]: double-dealer: the poet's dagābāz. BSS: the trickster who had come over from Wazira with a thousand men and passed himself off as a Hindu (Episode 37); as he fell his foot caught in the stirrup. Kulwant Singh: a traitor among the Khalsa. The poet's ḍār lūṭ, here taken as turning to plunder, is unclear.
[^13]: arrow: the poet's tīr. BSS: one of the arrows the Satguru had given Banda for the hour of need (Episode 29).
[^14]: BSS: the Singhs were so angry with him that they drove the stake into his anus; and he was burned alive, for the Satguru had ordered that his body be defiled. Kulwant Singh: a wooden peg driven through his body.
[^15]: Jeth: the month from mid-May to mid-June; the bright fortnight (sudī) is the half of the month when the moon is waxing.
[^16]: Chappar Chiri: a village on the road from Kharar to Banur, near present-day Mohali, where the battle was fought.

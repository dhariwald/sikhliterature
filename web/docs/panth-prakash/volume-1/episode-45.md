# Panth Prakash, Episode 45

## The Episode of the Men of Salaudi

### (ālī siṅgh de do putrāṅ dī achānak maut te bande dā var: The sudden death of Ali Singh's two sons, and Banda's boon)

### (...'ham ne tumko aur jug dayo': ...'I have given you another pair')

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਏਕ ਦਿਵਸ ਆਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਆਯੋ ॥ ਦੇਖ ਬੰਦੇ ਨੇ ਸੀਸ ਹਿਲਾਯੋ ॥\
ਤੋ ਆਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਦੁਇ ਕਰ ਜੋੜੇ ॥ ਹਜ਼ੂਰ ਨੇਤ੍ਰ ਕਿਮ ਹਮ ਸੋਂ ਮੋੜੇ ॥੧॥

One day Ali Singh of Salaudi[^1] came into Banda's presence,\
and when Banda saw him, he shook his head in sorrow.\
Then Ali Singh joined his two hands before him and said,\
"Your presence, why have you turned your eyes away from me? (1)

ਹਮ ਕੋ ਬਹੁਤ ਸੁ ਚਿੰਤਾ ਪਈ ॥ ਹਮ ਤੇ ਕਯਾ ਤਕਸੀਰ ਸੁ ਭਈ ॥\
ਤਬ ਬੰਦੇ ਨੇ ਬਚਨ ਉਚਾਰੇ ॥ ਪੁਤ੍ਰ ਮਰੈਂ ਦਿਨ ਬਾਈਅਨ ਥਾਰੇ ॥੨॥

"It troubles me deeply, and I am full of worry;\
tell me, what wrong have I done, what fault have I committed?"\
Then Banda spoke these words to him:\
"Twenty-two days from today, both of your sons will die. (2)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਲਗੇ ਗੋਲੀ ਇਕ ਸੀਸ ਮੈਂ ਦੂਜੋਂ ਇਮੀ ਮਰ ਜਾਇ ॥\
ਸੋ ਸੁਨਿਕੇ ਅਚਰਜ ਭਯੋ ਕਰੀ ਤਕੜਾਈ ਵਾਇ ॥੩॥

"One of them will be struck in the head by a bullet, and the other will simply die along with him, of no cause at all."\
Ali Singh was astonished when he heard it, and from that day on he kept a close guard over his sons. (3)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਦਿਨ ਬਾਈ ਜਬ ਪੁਜਯੋ ਆਇ ॥ ਹੁਤੋ ਬਹਾਲਯੋ ਤੰਬੂ ਮਾਇ ॥\
ਖਾਖ ਤੋਦੋ ਕਿਤੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਮਰਾਵੈਂ ॥ ਫੜ ਫੜ ਬੰਦੂਕਨ ਗੋਲੀ ਚਲਾਵੈਂ ॥੪॥

When the twenty-second day came round at last,\
Ali Singh kept the boy seated close beside him inside his own tent.\
Somewhere outside, the Singhs were practising their aim at a mound of earth,\
taking up musket after musket[^2] and firing at the target. (4)

ਸੋ ਹੋਣੀ ਨੇ ਖੜਾ ਕਰਾਯਾ ॥ ਉਤ ਵਲ ਹੁਇ ਉਨ ਝਾਕਾ ਲਾਯਾ ॥\
ਛੁਟੀ ਗੋਲੀ ਉਸ ਸੀਸੇ ਵੜੀ ॥ ਹੋਣੀ ਕਹੋ ਕਹਾਂ ਕਿਮ ਟਰੀ ॥੫॥

But fate itself made the boy get up and stand;\
he turned toward the firing and looked out of the tent to watch.\
A shot went off, and the bullet went straight into his head.\
Tell me, where has fate ever been turned aside, and by what means? (5)

ਦੋਹਰਾ *Dohra*

ਖਾਨੂੰ ਸਿੰਘ ਐਸੇ ਮੁਯੋ ਬਚਯੋ ਮਾਨ ਸਿੰਘ ਨਾਂਹਿਂ ॥\
ਹੁਤੀ ਜੁ ਅੱਵਲ ਏਕ ਉਸ ਇਮ ਕਰ ਦੋਊ ਮਰਾਹਿ ॥੬॥

So Khanu Singh died in that way, and his brother Man Singh could not live on without him;\
the two were twins who had shared one placenta[^3] in their mother's womb, and so they both died together. (6)

ਚੌਪਈ *Chaupai*

ਤਬ ਬੰਦੈ ਸਿੰਘ ਆਲੀ ਬੁਲਾਯਾ ॥ਮਾਰੋ ਉਸੈ ਜਿਨ ਤਵ ਸੁਤ ਘਾਯਾ ॥\
ਜਬ ਬੰਦੇ ਨੇ ਐਸ ਫਰਮਾਯਾ ॥ ਉਲਟ ਆਲੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਉਨੈਂ ਸੁਨਾਯਾ ॥੭॥

Then Banda sent for Ali Singh and said to him,\
"Go and kill the man whose shot has killed your son."\
But when Banda had given him this command,\
Ali Singh answered him in quite another way. (7)

ਹਮਰੇ ਸੁਤ ਨਹਿ ਕਿਨੈ ਮਰਾਏ ॥ ਮੁਏ ਸੁ ਅਪਨੀ ਉਮਰ ਬਿਤਾਏ ॥\
ਅਬ ਹਮ ਕਿਸਕੋ ਦੇਂ ਕਿਮ ਦੋਸ ॥ ਨਹਿ ਕਿਨ ਮਾਰਯੋ ਫੜ ਕਰ ਓਸ ॥੮॥

"No man has killed my sons;\
they died because the years that were given to them had run out.\
Whom should I blame for it now, and how could I blame anyone?\
No one took hold of my son and killed him; the shot was never aimed at him." (8)

ਮਿਹਰਵਾਨ ਹੁਇ ਬੰਦੈ ਕਹਯੋ ॥ 'ਹਮ ਨੇ ਤੁਮਕੋ ਔਰ ਜੁਗ ਦਯੋ'॥\
ਵੈ ਭੀ ਜਨਮੈਂ ਜੌੜੈ ਦੋਊ ॥ ਮਾਹੀ ਸਿੰਘ ਔ ਬਲੋ ਸਿੰਘ ਸੋਊ ॥੯॥

Banda was pleased with his answer, and in his kindness he said to him,\
"Then I have given you another pair of sons in their place;\
they too will both be born to you as twins,\
and their names will be Mahi Singh and Balo Singh."[^4] (9)

### Notes

[^1]: Ali Singh of Salaudi: the Singh who left Wazir Khan's service to join Banda (Episode 34). Kulwant Singh: after Wazir Khan's death Banda made him deputy governor of Sirhind, and he was martyred with Banda at Delhi. BSS: he was a commander and jathedar in Banda's army.
[^2]: musket: the poet's bandūk.
[^3]: placenta: the poet's avval (aul). BSS: they lay together in their mother's womb, twins, and so the death of one was the death of both.
[^4]: Balo Singh: as printed; Kulwant Singh writes Ballo. BSS: both sons were born to Ali Singh afterwards, by Banda's blessing.
